Suriya's Scene earns ₹81.5 crore worldwide in two days

The Diwali action-comedy added ₹20.25 crore net in India on Saturday, up 5.7% on opening day.

accha? daily · 8 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short

  • Worldwide gross: ₹81.50 crore
  • India net: ₹39.40 crore
  • Day 2 net: ₹20.25 crore (+5.7%)

The numbers

Sacnilk's tracking puts Scene at about ₹81.50 crore worldwide gross after two days. In India it made ₹20.25 crore net on Saturday, up 5.7% from Friday, taking its two-day India net to ₹39.40 crore.

Great Andhra says the film is expected to cross ₹100 crore worldwide over the opening weekend if the momentum holds.

The Diwali clash

Scene opened on 6 November against Ranbir Kapoor and Yash's Ramayana: Part One. Advance bookings were about ₹20 crore across six days, and its Kerala rights were reportedly the highest ever paid for a Suriya film in the state. Trackers' figures are estimates and can change.

TRAI backs satellite-to-phone services with a separate licence of up to 20 years

The regulator's recommendations, now with the Department of Telecommunications, would open the door to direct-to-device connectivity.

accha? daily · 8 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
20 yrsproposed licence term, renewable
  • Licence: national, up to 20 years
  • Services: FSS, MSS, D2D
  • Status: awaiting DoT

What TRAI proposes

TRAI has recommended a separate 'Satellite Communication Network Provider' authorisation for the whole country, valid for up to 20 years with a renewal of up to another 20. Permitted services could include fixed satellite, mobile satellite and direct-to-device services using mobile (IMT) spectrum.

Direct-to-device traffic starting or ending in India would have to pass through gateways in India and meet lawful-interception and monitoring rules. A network operator could also sell capacity to other service providers.

What happens next

The recommendations go to the DoT for approval, so nothing changes for phone users yet. One report mentions an 8% revenue-linked authorisation fee; check TRAI's press release No. 151 of 2026 for the exact terms.

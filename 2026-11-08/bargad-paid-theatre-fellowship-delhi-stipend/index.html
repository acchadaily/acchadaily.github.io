A Delhi fellowship pays rural performers a stipend to learn theatre

Bargad's six-month programme pays ₹10,000 a month, rising to ₹15,000 from January 2027, funded by individual donors.

accha? daily · 8 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp

₹15,000monthly stipend from January 2027
  • Duration: 6 months
  • First cohort: 14 fellows
  • Donors: ~20–25, ₹5,000–₹5 lakh

The fellowship

Bargad runs a six-month theatre fellowship in Delhi, with classes from 4 pm to 8:30 pm six days a week and visiting faculty from Mumbai, Bengaluru and Chennai. The first cohort had 14 fellows, and the stipend was ₹10,000 a month for the first two cohorts and rises to ₹15,000 for the third, starting January 2027.

Who pays

About 20 to 25 individual donors have given between ₹5,000 and ₹5 lakh each over two years. The founders say they now want longer-term partners to keep it going. One participant said it was the first time he was paid to do theatre.

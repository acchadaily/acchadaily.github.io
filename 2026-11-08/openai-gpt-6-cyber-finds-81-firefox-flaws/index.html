OpenAI's GPT-6 Cyber finds 81 Firefox flaws and gets a 'Critical' risk rating

The new security model is limited to vetted customers. OpenAI says it exploited 48.5% of test vulnerabilities, up from 32.1% for its previous model.

accha? daily · 8 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
81new high-severity Firefox bugs found
  • Firefox: 81 high-severity bugs
  • Linux: exploits for 948 vulnerabilities
  • Rating: 'Critical'

What it is

OpenAI has launched GPT-6 Cyber, a model built to find and validate software vulnerabilities and help write fixes. OpenAI says it found 81 previously unknown high-severity flaws in Firefox, many already patched by Mozilla.

On the Linux kernel it flagged 2,308 potential flaws, reproduced 1,724 and built local privilege-escalation exploits for 948 of them.

Why it is locked down

OpenAI rates the model 'Critical' for cybersecurity under its Preparedness Framework, one step above the 'High' given to GPT-5.6 Cyber. On the ExploitGym benchmark it exploited 48.5% of vulnerabilities against 32.1% for the older model.

Access is limited to approved customers in its Daybreak Red programme. OpenAI says there is 'a limited window to use frontier models to secure software'. All figures are OpenAI's own and not independently verified.

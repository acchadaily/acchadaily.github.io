LPG check for November: commercial cylinder up ₹10, home cylinder unchanged at ₹942

Petrol and diesel prices are also unchanged, while jet fuel went up ₹1.30 a litre.

accha? daily · 8 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹94214.2 kg home cylinder in Delhi, unchanged
  • Commercial 19 kg (Delhi): ₹2,820
  • Domestic 14.2 kg (Delhi): ₹942
  • ATF: +₹1.30/litre

What changed on 1 November

The 19 kg commercial LPG cylinder rose ₹10 in Delhi to ₹2,820, from ₹2,810 the month before. Jet fuel (ATF) rose ₹1.30 per litre.

Retail petrol and diesel rates stayed the same across the country.

For your kitchen

Domestic 14.2 kg LPG has been held at ₹942 in Delhi since 7 June 2026. Rules that continue include biometric Aadhaar authentication for LPG and the 30-day cylinder termination rule introduced in May 2026. Check Indian Oil's city-wise page for your exact price.

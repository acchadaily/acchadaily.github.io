A Bengal professor runs 20+ free coaching centres that also feed children

Chandrashekhar Kundu, known as the 'Foodman', started with dinners for children and built learning centres around them.

accha? daily · 8 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
20+free coaching centres
  • Centres: 20+
  • Early reach: ~180 children, 3 villages
  • Meals served with lessons

What he does

Chandrashekhar Kundu, a college professor in West Bengal known as the 'Foodman', began by giving children dinners and now runs more than 20 free coaching locations under the FEED initiative, still serving meals alongside lessons.

In the early stage the meals reached nearly 180 children across three villages. The Better India does not give a current total.

How it runs

The programme depends on volunteers and local teachers, with support from others in its early years. The article doesn't say how it is funded today.

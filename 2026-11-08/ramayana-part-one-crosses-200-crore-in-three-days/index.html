Ramayana: Part One crosses ₹200 crore gross in India in three days

Nitesh Tiwari's Diwali release, starring Ranbir Kapoor and Yash, is at ₹208 crore gross after its first weekend day, though Hindi screens are running at only 35% full.

accha? daily · 8 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹208 crIndia gross after 3 days
  • Gross India: ₹208.01 crore
  • Net India: ₹174.94 crore
  • Hindi occupancy: 35.1%

The numbers

According to The Week, which cites Sacnilk for the latest day, Ramayana: Part One stands at about ₹208.01 crore gross and ₹174.94 crore net in India after three days. Day 1 brought ₹64.85 crore net, Day 2 dipped to ₹48.05 crore, and Day 3 was tracking ₹62.04 crore net by 9 pm. Day-3 figures are provisional and different trackers differ.

Worldwide, the film had ₹230.05 crore through Day 2, including ₹95.25 crore from overseas.

Where it is strong

Hindi gave about 75% of Day-3 net, ₹48.33 crore, but at only 35.1% occupancy. The smaller markets are fuller: Tamil 76.3%, Kannada 64.7% and Telugu 53%.

Trade coverage calls Sunday the real test of whether the weekend holds. The film is described as one of the biggest releases in Bollywood's Diwali history, with Sai Pallavi as Sita.

Vivaan Kapoor wins trap silver at the shooting World Championships and India's first shotgun quota for LA 2028

In his first senior World Championship final, the 24-year-old from Jaipur lost gold only in a shoot-off.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
29/30Kapoor's score in the final
  • Final score: 29/30
  • Qualification: 120/125
  • India's LA 2028 shooting quotas: 3

The final

Vivaan Kapoor qualified sixth with 120/125 in Doha on 12 November, then hit 29/30 in the final to tie with Kuwait's Abdulrahman Alfaihan. Alfaihan won the shoot-off 3–2 to take gold, and China's Qi Ying got bronze. "I just gave my best. I focused on my job like always," Kapoor said.

Quota for LA 2028

The silver earns India its first shotgun quota for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics; Kapoor is India's third shooter with an LA quota after Jaypal Malviya and Niraj Kumar. Udaivir Singh Jaiji finished sixth, and India's men's trap team came fourth with 354, missing bronze on a count-back.

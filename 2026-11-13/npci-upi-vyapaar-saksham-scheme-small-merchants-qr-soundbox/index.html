NPCI's new 'UPI Vyapaar Saksham' scheme will pay banks ₹300 per QR code and ₹750 per soundbox to bring small-town shops onto UPI

The three-year plan targets shops with turnover up to ₹40 lakh in smaller towns, the Northeast, J&K and Ladakh.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹750per soundbox placed with a new shop
  • QR code incentive: ₹300
  • Soundbox incentive: ₹750
  • Shop turnover limit: ₹40 lakh

How it works

The National Payments Corporation of India announced the scheme in a circular on 11 November. Banks will get ₹300 for each QR code and ₹750 for each payment soundbox they set up with an eligible new merchant. It targets shops with annual turnover up to ₹40 lakh in Tier III to VI towns, the Northeast, Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh. The yearly goal is 6 lakh to 1 crore new QR codes and 5–10 lakh soundboxes.

Who pays

The money will not come from the government budget. It will come from the merchant fee (MDR) planned on UPI shop payments: banks and UPI apps will put 5% of their share into the fund. The scheme runs for three years from when that fee starts, which has not been notified yet. The proposed fee is 0.4% on payments above ₹2,000, capped at ₹300.

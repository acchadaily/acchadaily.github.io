India lose 5-0 to New Zealand in Auckland; goalkeeper Vishal Kaith saves a penalty

India have now scored 2 and conceded 16 in four friendlies; the teams meet again in Christchurch on Sunday.

The match

New Zealand beat India 5-0 at Go Media Stadium on 12 November, India's first match on New Zealand soil. An own goal in the 5th minute set the tone; Jesse Randall scored twice and Chris Wood and Lachlan Bayliss added one each. Vishal Kaith saved a second-half penalty and several other shots. Anirudh Thapa captained India, and two players made debuts.

Where India stand

Across friendlies against Panama, Brazil, Uruguay and New Zealand, India have scored 2 and let in 16. They are ranked 138th, against New Zealand's 88th. Coach Khalid Jamil said "many mistakes were there". The second match is on Sunday, 15 November, in Christchurch at 8:30 am IST.

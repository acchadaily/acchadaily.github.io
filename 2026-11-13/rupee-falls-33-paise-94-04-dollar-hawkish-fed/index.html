Rupee slips 33 paise to 94.04 against the dollar as US rate-hike bets grow, despite strong foreign buying

A day after hitting its strongest level since April, the rupee gave up ground to a firmer dollar.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
94.04rupee per dollar on 12 Nov
  • USD/INR: 94.04 (provisional)
  • Change: -33 paise
  • Foreign buying in Nov: ~$6.4 billion

What moved it

The rupee closed at a provisional 94.04 per US dollar on 12 November, down 33 paise from 93.71, after opening at 93.73, PTI reported. Traders said a hawkish speech by a US Federal Reserve official and rising odds of a December US rate hike lifted the dollar; the dollar index was at 103.03.

The other side

Foreign investors have been big buyers of Indian shares this month, about $6.4 billion so far in November according to PTI, which normally supports the rupee. Higher oil prices after the Houthi attacks work the other way, since India imports most of its crude.

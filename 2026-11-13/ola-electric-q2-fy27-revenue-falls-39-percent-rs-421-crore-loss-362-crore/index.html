Ola Electric's Q2 revenue falls 39% to ₹421 crore; it shuts company-run stores and moves to dealers

The loss narrowed to ₹362 crore from a year ago, and the company says supply, not demand, is now its problem.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp

-39%drop in quarterly revenue to ₹421 crore
  • Q2 revenue: ₹421 crore
  • Q2 net loss: ₹362 crore
  • Dealer stores: 319

The results

Ola Electric's revenue for July–September 2026 was ₹421 crore, down 39% from ₹690 crore a year earlier and below ₹455 crore in the previous quarter. Its net loss was ₹362 crore, smaller than ₹418 crore a year ago but wider than ₹336 crore in April–June. "Demand is no longer our constraint; supply is," the company said.

A new sales model

Ola stopped taking orders at its company-owned stores from 1 November and expects most of them to close by December. All new scooters will now be sold through dealers: 255 partners running 319 stores as of 8 November. Separately, the consumer regulator fined the Ola cab app (a different company, ANI Technologies) ₹10 lakh over its tipping prompt.

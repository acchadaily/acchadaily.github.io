Ramayana: Part 1 crosses ₹1,000 crore worldwide in 8 days, according to Sacnilk

Ranbir Kapoor and Yash's epic made ₹53.5 crore net in India on its eighth day alone.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹1,034 crworldwide gross in 8 days (Sacnilk)
  • Worldwide gross: ₹1,033.55 crore
  • India net: ₹564.6 crore
  • Day 8 India net: ₹53.5 crore

The numbers

Tracker Sacnilk puts Ramayana: Part 1's worldwide gross at ₹1,033.55 crore after eight days, with ₹675 crore gross from India and about ₹358.5 crore from overseas (an estimate). India net collections stand at ₹564.6 crore. On Thursday, Day 8, it earned ₹53.5 crore net, ₹43 crore of it from the Hindi version.

A note on numbers

Box office trackers differ: Box Office Worldwide says over ₹1,025 crore, while some others are lower, and the makers have not released official worldwide figures. Either way, it is one of the fastest Indian films to reach the ₹1,000 crore mark.

Houthis claim attacks on a Saudi oil pipeline and Yanbu; Brent jumps 3.3% to $95.82

Saudi Arabia has been sending about 4 million barrels a day through this pipeline to avoid the Strait of Hormuz.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$95.82Brent crude, up $3.06 on 12 Nov
  • Brent: $95.82 (+3.3%)
  • WTI: $88.74
  • Rerouted via pipeline: ~4 mn barrels/day

The claims

Yemen's Houthis said on 12 November they hit pumping station No. 7 on Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline with a drone, fired a ballistic missile at the Yanbu oil facility and attacked an Aramco building in Riyadh. The Saudi-led coalition said it shot down two drones, over the Riyadh region and the Eastern Province. Aramco did not comment, and there were no immediate reports of damage.

Why oil jumped

With Hormuz traffic disrupted, Saudi Arabia has rerouted about 4 million barrels a day, roughly 4% of world supply, through the East-West pipeline to the Red Sea port of Yanbu. Any threat to that route worries traders. Brent settled at $95.82 a barrel, up $3.06, and WTI at $88.74, Reuters reported, though both are still lower for the week.

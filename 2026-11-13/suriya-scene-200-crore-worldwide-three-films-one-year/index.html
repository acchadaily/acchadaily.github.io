Suriya's Scene crosses ₹200 crore worldwide; he becomes the first Tamil actor with three ₹200-crore films in a year

The film joins Karuppu and Vishwanathan & Sons, though Ramayana is now taking away screens.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹200.6 crScene's worldwide gross in 7 days
  • Worldwide: ₹200.60 crore
  • India net: ₹111.55 crore
  • Day 7: ₹6.1 crore net

The record

Scene has grossed ₹200.60 crore worldwide in seven days, The Week reported on 12 November, with ₹111.55 crore net in India and ₹71 crore overseas. That makes Suriya the first Tamil star with three ₹200-crore films in one year, after Karuppu and Vishwanathan & Sons.

Slowing down

Daily collections are dropping: ₹6.1 crore net on Day 7 against ₹23.65 crore on its first Sunday, as Ramayana grabs screens. Directed by Jithu Madhavan with Naslen and Nazriya, it is Suriya's best-ever earner in Kerala.

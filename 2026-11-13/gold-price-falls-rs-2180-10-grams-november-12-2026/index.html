Gold falls ₹2,180 per 10 grams in a day to ₹1,39,800, then holds steady on Friday

A stronger dollar after hot US inflation data pushed gold down, a small relief for wedding-season buyers.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹1.4L24K gold per 10g on 13 Nov
  • 24K: ₹1,39,790/10g (13 Nov)
  • 22K: ₹1,28,140/10g
  • Silver: ₹1,99,900/kg

Today's rates

On 12 November, 24-carat gold fell ₹2,180 to ₹1,39,800 per 10 grams and 22-carat dropped ₹2,000 to ₹1,28,150, according to Goodreturns. On Friday morning, 24K was nearly flat at ₹1,39,790 and 22K at ₹1,28,140. Silver was ₹1,99,900 a kg. These rates exclude GST.

Why it fell

US prices rose faster than expected in October and Federal Reserve officials sounded tough on inflation, which lifted the dollar. A stronger dollar usually makes gold less attractive. International gold slipped below $4,000 an ounce. On MCX, December gold traded around ₹1,38,726.

Assam bans drones over Amur falcon roosts as more than 1 lakh of the tiny raptors arrive

The birds stop in the Northeast on one of the longest migrations of any bird of prey.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
1 lakh+Amur falcons now in Assam
  • Falcons in Assam: 1 lakh+
  • Protected roosts: 2
  • Tagged birds' route: 23 countries

The protection

Assam's Forest Department has banned recreational, commercial and other non-essential drone flights around Amur falcon roosts at Umrangso in Dima Hasao and Dharamtul in Morigaon, The Better India reported on 12 November. The ban lasts while the birds are present, and officers can mark temporary no-drone zones. An earlier roost at Habang was abandoned after people disturbed it.

A long journey

More than 1 lakh falcons have arrived; Umrangso once counted over 2 lakh. In October, two satellite-tagged falcons, Apapang and Alang, returned to Manipur's Tamenglong after flying through 23 countries.

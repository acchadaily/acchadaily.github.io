China switches on the world's largest liquid-air energy storage plant, storing 10 hours of power

It freezes air into liquid with spare solar power and turns it back into electricity when needed.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
600 MWhstorage capacity
  • Power: 60 MW
  • Capacity: 600 MWh (~10 hours)
  • Solar farm alongside: 250 MW

The plant

The 60 MW / 600 MWh plant in Qinghai, on the Tibetan Plateau, can supply power for about 10 hours, and sits next to a new 250 MW solar farm. ESS News calls it the world's largest grid-connected liquid-air storage plant. It was built by China Green Development Investment Group with the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

How it works

Extra solar power cools air to about -190°C until it turns liquid. When power is needed, the liquid is warmed, expands rapidly and spins a turbine. Unlike batteries, it uses no lithium, which could help store clean energy for longer at lower cost.

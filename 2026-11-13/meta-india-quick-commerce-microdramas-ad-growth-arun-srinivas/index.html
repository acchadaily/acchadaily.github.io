Meta India's Arun Srinivas names quick commerce and microdramas as its next big ad drivers; 2 lakh creators joined its festive programme

Over 2 lakh creators made shoppable content for Flipkart and Myntra on Meta's platforms this festive season.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read

In short
2 lakh+creators in Meta's festive affiliate push
  • Festive creators: 2 lakh+
  • WhatsApp Pay UPI share: 0.8%
  • AI dubbing: 6 Indian languages

What Meta sees

Meta's India head Arun Srinivas told Moneycontrol, as reported by Storyboard18 on 12 November, that quick commerce and short-form drama apps are two fast-growing ad categories in India. More than 2 lakh creators made content for Meta's affiliate commerce programme with Flipkart and Myntra during the festive season. Meta's AI dubbing with lip-sync now works in 6 Indian languages.

WhatsApp Pay's struggle

WhatsApp Pay handled just 0.8% of UPI volume in September 2026, while PhonePe and Google Pay together had nearly 78%. Srinivas said WhatsApp "was curbed in terms of user acquisition in the past". This comes as Kunal Shah takes charge of WhatsApp.

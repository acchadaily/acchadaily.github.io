Your Wallet

PM Modi says the government absorbed ₹43 of a ₹50-a-litre diesel hike, costing ₹14,000 crore a month

Speaking in Delhi, the PM said diesel went up only ₹7 a litre even though the war in West Asia sent crude oil soaring.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹43per litre of diesel cost absorbed, says PM
  • Hike needed (PM): ₹50/litre
  • Actual hike: ₹7/litre
  • Delhi diesel: ₹95.20/litre

What he said

At the India Today Kisan Conference in New Delhi on 12 November, PM Narendra Modi said crude oil rose from $70 to $122 a barrel within weeks after the West Asia war began. He said diesel needed a hike of at least ₹50 a litre, but prices went up by only ₹7, with the government absorbing the other ₹43. He put that extra burden at ₹14,000 crore a month, and said diesel became 40–50% costlier in several neighbouring countries.

At the pump

Fuel prices were unchanged on 12 November. In Delhi, petrol costs ₹102.12 a litre and diesel ₹95.20. These under-recovery figures are the government's own; no independent breakdown has been published.

India now buys more oil from the Gulf than from Russia, for the first time since the Iran war began

Early November tanker data shows Gulf supplies back at about 2.1 million barrels a day as some Hormuz traffic resumes.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
2.1Mbarrels a day from the Gulf in Nov so far
  • Gulf imports: ~2.1 mn barrels/day
  • Russia: ~1.8 mn barrels/day
  • Gulf in April: ~0.25 mn barrels/day

The shift

Ship-tracking data from Kpler, reported by Bloomberg and Business Standard on 12 November, shows India importing about 2.1 million barrels a day from Persian Gulf producers so far this month, against about 1.8 million from Russia. In April, Gulf flows to India had crashed to about 2.47 lakh barrels a day, from roughly 2.8 million before the war.

Why it is happening

Shuttle tanker runs have restored some flows through the Strait of Hormuz, and Gulf sellers are pricing more competitively. BPCL said it would cut Russian purchases for November, and Ukrainian strikes have hit Russian export facilities. This is an early-month snapshot, so the full-month picture could change.

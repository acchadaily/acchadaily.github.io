Delhi head constable traces 119 missing children in a year and reunites them with their families

Head Constable Chanchal of Jaitpur police station also found 16 missing adults.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
119missing children traced in 12 months
  • Children traced: 119
  • Under 14: 41
  • Total people found: 135

Her work

Between November 2025 and October 2026, Head Constable Chanchal of Jaitpur police station in southeast Delhi traced 119 missing children, PTI reported: 41 under 14 and 78 aged 14 to 18. Including 16 adults, she found 135 people in total.

How she did it

The children were found across Delhi-NCR and other states. She relied on tip-offs, technical tracking and repeated follow-ups, working with child care homes, NGOs and Child Welfare Committees to bring them home safely.

Kia plans a second Indian plant in Anantapur, a $2 billion investment with its suppliers

The new factory will add 2 lakh cars a year and build both fuel and electric vehicles for India and export.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$2BKia and suppliers' planned investment
  • Investment: ~$2 billion
  • New capacity: 2 lakh units a year
  • Total capacity: 6.3 lakh a year

The plan

Kia signed an agreement with the Andhra Pradesh government on 12 November for a second plant next to its existing factory in Anantapur. Kia and its suppliers together plan to invest about $2 billion. The new plant adds 2 lakh units of capacity, taking Kia's total in India to 6.3 lakh vehicles a year. Most reports say production will begin in early 2030.

Why it matters

The plant will make petrol, diesel and electric cars for India and for export. Kia's total investment in India will reach about $5 billion. It is an MoU for now, not a final investment decision.

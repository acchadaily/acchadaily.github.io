Sensex ends 32 points higher at 81,911, Nifty at 25,299, for a third straight gain; realty stocks jump nearly 4%

Foreign buying held the market up in a choppy session, while smaller stocks did better than the big names.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read

  • Sensex: 81,911 (+31.6)

  • Realty index: up nearly 4%

How the day went

The Sensex closed at 81,911.01 on 12 November, up 31.61 points, after swinging between 81,576 and 82,325. The Nifty 50 rose 44.30 points, or 0.18%, to 25,299.35. The Nifty Realty index jumped nearly 4%, while autos were the weakest. Midcaps rose about 0.9% and smallcaps about 1.1%.

Winners and losers

Bajaj Finance, SBI, BEL, Bajaj Finserv, ICICI Bank and Eternal were among the top gainers; HUL, Maruti, Power Grid and TCS fell. "Strong foreign inflows remained the key support," said Geojit's Vinod Nair, while Religare's Ajit Mishra said markets were "taking a breather".

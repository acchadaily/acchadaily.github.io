Tamil Nadu's simple TB check cut deaths by at least 38%, and by 56% in the latest group

Five quick checks at diagnosis send the sickest patients straight to hospital.

accha? daily · 13 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
-56%TB deaths in the latest patient group
  • Death rate: 6.3% → 2.8%
  • Patients a year: ~60,000
  • Checks: 5 simple signs

How it works

Under Tamil Nadu's TNKET programme, every adult diagnosed with drug-sensitive TB is checked for five warning signs: body mass index, swollen feet, breathing rate, oxygen level and whether they can stand on their own. The most severely ill are admitted to hospital right away. About 60,000 such adults are diagnosed every year in the state's public health system.

The results

A study by ICMR's National Institute of Epidemiology in BMJ Global Health found deaths fell from 6.3% to 3.9% (38% lower) and then to 2.8% (56% lower) for patients diagnosed in July–September 2025. The researchers say follow-up of the sickest patients still needs work.

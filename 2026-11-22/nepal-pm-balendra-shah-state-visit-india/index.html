Nepal PM Balendra Shah begins his first state visit to India; talks with PM Modi on Monday will focus on flood rebuilding, trade and energy

The 36-year-old leader's two-day trip is his first bilateral visit since Nepal's March election.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • 2-day visit, 22–23 Nov
  • First bilateral trip since March election
  • August floods: 1,500 dead

The visit

Nepal's Prime Minister Balendra Shah, 36, arrives in India on Sunday for a two-day state visit. He holds delegation-level talks with Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Monday, meets External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar and calls on President Droupadi Murmu.

Finance Minister Swarnim Wagle and Foreign Minister Shisir Khanal are travelling with him, along with business officials.

On the table

Indian help to rebuild after August's floods in Nepal, which killed 1,500 people and left more than 5,200 missing, according to Reuters, is high on the agenda. Trade, energy and connectivity are also expected to come up.

Nepal wants a new air route from the west so that flights can reach Bhairahawa, near Lumbini, and Pokhara more easily.

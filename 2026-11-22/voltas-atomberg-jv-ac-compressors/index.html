Tata's Voltas and Atomberg sign a joint venture to make AC compressors in India, each committing up to ₹100 crore

Voltas takes 50% of the venture and will control its board, making it a Voltas subsidiary.

The deal

Voltas, Tata's air-conditioner brand, is buying 50% of Atomberg Airtech. Each partner will put in up to ₹100 crore in tranches. Voltas gets to name four of the five directors, so the venture becomes a Voltas subsidiary and will be renamed.

The term sheet was signed in August; the agreement has now been executed.

Why compressors

The compressor is the heart of an AC, and much of India's supply is imported. Voltas will be the anchor customer, but the venture can sell to other brands too.

For Atomberg — founded in 2012 by IIT Bombay alumni, best known for energy-saving fans, and preparing for an IPO — it is a big step into heavier manufacturing.

COP31 talks run into Sunday as summit president Murat Kurum says a final climate deal is close

A new text tries to answer complaints that an earlier draft dropped all mention of moving away from fossil fuels.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Talks ran past Friday deadline
  • Nearly 200 delegations
  • Earlier draft upset 23+ countries

Where things stand

Negotiators in Antalya, Turkey, missed Friday's deadline and worked through Saturday into the early hours of Sunday. COP31 President Murat Kurum said he had produced "a text which all the parties can agree upon", and that concerns over fossil fuels and money for adaptation had been addressed.

Countries had until 0300 GMT to object, with a session to approve the deal set for 0600 GMT (11:30 am IST). Final adoption had not been confirmed at the time of writing.

The fight

An earlier draft dropped all fossil-fuel language and a $120 billion adaptation figure, upsetting at least 23 countries, Carbon Brief reported. Panama threatened not to sign. Nearly 200 delegations are taking part, with Turkey hosting and Australia leading the negotiations.

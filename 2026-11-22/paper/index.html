Indian startup GG Technologies sues Apple for ₹1,500 crore, saying the iPhone's NameDrop copies its patented contact-sharing tech

The maker of the StayTouch app has gone to the Bombay High Court and wants NameDrop switched off in India.

GG Technologies, the company behind the contact-sharing app StayTouch, has filed a patent suit against Apple in the Bombay High Court. It is asking for ₹1,500 crore in damages, a figure it calls a "conservative" estimate.

The startup was founded in 2018 by IIT Delhi alumnus Gaurav Goel. Its claim is simple: NameDrop, the iPhone feature that lets two people swap contact details by holding their phones close together, works the way its own invention does.

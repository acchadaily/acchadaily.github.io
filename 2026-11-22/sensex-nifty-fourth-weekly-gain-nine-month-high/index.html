Sensex gains 2,327 points this week and Nifty rises 2.6% for a fourth straight weekly gain, as bank stocks lead

India held up while Asia and the Nasdaq slumped on AI worries, helped by record foreign buying.

The week

The Sensex rose 2,327.26 points, or 2.85%, over the week to close at 83,919.71 on Friday. The Nifty 50 gained 656.30 points, or 2.61%, to 25,800.60 — both at nine-month highs.

Banks led: Nifty Bank rose 5.9% and PSU banks 6.72%. Metals fell 3%, while midcaps were flat.

Foreign money

Foreign investors were net buyers on all five days, putting in about ₹32,647 crore (provisional). November has drawn a record $12.21 billion so far. That kept India steady even as the MSCI Asia-Pacific index lost 3.3% on Friday and the Nasdaq fell 5.28%.

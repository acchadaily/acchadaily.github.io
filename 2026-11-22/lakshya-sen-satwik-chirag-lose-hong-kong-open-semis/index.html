Lakshya Sen and Satwik–Chirag both lose in the Hong Kong Open semi-finals, ending India's run

Lakshya's 13-match winning streak ends against Denmark's Anders Antonsen.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
13Lakshya's winning streak that ended
  • Lakshya lost 13-21, 13-21
  • Satwik–Chirag lost 18-21, 19-21
  • World Tour Finals 9–13 Dec

The results

Lakshya Sen lost 13-21, 13-21 to third seed Anders Antonsen, ending a 13-match run that included titles at the Hylo Open and the Kumamoto Masters. Antonsen now leads their head-to-head 6-3.

World No. 6 pair Satwik Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty lost 18-21, 19-21 to Malaysia's Man Wei Chong and Tee Kai Wun, despite leading that rivalry 4-1.

Next up

India have no finalist in Hong Kong. Lakshya has already qualified for the BWF World Tour Finals in Hangzhou, from 9 to 13 December.

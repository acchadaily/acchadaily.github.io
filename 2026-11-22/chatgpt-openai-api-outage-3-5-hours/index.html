ChatGPT and OpenAI's API go down for about 3.5 hours on Saturday, with up to 75% of requests failing

A database fault during planned maintenance knocked out logins, chats and voice for millions — in India it hit during the evening.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
75%peak share of failed requests
  • ~3.5 hours down
  • Hit chats, login, voice and API
  • Similar maintenance paused

What broke

The outage ran from 1:48 to 5:21 am Pacific time on 21 November — roughly 3:18 to 6:51 pm in India. OpenAI says scheduled maintenance on a database service went wrong when both of its backup copies failed at once. Because that service handles logins, everything that depended on it failed too.

Failures peaked at 75% for both free and paid ChatGPT users, hitting chats, login, search, file uploads and voice mode, plus API services used by developers.

The fix

OpenAI says it has paused similar maintenance and added a way to move traffic off the backup copies, so a repeat should do less damage.

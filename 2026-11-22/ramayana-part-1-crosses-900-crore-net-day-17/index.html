Ramayana: Part 1 crosses ₹900 crore net in India on day 17, joining only Pushpa 2 and Dhurandhar 2

Saturday's collection jumped 74% from Friday, taking the worldwide gross past ₹1,616 crore.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹906.9 CrIndia net after 17 days
  • Day 17: ₹27.40 crore net
  • Worldwide ₹1,616.70 crore
  • Overseas ₹533 crore

Saturday surge

The film earned ₹27.40 crore net on its third Saturday, up 74% from Friday's ₹15.75 crore, according to Sacnilk. Its India net total is now ₹906.90 crore, or ₹1,083.70 crore gross.

Overseas added about ₹10 crore on the day, taking the overseas gross to ₹533 crore and the worldwide total to ₹1,616.70 crore.

In rare company

Sacnilk says it is the third-fastest Indian film to cross ₹900 crore net, after Pushpa 2 and Dhurandhar 2. With Sunday still to come, the gap to Pushpa 2's all-time numbers keeps shrinking.

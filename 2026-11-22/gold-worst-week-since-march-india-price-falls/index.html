Gold has its worst week since March, sliding about 9% globally; 24-carat in India falls to about ₹1.26 lakh per 10 grams

A firm dollar and bets on a US rate hike knocked precious metals, and silver fell even harder.

The slide

Spot gold closed at about $3,537.90 an ounce on Friday, down about 9% for the week, Kitco reports, after touching about $3,502.93 — roughly a 14-month low. A firm dollar, bets that the US Federal Reserve could raise rates in December and the AI-led stock rout were all blamed.

In India, 24-carat gold was ₹1,25,570 per 10 grams on Friday morning, down ₹5,120 on the day; 22-carat was ₹1,15,100 (Goodreturns).

Silver

Silver on MCX fell about 6% on Friday to around ₹1.48 lakh per kg and was heading for a weekly fall of more than 13%. For buyers waiting for the wedding season, prices are now well below this year's highs.

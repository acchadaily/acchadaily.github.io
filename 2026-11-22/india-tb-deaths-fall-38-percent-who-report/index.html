India's TB deaths have fallen 38% since 2015, faster than the world average, WHO says

There is still a long way to go, but the trend is moving the right way.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
-38%India TB deaths, 2015–2025
  • World: down 35%
  • ~2.63 lakh deaths in 2025
  • Report released 12 Nov

The report

The World Health Organization's Global TB Report 2026 says tuberculosis deaths in India fell 38% between 2015 and 2025, compared with 35% worldwide. India recorded about 2.63 lakh TB deaths in 2025, The Week reports.

Work left

India still carries about a quarter of the world's TB cases, and the global goal was a 75% cut in deaths by 2025. WHO is reviewing India's district-level data before publishing new case estimates in early 2027.

IPO week ahead: Foodlink F&B and Integris Medtech open, while Mahanadi Coalfields' ₹8,981 crore IPO closes on Monday

Coal India's subsidiary still needs strong bids on its last day after a slow start.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
0.48xMahanadi Coalfields subscription after day 2
  • MCL price band ₹129–136
  • Integris fresh issue cut to ₹600 crore
  • Crystal Crop lists 26 Nov

Closing Monday

Mahanadi Coalfields' ₹8,981 crore IPO, priced at ₹129–136 a share, closes on 23 November. After two days it was subscribed 0.48 times: big institutions bid 1.37 times their share, retail just 0.24 times. Listing is expected around 27 November.

Opening this week

Foodlink F&B Holdings opens 26–30 November with a ₹192 crore fresh issue plus an offer for sale of 88.35 lakh shares. Integris Medtech opens 27 November–1 December with a ₹600 crore fresh issue, cut from ₹925 crore, plus an offer for sale. Price bands are yet to be announced. Crystal Crop Protection lists on 26 November.

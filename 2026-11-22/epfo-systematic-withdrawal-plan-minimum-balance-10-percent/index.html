EPFO lets retirees take their PF as a monthly payout and cuts the balance that must stay in the account from 25% to 10%

A new Systematic Withdrawal Plan lets the rest of your money keep earning EPF interest.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
10%new minimum balance (was 25%)
  • SWP: monthly, quarterly or half-yearly
  • Final settlement wait: 12 → 2 months
  • ~6.32 lakh members to benefit first

What changed

The EPFO's Central Board of Trustees, chaired by Labour Minister Mansukh Mandaviya, approved a voluntary Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) at its 240th meeting. Retirees can take monthly, quarterly or half-yearly payouts, while the money left behind keeps earning EPF interest. They can stop at any time and take the rest as a lump sum.

The share of the balance that must stay in an EPF account has been cut from 25% to 10%, and the waiting period for final settlement drops from 12 months to two.

Who benefits first

About 6.32 lakh members aged over 55 with more than ₹5 lakh in their PF are expected to benefit first. The formal notification is still pending, and the tax treatment of the payouts has not yet been made clear.

💡 Explained

What is a Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP)?

An SWP lets you take out a fixed amount from your savings at regular intervals — say every month — instead of withdrawing everything at once. The money you leave behind keeps earning returns. Mutual funds have offered SWPs for years; EPFO is now bringing the idea to provident fund savings, so retirees can turn their PF into something like a pension while the rest keeps earning EPF interest.

Shah Rukh and Aryan Khan's D'YAVOL X enters physical stores for the first time with a Golden Goose sneaker collab, on sale from 24 November

The luxury streetwear label is going from online drops to real shelves, with pieces set to appear in SRK's film King.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • On sale from 24 Nov
  • 3 unisex sneaker colourways
  • Second drop early 2027

The drop

D'YAVOL X, the luxury label co-founded by Shah Rukh Khan, Aryan Khan, Leti Blagoeva and Bunty Singh, is teaming up with Italian brand Golden Goose. The collection goes on sale on 24 November in Golden Goose stores in India — the brand's first time in physical retail.

It reworks Golden Goose's Super-Star sneakers in three unisex colourways, carrying the lines "Forever King" and "No crown needed".

More than shoes

There is also a charcoal workwear jacket, four T-shirts, three hoodies, a cropped crystal hoodie and a burgundy cap. A second drop is planned for early 2027.

Pieces from the collection will show up in Shah Rukh Khan's upcoming film King — a neat bit of product placement that turns a movie into a shop window.

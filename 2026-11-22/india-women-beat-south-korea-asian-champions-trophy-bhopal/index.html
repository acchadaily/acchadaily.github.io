India's women beat South Korea 2-1 at the Asian Champions Trophy in Bhopal for their second straight win

Mumtaz Khan and Sunelita Toppo scored as the defending champions stayed unbeaten at home.

In short
2-1India vs South Korea
  • Second straight win
  • Opened with 4-1 over Malaysia

The match

Mumtaz Khan and Sunelita Toppo scored for India at the International Sports Complex in Bhopal on Saturday. Korea pulled one back in the 56th minute, but India held on.

The defending champions had opened with a 4-1 win over Malaysia on 19 November.

Next

India play Thailand on Sunday. In the day's other game, Malaysia and China drew 2-2.

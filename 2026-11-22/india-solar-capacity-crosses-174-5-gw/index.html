India's solar power capacity crosses 174.5 GW, up 34% in a year

The country added about 38.7 GW of solar between January and October alone.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
174.5 GWsolar capacity
  • Up 34.35% in a year
  • Rooftop solar 34.97 GW
  • Renewables (ex-large hydro) 303.46 GW

The numbers

India had about 174.5 GW of solar installed by the end of October 2026, up 34.35% from a year earlier, according to Ministry of New and Renewable Energy data reported by SolarQuarter. October alone added about 3.5 GW, and January–October added about 38.7 GW.

Big ground-mounted plants make up 127.33 GW, and rooftops 34.97 GW.

The bigger picture

Total renewable capacity, not counting large hydro, is now 303.46 GW. India can also make about 230 GW of solar modules a year, so more of the panels going up are made at home.

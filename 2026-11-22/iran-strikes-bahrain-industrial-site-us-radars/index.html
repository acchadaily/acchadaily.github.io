Iran's missile and drone attack hits an industrial site in Bahrain; Tehran says it was aiming at US radars

Bahrain says no one was hurt and has asked the UN Security Council to act.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Bahrain hosts US Fifth Fleet
  • No injuries reported
  • Strait of Hormuz still effectively closed

What happened

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said on Saturday they fired missiles and drones at US Patriot radar systems at the Al-Riffa and Awali bases in Bahrain. Bahrain said a civilian industrial facility was hit and caught fire, with no injuries, and that its air defences shot down several other attacks.

A US official told CBS there was no impact on US forces. Bahrain, home to the US Navy's Fifth Fleet, urged the UN Security Council to act.

Hormuz still shut

On Sunday, Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said reopening the Strait of Hormuz would "not be difficult" if the US met "reasonable conditions". The key oil route remains effectively closed, which keeps pressure on fuel prices worldwide, including in India.

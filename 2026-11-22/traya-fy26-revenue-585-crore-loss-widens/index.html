Hair-care brand Traya's revenue jumps 73% to ₹585 crore in FY26, but its loss widens 23% to ₹29 crore

The D2C brand is growing fast and spending heavily, with Rajkummar Rao fronting its latest campaign.

WhatsApp

The numbers

Traya's operating revenue rose to ₹585.1 crore in FY26 from ₹338.4 crore a year earlier, Inc42 reports. Its net loss grew 23.4% to ₹29 crore, and its EBITDA margin was minus 4.81%.

Founded in 2019 by Saloni Anand and Altaf Saiyed, Traya sells hair-fall treatment plans that mix ayurveda, dermatology and nutrition, and now runs more than 20 offline centres.

What's next

Inc42 reported in October that Traya is in talks to raise about ₹300 crore from Verlinvest at a valuation of ₹2,500–3,000 crore. On the marketing side, actor Rajkummar Rao is the face of the next phase of its "Proof Hai Boss" campaign.

Leeds thrash Chelsea 4-1 for their first win at Stamford Bridge since 1999; leaders Arsenal draw 2-2 at Newcastle

Noah Okafor scored twice as Leeds jumped above Chelsea into sixth in the Premier League.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
4-1Leeds win at Chelsea
  • First Leeds win at Chelsea since 1999
  • Leeds 6th on 17 points
  • Arsenal 6 points clear

Shock in London

Chelsea went ahead through Emegha's first Premier League goal, but Leeds hit back hard. Noah Okafor scored twice, Dominic Calvert-Lewin converted a 53rd-minute penalty and Brenden Aaronson added a fourth in stoppage time.

Leeds go sixth on 17 points, one ahead of Chelsea. Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso admitted his side are in "a fragile moment".

Arsenal held

At Newcastle, Eze, Barnes, Thiaw and Madueke scored in a 2-2 draw. Arsenal goalkeeper David Raya went off with a shoulder injury, but the Gunners stay top, six points clear of Brighton. Elsewhere, Manchester City drew 1-1 with Fulham and Tottenham beat Ipswich 2-0.

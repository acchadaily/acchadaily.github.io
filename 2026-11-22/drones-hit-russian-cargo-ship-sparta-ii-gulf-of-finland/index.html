Drones set a sanctioned Russian military-linked cargo ship on fire in the Gulf of Finland; it drifts into Finnish waters

The Sparta II was sailing from St Petersburg to Kaliningrad when it was hit near Estonia.

accha? daily · 22 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Hit 11 nautical miles off Estonia
  • Crew evacuated
  • Russia claims 80 drones downed

The attack

The Sparta II, run by Oboronlogistika — a logistics company of Russia's Defence Ministry that is under US sanctions — was hit about 11 nautical miles north of Estonia's Vaindloo Island on Saturday. Russia said two drones struck it and its bridge caught fire.

The crew was evacuated. The ship lost steering and drifted into Finnish waters near Loviisa.

Reactions

Finnish Prime Minister Petteri Orpo said Ukraine has the "right to fight back". Finland twice put temporary sea and air restrictions in place. Russia said it shot down 80 drones over the Leningrad region. The strike brings the war closer to NATO's Baltic shores.

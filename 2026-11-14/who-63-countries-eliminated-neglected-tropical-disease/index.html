63 countries have now beaten at least one neglected tropical disease; sleeping sickness cases are down 98% since 1999

A WHO report shows 36% fewer people need treatment for these diseases than in 2010.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read

63countries that eliminated at least one NTD
  • People needing help: 1.41 bn
  • Countries in 2020: 42
  • Guinea worm cases (2025): 10

The progress

According to a WHO report, about 1.41 billion people needed help against neglected tropical diseases in 2024, 36% fewer than in 2010. By June 2026, 63 countries had eliminated at least one of these diseases, up from 42 at the end of 2020. Togo became the first to eliminate four.

Record lows

Sleeping sickness cases have fallen 98% since 1999, and Guinea worm reached a record low of 10 human cases in 2025. WHO calls the progress uneven and fragile, with funding the biggest barrier.

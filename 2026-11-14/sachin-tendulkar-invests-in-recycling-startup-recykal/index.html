Sachin Tendulkar invests in recycling startup Recykal, which made ₹1,498 crore in revenue last year

The cricket legend's newest bet is a waste-management platform used by brands like HUL, Samsung and BMW.

The deal

Sachin Tendulkar has invested an undisclosed amount in Hyderabad-based Recykal as a strategic investor, through an extension of its recently closed $23 million bridge round. Recykal has raised more than $60 million in total, from backers including Morgan Stanley, 360 ONE, Circulate Capital and the Murugappa group.

The business

Recykal connects waste generators with more than 5,000 aggregators and recyclers and works with about 650 brands. Its FY26 gross revenue rose 53.2% to ₹1,498 crore from ₹978 crore, while its loss widened 7% to ₹60 crore.

It is Tendulkar's second startup bet in about two weeks after gaming company Hitwicket; his portfolio also includes Spinny and Kissht.

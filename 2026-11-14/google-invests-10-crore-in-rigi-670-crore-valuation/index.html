Google puts ₹10 crore into creator-economy startup Rigi at a ₹670 crore valuation

A small but strategic cheque as India's microdrama and creator-video race heats up.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹670 crRigi's valuation
  • Google stake: 1.43%
  • Shares allotted: 1,543
  • FY25 revenue: ₹19.92 crore

The deal

Google International has been allotted 1,543 preference shares in Rigi at ₹61,796 each, about ₹10 crore in total, for a 1.43% stake, according to filings reported by Entrackr. That values the Bengaluru startup at about ₹670 crore.

Why it matters

Rigi helps creators earn from their audiences and has moved into microdramas, the short vertical-video series that apps like QuickTV are scaling quickly. Its last big round was about ₹100 crore in January 2023, led by Elevation. In FY25 it reported revenue of ₹19.92 crore and a net loss of ₹20.5 crore.

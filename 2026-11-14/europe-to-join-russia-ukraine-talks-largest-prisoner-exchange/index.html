Europe will join the next round of US-brokered Russia–Ukraine talks; US works toward the 'largest prisoner exchange of the war'

After meeting Putin, US envoys Witkoff and Kushner flew to Ireland to brief Zelensky.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Russian-held territory: ~20% of Ukraine
  • Next round: date not set
  • Previous round: Abu Dhabi

What's new

US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky near Dublin on 12 November, straight after their talks with Vladimir Putin in Moscow. Kushner said both sides agreed to widen the next round to include European partners, and Witkoff said Putin had agreed to let European representatives join.

Kushner also said the US is working toward the "largest prisoner exchange of the war". No numbers were given; Zelensky called it "a real result".

Still unresolved

No date or venue has been set for the next round. Territory remains the main sticking point: Russia holds about a fifth of Ukraine and Kyiv refuses to give up the rest of the Donbas. Attacks by both sides continued while the talks went on.

Ramayana: Part 1 reaches about ₹1,110 crore worldwide in 9 days, with ₹47.85 crore net on its second Friday

Nitesh Tiwari's epic held strongly on day 9, and its second weekend should push it further.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹1,110 crworldwide gross in 9 days
  • India net: ₹612.45 crore
  • Overseas: ~₹378.50 crore
  • Day 9 drop: 10.6%

The numbers

According to Sacnilk, Ramayana: Part 1 earned ₹47.85 crore net in India on its ninth day, Friday 13 November, down just 10.6% from day 8. Hindi brought in ₹36.25 crore of that, with the rest from the Malayalam, Kannada, Tamil and Telugu versions.

Its India net total stands at ₹612.45 crore (₹732.20 crore gross), and overseas earnings are estimated at ₹378.50 crore, taking the worldwide total to about ₹1,110.70 crore.

Why it matters

The film, starring Ranbir Kapoor and Yash, opened on 5 November and crossed ₹1,000 crore worldwide in eight days. A second Friday this strong suggests it has a long run ahead. Sacnilk notes overseas numbers are rough estimates.

US halts every departing flight nationwide for hours; over 4,000 cancellations and 10,000+ delays

The FAA blamed a 'telecommunication outage' in air traffic control systems. Officials said there was no sign of foul play.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
4,000+US flights cancelled
  • Ground stop: 3:54–8:15 pm ET
  • Delays: 10,000+
  • Last nationwide stop: Jan 2023

What happened

The US Federal Aviation Administration ordered a nationwide ground stop on all departing flights on Friday, 13 November, at 3:54 pm ET, citing "extraordinary circumstances" and safety. It was lifted at 8:15 pm ET. FlightAware showed more than 4,000 cancellations and over 10,000 delays.

The FAA and the White House called the cause a "telecommunication outage". Transport Secretary Sean Duffy blamed "an incredibly old air traffic control system" whose routers were overloaded with data.

Why it matters

It was the first nationwide ground stop since January 2023. Homeland Security said there was no indication the outage was malicious. Knock-on delays were expected through the weekend, including for international travellers connecting through US airports.

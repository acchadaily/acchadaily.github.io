An Indian-made 'surgical GPS' has helped in over 20,000 operations across 200+ hospitals

Bengaluru's HRS Navigation built a cheaper alternative to imported systems, so precise surgery can reach smaller cities.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
20,000+surgeries supported
  • Hospitals: 200+
  • Founded: 2015
  • Based in: Bengaluru

How it works

HRS Navigation, founded in Bengaluru in 2015 by Arpit and Shweta Paliwal, makes easyNav. It builds a 3D map of the patient from CT scans and uses infrared cameras to track a surgeon's instruments in real time, helping locate tumours and see how close they are to nerves and blood vessels.

Why it matters

Such systems are mostly imported and costly, often with extra charges for every procedure. easyNav fits into existing operating theatres and avoids those per-surgery costs. The company says it is now in more than 200 hospitals.

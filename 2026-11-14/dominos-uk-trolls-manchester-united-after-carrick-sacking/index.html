Domino's UK trolls Manchester United again, with a pizza-box reel after the club sacks Michael Carrick

The pizza chain has made mocking United a running joke, and its latest post landed within hours of the club's managerial change.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • United's league wins: 1 in 10
  • 'Not you, Erik' van: 2024
  • Platform: Instagram reel

The post

After Manchester United sacked manager Michael Carrick, Domino's UK posted an Instagram reel showing raw pizza ingredients being shaken around inside a pizza box. The caption joked about "whoever United appoint next trying to cook up something special", followed by a simple "Good luck."

The joke works because it says two things at once: United's next boss is starting with a messy box of ingredients, and Domino's is the expert at turning those into something people want.

Why United are an easy target

United are in the Premier League relegation zone, with one league win in their first 10 matches. Reports say the club is close to hiring Mauricio Pochettino, but nothing has been announced.

A long-running bit

This is not a one-off. Domino's UK put out a 'We're hiring (not you, Erik)' van in 2024 aimed at then-manager Erik ten Hag, posted after United's loss to Brighton in September 2026, and predicted in August 2026 that United would be relegated.

The marketing lesson

Topical banter like this costs little and travels fast because fans of rival clubs share it. The risk is annoying United supporters, but Domino's has clearly decided the attention is worth it. Indian brands like Amul and Zomato use the same playbook around cricket and pop culture.

Dubai airport halts SpiceJet check-ins over about ₹35.8 crore in unpaid dues; Dubai–India flights cancelled

If you hold a SpiceJet ticket from Dubai, check your booking: the airline has been told to rebook passengers and provide hotels.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹35.8 crunpaid dues to Dubai Airports
  • Dues: AED 14 million
  • Last payment: before 13 Oct
  • Daily cancellations: 15–20

What happened

Dubai Airports stopped checking in SpiceJet passengers from 12 November over unpaid charges of AED 14 million, about ₹35.8 crore. It says SpiceJet has not paid since 13 October and missed a deadline after a warning on 9 November.

Four flights to Amritsar, Jaipur, Mumbai and Delhi were cancelled on 12 November, and reports say no SpiceJet flight left Dubai for India on Friday.

What it means for passengers

Dubai's aviation regulator summoned SpiceJet's station manager and ordered the airline to inform passengers, rebook them, and provide hotels and transport. SpiceJet is flying about 65 flights a day overall and cancelling 15–20 daily, according to Business Today, as it battles money troubles.

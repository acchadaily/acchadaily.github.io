Dhanush and Mammootty's Tamil action thriller OM drops its trailer; in cinemas worldwide on 20 November

The next big Tamil release arrives in a week, with Sai Pallavi also in the cast.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Release date: 20 Nov
  • Director: Rajkumar Periasamy
  • Language: Tamil

The film

OM – Chapter 1: Udhiram – The Blood Wood is a Tamil action thriller directed by Rajkumar Periasamy, starring Dhanush and Mammootty, with Sai Pallavi. It is produced by Wunderbar Films and RTake Studios. The trailer came out on 13 November, and the film releases worldwide on 20 November.

The box-office backdrop

It arrives as Ramayana: Part 1 dominates cinemas and Suriya's Scene has crossed ₹200 crore worldwide. Friday's smaller Telugu openers, such as Donga Naa Koduku, made around ₹1 crore net on day one, according to early Sacnilk estimates.

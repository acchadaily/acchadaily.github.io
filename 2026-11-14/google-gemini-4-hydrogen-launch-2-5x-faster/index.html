Google launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a faster model it says finishes tasks 2.5x quicker than Gemini 3.8 Flash

It is rolling out across the Gemini app, Search and developer tools at once, priced at $1.50 per million input tokens.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
2.5xfaster than Gemini 3.8 Flash, says Google
  • Terminal-Bench 4.1: 57%
  • Output price: $7.50 / mn tokens
  • Context: ~1 mn tokens

What it is

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen on 12 November as the fast, lower-cost tier of its Gemini 4 family, replacing Gemini 3.8 Flash. It is aimed at coding, documents and AI agents that carry out multi-step tasks. Google says it scores 57% on the Terminal-Bench 4.1 coding test, against 18.2% for 3.8 Flash.

It is available through the Gemini app, Google Search, AI Studio, the Gemini API and Android Studio, with a context window of about 1 million tokens.

Pricing

Developers pay $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, with batch use at half price. That is more expensive than older Flash models, a sign Google is betting buyers will pay for speed and smarter agents.

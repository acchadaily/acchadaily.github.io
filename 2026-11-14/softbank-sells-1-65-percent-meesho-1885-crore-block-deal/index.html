SoftBank sells another 1.65% of Meesho for ₹1,885 crore in a block deal; Fidelity, GIC and Norges Bank buy in

Big global funds are lining up for India's newly listed consumer internet stocks.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹1,885 crvalue of SoftBank's Meesho sale
  • Shares sold: 7.64 crore
  • Sale price: ₹246.75
  • SoftBank stake now: ~5.19%

The deal

SoftBank sold 7.64 crore Meesho shares at ₹246.75 each on 13 November, about 4% below the previous close of ₹257.07. Buyers included Fidelity, Societe Generale, Norges Bank, Singapore's GIC and HDFC Mutual Fund. SoftBank's stake falls from 6.84% to about 5.19%. It had already sold 1.73% for ₹1,650 crore in September.

Meesho's numbers

Meesho is worth about ₹1.19 lakh crore on the stock market. In July–September its revenue rose 38% to ₹4,132 crore and its loss narrowed 68% to ₹132 crore. It is also being added to the MSCI India Standard Index, which can bring in money from index-tracking funds.

Sensex falls 319 points to 81,592 and Nifty ends at 25,144, snapping a three-day winning streak

Oil worries and the jump in retail inflation weighed on rate-sensitive stocks; FMCG was the only sector in the green.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
-0.39%Sensex on Friday
  • Sensex weekly: +0.7%
  • Nifty weekly: +0.5%
  • Only gaining sector: FMCG

The close

The Sensex fell 318.56 points, or 0.39%, to 81,592.45 on Friday, 13 November. The Nifty 50 dropped 155.05 points, or 0.61%, to 25,144.30. Every sector index fell except FMCG; auto, metal and realty lost about 1% each.

Shriram Finance (-3.37%) and Tata Motors PV (-3.31%) were the biggest Nifty losers, while Eicher Motors (+1.34%) and HUL (+0.96%) led the gainers.

Why

Brent crude stayed near $96 after attacks on Saudi energy sites, and October's 6.09% retail inflation raised fears of an RBI rate hike. Still, both indices ended the week higher for a third week in a row: the Sensex rose 0.7% and the Nifty 0.5%. Foreign investors have kept buying, with November inflows the highest since December 2020.

A new scorpion species has been found in Odisha's Similipal Tiger Reserve, the first of its kind recorded in the state

Named Liocheles similipalensis, it's a reminder of how much of India's wildlife is still undiscovered.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
7known species in the genus now
  • Length: 44–53 mm
  • Altitude: ~1,000 m
  • Found in: Similipal only

The find

Researchers have described a new scorpion, Liocheles similipalensis, named after Similipal Tiger Reserve in Mayurbhanj district. It is 44–53 mm long, with a dark black body and a bluish sheen, and lives in moist forest among rocks at about 1,000 m altitude. It is the first Liocheles species confirmed in Odisha, and the genus now has seven known species.

Teamwork

Forest staff first collected the specimens and sent them to specialists at the Thackeray Wildlife Foundation, who described it with Similipal's field director. So far the species is known only from the reserve.

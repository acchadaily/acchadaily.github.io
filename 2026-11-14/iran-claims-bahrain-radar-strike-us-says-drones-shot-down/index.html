Iran says it destroyed a US radar in Bahrain; US Central Command says both Iranian drones were shot down with no damage

The two sides gave opposite accounts, as Gulf states urge Washington to hold off on wider strikes.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Drones launched (US): 2
  • US Fifth Fleet base: Bahrain
  • Mediators: Qatar, Pakistan

Two versions

Iran's Revolutionary Guard said on 13 November that it destroyed a US early-warning radar in Bahrain, calling it retaliation for a US strike a day earlier on a telecoms tower in southern Iran's Hormozgan province. US Central Command said two Iranian one-way attack drones were launched toward Bahrain and both were shot down, with "no injuries or damage". Bahrain hosts the US Navy's Fifth Fleet.

The wider picture

President Trump said Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, the UAE and Bahrain had asked him to postpone broader attacks on Iran, and that he was holding off for now. The US naval blockade of Iranian ports remains in place. Qatar and Pakistan are still trying to mediate.

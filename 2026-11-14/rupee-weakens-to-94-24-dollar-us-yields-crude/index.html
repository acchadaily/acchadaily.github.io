Rupee weakens 21 paise to 94.24 against the dollar as US yields and crude rise

Hot US inflation data and bets on a December Fed hike lifted the dollar; strong foreign buying limited the fall.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
94.24rupee per US dollar, Friday close
  • Previous close: 94.03
  • Dollar index: ~103.2
  • Expected range: 93.85–94.60

The move

The rupee closed at 94.24 per dollar on Friday, 13 November, down 21 paise from 94.03, according to HDFC Sky's market wrap. The dollar index rose to about 103.2, near a 19-month high, after US inflation data came in hot. Traders are pricing in a high chance of a US Federal Reserve rate hike in December.

What's holding it

Steady buying of Indian shares by foreign investors kept the rupee from sliding further. Dealers expect it to trade between roughly 93.85 and 94.60 in the near term, with oil prices and Gulf tensions the main risks.

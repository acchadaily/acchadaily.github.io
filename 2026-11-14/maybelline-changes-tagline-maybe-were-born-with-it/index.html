Maybelline rewrites its famous 'Maybe she's born with it' tagline after 35 years: it's now 'Maybe. We're born with it.'

One of advertising's best-known lines just lost a pronoun, and marketers are already debating what the swap says.

What changed

Maybelline New York has quietly updated the slogan it has used on and off since 1991. The old line was "Maybe she's born with it. Maybe it's Maybelline." The new one reads "Maybe. We're born with it. Maybe. It's Maybelline."

The edit is tiny on paper: 'she's' becomes 'we're', and the line is broken up with full stops. But in branding terms it shifts the voice from talking about a woman to speaking as a group that includes the reader.

Why the line matters

Few taglines are as famous. CBS Outdoor once ranked it the most recognisable tagline of the past 150 years. Maybelline dropped it in 2015 for "Make It Happen", then brought it back in 2024, and has now reworked it again.

afaqs, which reported the change, reads it as a move towards a more inclusive, gender-neutral message. That is the publication's interpretation; Maybelline has not been quoted explaining the reason.

A 111-year-old brand

The brand began in 1915 as Lash-Brow-Ine. Founder Thomas Lyle Williams later renamed it by joining his sister Mabel's name with Vaseline. L'Oréal bought it in 1996 and added 'New York' to the name.

The takeaway for marketers

Legacy brands rarely touch a line people can sing back to them. Maybelline kept the rhythm and the 'Maybe' hook, and changed just one word, a low-risk way to modernise without losing the memory.

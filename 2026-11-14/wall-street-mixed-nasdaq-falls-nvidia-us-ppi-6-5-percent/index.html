Wall Street ends mixed: Nasdaq falls 0.91% as Nvidia slides again and US wholesale inflation hits 6.5%

Tech stocks fell while consumer staples and energy held the Dow up, but all three indices still gained over the week.

The close

On Friday, 13 November, the Dow edged up 0.1% to 52,347.55, the S&P 500 fell 0.4% to 7,561.67 and the Nasdaq dropped 0.91% to 25,136.27. Tech was the worst sector, down 1.7%. Nvidia fell another 2% and closed below $4 trillion in market value for the first time since July 2025.

The inflation problem

US producer prices rose 0.3% in October and 6.5% from a year earlier, the highest since November 2022. Combined with the earlier consumer price data, that is pushing up bets on a Fed rate hike. Over the week, the S&P 500 still rose 2.5%, the Dow 2.1% and the Nasdaq 2.3%.

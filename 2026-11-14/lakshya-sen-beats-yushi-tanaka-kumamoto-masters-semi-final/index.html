Lakshya Sen beats home player Yushi Tanaka 21-16, 21-15 to reach the Kumamoto Masters semi-finals; Srikanth is out

India's last player in the draw now faces Indonesia's Alwi Farhan, who has beaten him twice before.

The quarter-final

Eighth seed Lakshya Sen beat Japan's Yushi Tanaka 21-16, 21-15 in about 40 minutes on Friday, 13 November, at the Kumamoto Masters, a Super 500 event in Japan. Kidambi Srikanth, who had upset fifth seed Kodai Naraoka a round earlier, lost his quarter-final to Japan's Koki Watanabe in straight games after a tight first game.

Up next

Lakshya plays Indonesia's sixth seed Alwi Farhan in the semi-final on Saturday, 14 November, around 7:20 am IST. Farhan has won both of their previous meetings. The winner meets Koki Watanabe or second seed Kunlavut Vitidsarn in the final.

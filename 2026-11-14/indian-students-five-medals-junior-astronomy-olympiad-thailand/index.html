All five Indian students win medals, including 2 golds, at the Junior Astronomy Olympiad in Thailand

104 students from 25 countries competed; every member of India's team came home with a medal.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
5/5Indian students who won medals
  • Countries: 25
  • Students: 104
  • India: 2 gold, 2 silver, 1 bronze

The results

At the 5th International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics for Juniors in Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand (1–8 November), Siddharth Laxmisha from Puducherry and Swara Srujal Patel from Gujarat won gold. Aarna Chaplot from Kota and Krish Bhaskar from Bagar in Rajasthan won silver, and Akshara Gulab Ambone from Maharashtra won bronze.

Made in Kolkata

The National Council of Science Museums selected and trained the team, with its training camp held at Kolkata's Birla Industrial & Technological Museum.

India's forex reserves rise $2.49 billion to $719.55 billion as gold holdings climb to $104.8 billion

Most of the weekly gain came from the higher value of gold, not fresh dollar buying.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$719.5Bforex reserves, week to 6 Nov
  • Gold reserves: $104.8 bn
  • Foreign currency assets: $591.35 bn
  • Record high: $785.7 bn (Sept)

The numbers

RBI data released on 13 November show India's foreign exchange reserves rose $2.49 billion to $719.55 billion in the week ended 6 November. Gold reserves rose about $2.21 billion to $104.8 billion, while foreign currency assets added $232 million to $591.35 billion.

Reserves are $32.51 billion higher than a year ago.

The bigger picture

Reserves are still about $66 billion below the all-time high of $785.7 billion reached in the week ended 4 September 2026. They fell for several weeks as the RBI sold dollars to steady the rupee, which is trading near record lows around 94 to the dollar.

Gaza's winter crisis: 150,000 tents are stuck at the borders as dozens of nations urge Israel to ease aid limits

Rain is flooding tents in Khan Younis, and the UN says about 3.5 lakh displaced people face freezing conditions.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read

  • Displaced facing floods: ~3.5 lakh
  • Tents let in (Israel): 91,000
  • Living in shelters: most of ~2 mn

The situation

UN aid chief Tom Fletcher says 150,000 tents are waiting at Gaza's borders while about 350,000 displaced people face flooding and cold. Most of Gaza's roughly 2 million people live in tents or makeshift shelters. A nine-month-old boy died of hypothermia this week after his family's tent in Khan Younis flooded, aid groups reported.

The diplomatic push

A joint statement read at the UN on 12 November by the UK's envoy, backed by about 49 countries (some outlets count 53 countries and organisations), asked Israel to ease limits on shelter, fuel, water, sanitation and medical items. The US did not sign. Israel's UN ambassador rejected the initiative and blamed Hamas, and Prime Minister Netanyahu said Israel has allowed in 91,000 tents.

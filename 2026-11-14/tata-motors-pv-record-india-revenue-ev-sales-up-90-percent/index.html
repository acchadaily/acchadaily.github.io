Tata Motors PV posts record ₹20,400 crore India revenue as EV sales jump 90%, but JLR charges push it into a loss

The Indian car business, led by the Sierra and EVs, is booming. Jaguar Land Rover is the drag.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
+90%jump in Tata's EV volumes
  • India revenue: ₹20,400 crore
  • EV market share: 42.8%
  • Sierra sales since Jan: 70,000

India is flying

Tata Motors' passenger vehicle business reported record India revenue of ₹20,400 crore for July–September, up 51%. It is now India's No. 2 carmaker with a 14.3% share. EV volumes rose about 90% to around 15,700 a month, giving Tata 42.8% of India's electric car market.

The new Tata Sierra has sold 70,000 units since January, and October was a record month with 75,400 vehicles.

The JLR problem

Consolidated revenue rose 34% to about ₹96,900 crore, but exceptional charges of about ₹6,700 crore pushed the company into a net loss. Jaguar Land Rover's revenue rose 20.3% to £5.89 billion; the Defender sold 30,000 units while Jaguar sold just 800 as it transitions to an all-electric brand. The stock fell about 3.3% on Friday.

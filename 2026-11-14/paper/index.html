Business

Paytm gets RBI's in-principle nod to run wallets again; shares close at ₹1,939.70, about 10% below the ₹2,150 IPO price

Two and a half years after the Payments Bank crackdown took its wallet away, Paytm is a step closer to getting it back, and its stock is nearly back where it listed.

The Reserve Bank of India has given Paytm Payments Services Ltd (PPSL), a wholly owned subsidiary of Paytm's parent One 97 Communications, an in-principle approval to issue and run prepaid payment instruments. That category covers digital wallets, the product that made Paytm a household name.

It is not the final green light yet. PPSL has to submit a system and cybersecurity audit within six months, or the approval lapses, and it still needs final authorisation from the RBI before it can issue a single wallet. Paytm applied in July 2026.

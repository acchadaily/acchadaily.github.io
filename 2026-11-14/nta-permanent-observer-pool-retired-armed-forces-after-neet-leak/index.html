After the NEET-UG leak, NTA will build a permanent pool of independent exam observers, including retired armed forces personnel

Observers will report straight to the testing agency instead of local exam staff, for exams like NEET-UG, JEE Main and CUET.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Observer groups: 3
  • Senior posts: 13
  • Applications close: 1 Dec

The plan

The National Testing Agency has invited expressions of interest for a standing pool of exam observers and for exam centres. Three groups can apply: retired central and state government officers; retired armed forces and paramilitary personnel; and retired or serving government teachers and professors.

Observers will report directly to NTA rather than to city coordinators or centre superintendents. At centres abroad, Indian nationals living in the same city will be empanelled.

Why now

The move follows this year's NEET-UG paper leak and aims to make supervision independent of local centre staff. NTA is also hiring for 13 senior posts on deputation, including seven directors; applications close on 1 December.

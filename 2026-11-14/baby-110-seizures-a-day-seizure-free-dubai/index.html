A baby who had about 110 seizures a day became seizure-free within 30 minutes of an experimental drug in Dubai

Genome sequencing found the exact faulty gene, letting doctors pick a targeted treatment.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
110seizures a day before treatment
  • Earlier treatments failed: 7
  • Discharged: 21 Sept
  • Time to effect: ~30 min

The case

Baby Adnan was born with an extremely rare genetic epilepsy and had a seizure roughly every 13 minutes. Seven treatments failed. Whole-genome sequencing found the gene variant behind it, and doctors at King's College Hospital London – Dubai gave him the experimental oral drug ABS-1230 on a compassionate-use approval on 14 September. His seizures stopped within 30 minutes.

Now

He went home on 21 September and was still seizure-free at follow-ups into November. He is reported to be the youngest patient in the world to receive the drug. It's a single case, and doctors will keep monitoring him.

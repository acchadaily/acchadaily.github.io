Andhra Pradesh signs ₹1.41 lakh crore of deals in a day, led by a 1,400 MW data-centre campus; Agnikul plans orbital data centres in Tirupati

Visakhapatnam and Tirupati are pitching themselves as hubs for AI data centres and space tech.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹1.41L crdeals signed on day 2 of the summit
  • AirTrunk campus: 1,400 MW
  • Agnikul investment: ₹510 crore
  • Expected jobs (day 2): 10,919

The deals

On 13 November, day two of the CII Partnership Summit in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh signed 12 agreements worth ₹1,41,585 crore, expected to create about 10,919 jobs. The biggest was AirTrunk India with JCK Infra, for 1,400 MW of data-centre campuses worth ₹1,03,018 crore. Dixon Technologies committed ₹700 crore.

Data centres in space

IIT Madras-incubated rocket startup Agnikul Cosmos signed an MoU for a ₹510 crore facility in Tirupati district, with 350 jobs, to build platforms for orbital data centres. Agnikul plans to turn the upper stage of its Agnibaan rocket into a host for computing hardware in orbit. The idea is still experimental, and some in the industry question the economics.

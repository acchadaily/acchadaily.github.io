India bowled out for 89 as New Zealand win the 4th ODI by 244 runs; Matt Henry takes six wickets

India needed one win to seal the series, and crashed inside 27 overs instead. Sunday's final game at the same ground now decides it.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
89India all out, in 26.4 overs
  • New Zealand total: 333
  • Will Young: 125 off 108
  • Series: India lead 2-1

What happened

At Bay Oval in Mount Maunganui on Friday, 13 November, New Zealand piled up 333 all out in 50 overs. Opener Will Young led the way with 125 off 108 balls, hitting 14 fours and 4 sixes.

Chasing 334, India collapsed to 89 all out in 26.4 overs. Fast bowler Matt Henry took six wickets and was named Player of the Match. He removed Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli and captain Shreyas Iyer early, and the chase never recovered.

The scorecard story

Ishan Kishan was India's top scorer with just 27 off 53 balls. Matthew Fisher chipped in with three wickets for New Zealand.

Series state

India still lead the five-match series 2-1. They had won the first two games, and the third in Hamilton was washed out, so this was New Zealand's first win of the series.

What's next

The decider is on Sunday, 15 November, again at Bay Oval, starting 7:30 am IST. India need to win to take the series; a New Zealand win would make it 2-2.

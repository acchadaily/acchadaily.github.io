Beavers are back around Loch Ness: a family of six has been released about 500 years after they vanished

It's the first step in a plan to bring at least 15 beaver families back around the famous Scottish loch.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
500 yrssince beavers lived around Loch Ness
  • Beavers released: 6
  • Target: 15+ families
  • Estate size: 10,000 acres

The release

A family of six beavers was released on 4 November into a small lake on the 10,000-acre Dundreggan rewilding estate in Glenmoriston, in the Scottish Highlands. The Ness Beaver Project, run by Trees for Life and Beaver Trust, plans at least 15 beaver family groups around Loch Ness over three years. NatureScot licensed the release in October.

Why beavers

Beavers were hunted out of Britain centuries ago. Their dams slow rivers, which can cut downstream flooding, clean water and create wetlands that help many other species.

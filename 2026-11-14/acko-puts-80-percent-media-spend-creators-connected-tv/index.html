ACKO now puts about 80% of its media spend into creators and connected TV, says its CMO

The insurance brand has moved away from traditional TV over 18 months, and is preparing for AI search.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
80%of ACKO's media spend on creators + CTV
  • CTV reach target: 25–30 mn homes
  • Bike-insurance leads: +21%
  • Shift took: 18 months

The shift

ACKO's chief marketing officer Nitin Khanna told afaqs the insurer moved about 80% of its media budget to creators and connected TV over the past 18 months. Its connected-TV push targets 25–30 million households in India's top 10–15 cities, and its new campaign has three films with creators including Jordindian and Vishaka Biswas.

What's working

Testing Google's AI Max lifted car-insurance leads by 8% and bike-insurance leads by 21%, the company says. ACKO is also shifting from classic SEO toward being found in AI-generated answers, but says it is cautious about advertising inside ChatGPT. Cricketer MS Dhoni is both a brand ambassador and an investor.

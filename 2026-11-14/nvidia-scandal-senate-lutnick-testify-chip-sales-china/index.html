Nvidia fallout reaches the US Senate: Commerce Secretary Lutnick to testify on 10 December over AI chip sales to China

Lawmakers want to know how a Singapore firm bought over $4.8 billion of Nvidia chips that may have reached China.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$4.8Bof Nvidia chips bought by Megaspeed
  • Lutnick hearing: 10 Dec
  • Huang asked for: 3 Dec
  • Nvidia shares Friday: -2%

What's new

Senate Banking Committee chair Tim Scott has asked Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick to testify on 10 December, and his spokesperson said he agreed to appear voluntarily. Scott has also asked former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang to testify on 3 December.

The focus is Megaspeed International, a Singapore company that bought more than $4.8 billion of advanced Nvidia AI chips over two years. US officials suspect some were diverted to China, and lawmakers are asking why Commerce delayed adding it to a trade blacklist.

Why it matters

Huang resigned on 12 November; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and Harvey C. Jones is Nvidia's first board chair. Senators from both parties are now pressing on export controls, which could shape future US rules on selling AI chips abroad. Nvidia shares fell another 2% on Friday and closed below $4 trillion in market value.

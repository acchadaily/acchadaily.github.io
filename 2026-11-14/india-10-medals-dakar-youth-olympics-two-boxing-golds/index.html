India finish the Dakar Youth Olympics with 10 medals, their second-best haul ever, including first-ever boxing golds

A small team of 17 athletes brought home medals in seven sports, with several Indian firsts.

accha? daily · 14 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
10medals for India at Dakar 2026
  • Gold: 2
  • Athletes sent: 17
  • Best ever: 13 medals (2018)

The haul

India ended the Youth Olympic Games in Dakar, Senegal, on 13 November with 10 medals: 2 gold, 3 silver and 5 bronze. Only the 2018 Games, with 13 medals, were better. Boxers Lairenlakpam Ambekar Meetei (boys' 50kg) and Gunjan (girls' 48kg) won India's first-ever Youth Olympic boxing golds.

Silvers came in judo and badminton, among others. Swimmer Dhinidhi Desinghu won two bronzes, becoming the first Indian with two medals at a single edition, and Shourya Ambure won India's first Youth Olympic athletics medal in the 100m hurdles.

Why it matters

India sent just 17 athletes in 11 sports, and nine of them won medals. Many are products of the Khelo India programme. These were the first Olympic-movement Games ever held in Africa.

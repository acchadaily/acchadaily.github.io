India's services sector picks up speed in October

The HSBC India Services PMI rose to 55.8, showing faster growth in businesses like IT, banking, travel and retail.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
55.8Services PMI in October (55.2 in September)
  • Services PMI: 55.8 (Oct)
  • Up from 55.2 (Sep)
  • Above 50 = growth

What happened

The HSBC India Services PMI — a monthly survey of purchasing managers — rose to 55.8 in October from 55.2 in September. Any reading above 50 means the sector is growing.

Why it matters

Services make up more than half of India's economy. A stronger reading during the festive month suggests demand is holding up even as some other numbers look softer. Other data this week was mixed: railway freight hit a record in October, while FMCG sales growth slowed.

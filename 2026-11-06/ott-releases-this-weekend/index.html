What to watch this weekend on OTT

Dulquer Salmaan's I'm Game, Netflix's Dilwali Diwali and a new season of Black Doves lead this week's releases.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • I'm Game — JioHotstar, 6 Nov
  • Dilwali Diwali — Netflix, 5 Nov
  • Motu Patlu movie — JioHotstar, 8 Nov

The big ones

I'm Game, starring Dulquer Salmaan, arrives on JioHotstar on 6 November in Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada. Netflix has Dilwali Diwali (from the makers of Musafir Cafe) and the suspense series Black Doves, both from 5 November.

SonyLIV has Haiwaan and Meesaya Murukku 2 on 6 November, and ZEE5 has Last Man In Tower in Hindi.

For the family

Motu Patlu & The Mystery of Nagmani lands on JioHotstar on 8 November, and Minions & Monsters is already streaming there. Netflix also has the festive A Dog's Perfect Christmas.

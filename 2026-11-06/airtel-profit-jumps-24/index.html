Airtel's profit jumps 24% as users pay more

Bharti Airtel's quarterly profit rose to ₹10,710 crore, helped by higher revenue per user.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹271average revenue per user, up from ₹256
  • Profit: ₹10,710 crore
  • Revenue: +16.7%
  • Customers: ~69 crore

The numbers

Airtel's consolidated net profit for the September quarter rose 24% to ₹10,710 crore. Revenue grew 16.7% to about ₹60,878 crore, and India revenue rose 9.4%.

Why it matters

The key number is ARPU — how much the average customer pays each month. It rose to ₹271 from ₹256 as more users moved to pricier plans. Airtel now has about 69 crore customers worldwide.

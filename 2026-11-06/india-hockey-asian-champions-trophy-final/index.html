India storm into the Asian Champions Trophy final

The defending champions beat South Korea 8-1 in the semi-final in Punjab to reach a record seventh final.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp

  • League stage: India 8-0 South Korea
  • Record 7th ACT final

What happened

India's men's hockey team beat South Korea 8-1 in the semi-final of the Asian Champions Trophy, being played in Punjab, according to The Bridge. It is India's seventh final in the tournament — a record.

India had already beaten Korea 8-0 in the league stage and finished the group unbeaten.

Why it matters

India are the defending champions and have been the strongest team in Asia this year. Winning again would build confidence ahead of a new-look squad for the FIH Pro League, which coach Craig Fulton has said he plans to field.

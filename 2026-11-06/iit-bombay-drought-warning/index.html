IIT Bombay's free tool warns farmers of drought 4 weeks early

KrishiDrought uses satellites and AI to forecast dry soil for every district in India.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
4 weeksadvance drought warning
  • Up to 4 weeks ahead
  • All districts in India
  • Free to use

What happened

Researchers at IIT Bombay launched KrishiDrought, a free website that forecasts soil moisture up to four weeks ahead for every district in India, using satellite data, weather forecasts and deep learning.

A colour-coded map shows where soil is likely to be drier or wetter than normal.

Why it matters

A few weeks' notice helps farmers plan irrigation and crops before a dry spell hits. It's free for anyone at KrishiDrought.in.

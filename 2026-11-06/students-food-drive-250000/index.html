Students fill a football field with food for families in need

A New Jersey high school collected more than 250,000 pounds of food in one drive.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
250,000 lbof food collected
  • 250,000+ pounds
  • ~30 food banks helped
  • Record drive

What happened

Hundreds of students at Howell High School in New Jersey filled their football field with more than 250,000 pounds of food — the biggest drive in the organisers' history.

The food goes to about 30 food banks across the state.

Why we love it

It's a reminder of what a few hundred teenagers can do in a few days when they decide to help.

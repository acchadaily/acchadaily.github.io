A viral video got a PVR juice counter shut in Bengaluru

A moviegoer alleged that used cups were being reused. Food safety officials inspected — and shut the counter.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Where: Forum Mall, South Bengaluru
  • Action: notice issued, counter shut
  • Status: PVR investigating

What happened

A customer at the PVR INOX multiplex in Forum Mall, South Bengaluru, posted a video on Instagram showing a stack of used disposable cups. He alleged that staff were reusing cups that customers had already drunk from.

The video spread quickly. On its own, a video can't prove the allegation — but it was enough to get officials to act.

What the authorities did

Karnataka food safety officers inspected the outlet. The state said they found violations of food safety rules, issued a notice, and shut the juice counter. The exact violations haven't been made public yet.

What the companies said

Forum South Bengaluru apologised to the customer and said the PVR team was investigating the complaint and would take action.

Why it matters

It shows how fast one customer's video can turn into government action against a big brand. For brands, the lesson is simple: in 2026, every customer is a reporter.

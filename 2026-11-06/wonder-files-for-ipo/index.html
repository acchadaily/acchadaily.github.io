Grubhub owner Wonder files to go public

Marc Lore's food company — which also owns Blue Apron — has confidentially filed for a US IPO.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Raised ~$650M in July
  • Reported $9B valuation (private)
  • Owns Grubhub, Blue Apron, Spyce

What happened

Wonder Group, founded by Marc Lore (who previously sold Jet.com to Walmart), confidentially submitted papers for a US stock market listing. The number of shares, price and valuation are not set yet.

Wonder owns food delivery app Grubhub, meal-kit brand Blue Apron and kitchen-robotics firm Spyce. It raised about $650 million in July at a reported $9 billion valuation, and plans drone deliveries in Texas with Zipline.

Why it matters

Wonder is trying to combine restaurants, delivery and kitchens under one roof — a similar bet to what Swiggy and Zomato make in India with their own kitchens and quick-commerce. Its IPO will be a test of whether investors like that model.

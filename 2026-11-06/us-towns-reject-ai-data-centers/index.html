40 US towns vote NO to AI data centers

Voters rejected new data-center projects, angry about power bills and pollution.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
~80%of voters in Trenton, Ohio backed a ban
  • ~40 towns said no
  • Trenton, Ohio: ~80% for a ban
  • Ban covers projects over 25 MW

What happened

In this week's US local elections, voters in nearly 40 towns rejected proposed data-center projects. In Trenton, Ohio, nearly 80% voted to ban large data centers that need more than 25 megawatts of power.

Why it matters

AI runs on huge data centers that use massive amounts of electricity, water and land. Growing local pushback — plus fears that the new Congress could add rules — was one of the reasons tech stocks crashed on Thursday.

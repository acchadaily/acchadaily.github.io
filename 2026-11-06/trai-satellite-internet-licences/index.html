TRAI proposes separate licences for satellite internet

The telecom regulator wants satellite players under a new set of rules — and not selling directly to customers.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Separate satellite authorisation
  • Network-as-a-service model
  • No direct-to-consumer sales

What happened

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) on Thursday recommended a separate authorisation for satellite communication providers, along with a ‘network-as-a-service’ model.

Under the proposal, satellite companies would not be allowed to sell telecom services directly to consumers.

Why it matters

Satellite internet is the next big battleground in Indian telecom. These rules will shape how players like Starlink, Jio and Airtel's satellite ventures reach customers. The government still has to decide whether to accept TRAI's recommendations.

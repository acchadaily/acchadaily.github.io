Palantir's revenue nearly doubles to $2.31 billion

The data and AI software company had its first $2 billion quarter and raised its outlook for the year.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$2.31Bquarterly revenue, nearly double last year
  • Q3 revenue: $2.31 billion
  • Full-year guide: ~$8.5 billion
  • First-ever $2B quarter

What happened

Palantir, which sells data and AI software to governments and companies, reported third-quarter revenue of $2.31 billion — almost twice what it made a year ago. It now expects about $8.5 billion in revenue for 2026.

Growth was led by US companies buying its AI platform.

Why it matters

Palantir has become one of the clearest examples of a company actually making money from AI, not just spending on it. But its shares are priced for a lot of future growth, so even strong results can be met with nervous trading — especially in a week when AI stocks were falling.

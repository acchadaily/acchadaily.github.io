Defence startup Shield AI buys Echodyne for $5.3 billion

The AI drone-pilot maker is buying a radar company and raising $2.5 billion at a $28.8 billion valuation.

accha? daily · 6 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$5.3Bdeal for radar maker Echodyne
  • Deal value: ~$5.3B
  • New funding: $2.5B
  • Valuation: $28.8B

What happened

Shield AI, which builds AI software that flies drones and aircraft, agreed to buy radar maker Echodyne for about $5.3 billion. To pay for it, Shield AI is raising $2.5 billion, including a $1 billion round at a $28.8 billion valuation, $1 billion from Blackstone-managed funds and $500 million of debt.

Echodyne sells radars in more than 40 countries. The deal is expected to close in early 2027.

Why it matters

Defence tech is one of the hottest areas for startup investors right now, driven by drones in modern warfare. Shield AI wants to own both the 'eyes' (radar) and the 'brain' (AI pilot) of autonomous systems.

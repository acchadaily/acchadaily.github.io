Tamil Nadu gives male government employees 15 days of paternity leave

Chief Minister Vijay announced the change on 26 November, and an order has been issued. Women staff already get a year of maternity leave.

accha? daily · 27 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
15 dayspaternity leave for male state staff
  • Leave window: 15 days before due date or within 6 months of birth
  • Women staff in TN: 365 days of maternity leave
  • Central staff have had 15 days since 1997

The new rule

Male employees of the Tamil Nadu government can now take 15 days of paternity leave, Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay announced on 26 November. The leave can be taken in the 15 days before the baby is due or within six months of the birth.

The government said a father's presence is important to support the mother and care for the newborn, and that the leave will help couples share family duties.

Context

Women in Tamil Nadu government jobs already get 365 days of maternity leave, and male staff had asked for leave for fathers too. Central government employees have had 15 days of paternity leave since 1997. Employee groups welcomed the decision.

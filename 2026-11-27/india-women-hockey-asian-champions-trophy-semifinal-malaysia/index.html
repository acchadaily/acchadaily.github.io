Unbeaten India women face Malaysia in the Asian Champions Trophy semi-final tonight; four Indian women reach Syed Modi quarter-finals

India have scored 23 goals and let in just 4 in Bhopal. In Lucknow, Tasnim Mir knocked out the sixth seed.

In short
23goals scored by India in 5 league games
  • Semi-final: India vs Malaysia, 7 pm IST
  • Deepika: 8 goals
  • 4 Indian women in Syed Modi singles quarters

Hockey

India's women won all five league games at the Asian Champions Trophy in Bhopal, scoring 23 goals and conceding 4. They play Malaysia in the semi-final on Friday 27 November at 7 pm IST, after beating them 4-1 in the group stage.

Deepika leads the scoring with 8 goals. India are chasing a record fourth title, after 2016, 2023 and 2024. Japan play China in the other semi-final.

Badminton

At the Syed Modi India International in Lucknow, four Indian women reached the singles quarter-finals: Tanvi Sharma, Unnati Hooda, Tasnim Mir and Anmol Kharb. Tasnim Mir beat sixth seed Kirsty Gilmour 21-19, 21-15. Top seeds Dhruv Kapila and Tanisha Crasto lost in mixed doubles.

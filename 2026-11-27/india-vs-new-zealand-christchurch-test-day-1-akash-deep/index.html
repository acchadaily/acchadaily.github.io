Christchurch Test: India strike 3 times without a run as New Zealand slip from 110/1 to 110/4 on Day 1

Debutant Gurnoor Brar took a wicket on the last ball before lunch, and Akash Deep removed Latham and Ravindra.

accha? daily · 27 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
0 runsfor 3 New Zealand wickets after lunch
  • Venue: Hagley Oval, Christchurch
  • Lunch: NZ 77/1 in 26 overs
  • Latham 59 off 128 balls

The morning

India captain Shubman Gill won the toss and chose to bowl on a green pitch at Hagley Oval in the second and final Test, which began at 3:30 am IST on Friday. Fast bowler Gurnoor Brar made his Test debut in place of Prasidh Krishna.

New Zealand reached 77/1 at lunch. Brar trapped Devon Conway lbw for 31 on the last ball of the session for his first Test wicket. Earlier, KL Rahul dropped Tom Latham at slip.

The collapse

After lunch, Akash Deep had Latham caught behind by Rishabh Pant for 59. Anshul Kamboj then removed Henry Nicholls for 16, and Akash Deep got Rachin Ravindra for a duck. New Zealand went from 110/1 to 110/4 in the second session, which was still going on at the time of writing. The first Test in Wellington was drawn, so this match decides the series.

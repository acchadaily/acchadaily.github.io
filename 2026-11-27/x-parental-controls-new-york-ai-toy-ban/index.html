X adds its first parental controls for teens, and New York bans AI chatbot toys for 5 years

Parents can now set screen-time limits on X for 13–17-year-olds. New York is the second US state to ban AI-powered toys.

X's new controls

X launched parental controls for teens aged 13–17 on 26 November, Engadget reported. Teen accounts are set to a protected mode that limits sensitive media and blocks targeted ads.

Parents can set daily time limits, approve new followers, filter words and choose who can message their teen. They cannot read messages or see search history, and both parent and teen must agree to link accounts.

New York's toy ban

New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed a law on 24 November banning the making and sale of toys powered by AI chatbots for five years, NBC News reported. It takes effect in February. New York is the second state after California to pass such a ban.

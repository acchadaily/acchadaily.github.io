Government reminds Netflix not to show Pakistani content in India, days before its first Pakistani original series

Film workers' groups have asked the PM to stop 'Jo Bachay Hain Sang Samait Lo', which premieres globally on 4 December.

accha? daily · 27 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp

What happened

The I&B Ministry reportedly told Netflix on 26 November to make sure Pakistan-made content is not available in India, repeating an advisory from 8 May 2025. The trigger is Netflix's first Pakistani original, 'Jo Bachay Hain Sang Samait Lo', which premieres on 4 December.

The All Indian Cine Workers Association wrote to PM Modi on 24 November seeking a permanent law, and the Federation of Western India Cine Employees (FWICE) also wrote, saying national interest should come before business. Netflix had not commented.

Also on Netflix

Netflix separately announced a documentary on conman Sukesh Chandrashekhar, premiering on 18 December.

A rubber glove filled with warm water saves an endangered peacock-pheasant chick at Chester Zoo

Only 15 Malay peacock-pheasants live in European zoos. The chick's mother took it back the next morning.

accha? daily · 27 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
15Malay peacock-pheasants in European zoos
  • Two chicks hatched
  • First egg raised by a related pheasant species
  • Wild home: Malaysia's lowland rainforests

The rescue

Chester Zoo in England hatched two chicks of the endangered Malay peacock-pheasant, Good Good Good reported. The mother left the second chick soon after it hatched, and it got cold.

Lead keeper Leanne Lowton filled a rubber glove with warm water to keep it warm overnight. The next morning, the mother took the chick back and has raised it well since. "We didn't want this chick to grow up not knowing how to be a pheasant," Lowton said.

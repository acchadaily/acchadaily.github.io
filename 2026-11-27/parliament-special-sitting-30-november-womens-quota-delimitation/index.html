Parliament called back for a special sitting from 30 November, with the bill for 850 Lok Sabha seats and women's quota expected

The Lok Sabha meets on Monday and the Rajya Sabha on 2 December. A similar constitutional amendment failed in April, short of a two-thirds majority.

accha? daily · 27 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
850proposed maximum Lok Sabha seats, up from 543
  • Lok Sabha: 30 Nov; Rajya Sabha: 2 Dec; may end 3 Dec
  • April vote: 298 for, 230 against (two-thirds needed)
  • Two-thirds of a full 543-member House: about 361

What's happening

Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju said on 26 November that the government had asked for both Houses to meet again. The Lok Sabha will sit from Monday 30 November and the Rajya Sabha from Wednesday 2 December, and the sitting may end on 3 December. He said it was for "essential government business".

The Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026 is expected to come up. It would raise the number of Lok Sabha seats from 543 to as many as 850, redraw constituencies across the country, and bring in 33% reservation for women in Parliament and state assemblies before the 2029 election.

The numbers

An earlier version was defeated on 17 April 2026: 298 MPs voted for it and 230 against, short of the two-thirds needed. In a full House of 543, two-thirds is about 361 votes. Reports say the government may bring changes to the bill.

What both sides say

Congress's Jairam Ramesh called the move an "assault on the Constitution". Home Minister Amit Shah said Congress could not block reservation for women.

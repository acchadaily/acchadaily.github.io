A Bengaluru startup turns waste fabric into reusable birthday decorations, used at 64 parties so far

Matter Party rents out bunting and banners stitched from scrap cloth, then takes them back to use again.

accha? daily · 27 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
100+ kgtextile waste upcycled
  • Started March 2025
  • 64 parties, ~35 themes
  • Kits: ₹1,750 and ₹3,500

The idea

Matter Party, started by Bhavna Kanoria in Bengaluru in March 2025, buys sorted waste fabric from the recycling group Hasiru Dala. Local tailors stitch it into bunting, fringes, wreaths and 'Happy Birthday' banners, The Better India reported.

Families rent a kit, and it comes back after the party to be cleaned and reused. A mini kit costs ₹1,750 and a party kit ₹3,500. Leftover scraps go back to the recycler.

So far

It has upcycled more than 100 kg of fabric and decorated 64 parties across about 35 themes, replacing single-use plastic decorations.

Funding round-up: Finnable raises ₹405 crore, jet-engine startup Green Aero gets $14 million, and GIVA ₹90 crore

Lender Finnable is profitable, Green Aero builds engines for fast drones, and jewellery brand GIVA is opening more stores.

accha? daily · 27 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹405 croreFinnable's Series C
  • Finnable: ₹405 crore Series C, AUM over ₹4,600 crore
  • Green Aero: $14 million Series A, led by Crane Ventures
  • GIVA: ₹90 crore debt from BlackSoil

The deals

Finnable, a Bengaluru lender to salaried people, raised ₹405 crore (about $43.8 million) in a Series C led by Bharat Value Fund with ₹150 crore. Its loan book is above ₹4,600 crore, and it made ₹51 crore profit in April–September, more than the ₹46 crore it made in all of FY26.

Green Aero, a Bengaluru startup founded in 2023, raised a $14 million Series A led by Crane Ventures, with 3one4 Capital, pi Ventures and Antler. It builds jet engines for high-speed drones and combat aircraft and has done more than 100 ground tests.

Also

Jewellery brand GIVA raised ₹90 crore in debt from BlackSoil to open more stores, and customer-experience platform XEBO.ai raised $10.5 million. Separately, Norwest sold a 2.15% stake in recently listed Amagi for ₹262.44 crore.

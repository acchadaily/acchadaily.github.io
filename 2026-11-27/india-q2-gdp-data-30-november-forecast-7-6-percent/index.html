Economy

India's July–September GDP data comes out on Monday, and economists expect growth of about 7.6%

Forecasts range from 7.2% to 7.8%. Growth was 7.8% in April–June, but a weak monsoon has hit farming.

What to expect

The statistics ministry will release GDP figures for the July–September quarter (Q2 of FY27) on Monday 30 November. A Reuters poll of 43 economists expects growth of 7.6%, Business Today reported.

SBI Research expects about 7.8%, ICRA 7.5% and CareEdge 7.6%. The RBI's own estimate is 7.2%, and the finance ministry's monthly review puts it at 7.35%.

What's holding it back

The economy grew 7.8% in April–June. A year ago, in July–September 2025, it grew 8.2%, which makes this year's comparison harder.

CareEdge expects farm output to grow just 0.8%, against 3.6% a year earlier, because of the weak monsoon. ICRA sees manufacturing growth slowing to 8.2% from 9.2%.

What is a front-of-pack warning label?

It is a simple symbol printed on the front of a food packet, next to the brand name, that tells you at a glance if the food has a lot of fat, sugar or salt. Nutrition tables on the back are small and hard to read, so many countries now use bold front labels instead. India's Supreme Court wants a separate hexagon for each problem nutrient, so a snack high in both salt and fat would carry two warnings.

What is the repo rate, and what is a basis point?

The repo rate is the interest rate at which the Reserve Bank of India lends money to banks for short periods. When it goes up, borrowing becomes costlier for banks, and they usually pass that on by raising loan rates, which pushes up EMIs. A basis point is one-hundredth of a percentage point, so a 25 basis-point hike means a rise of 0.25%, for example from 5.50% to 5.75%.

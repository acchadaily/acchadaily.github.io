SAP weighs cutting at least 10,000 jobs, about 9% of staff, as it reshapes itself around AI

The plan is not final. Engineering and sales are the main focus, Bloomberg reported.

accha? daily · 27 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
10,000+jobs that could go
  • Staff at end-2025: 111,397
  • 2024 restructuring cost about €3.1 billion
  • SAP usually announces restructuring with results in late January

The plan

German software giant SAP is preparing to cut at least 10,000 positions, or at least 9% of its global staff, Bloomberg reported on 26 November. Every department was asked to find possible cuts, with product, engineering and sales in focus.

SAP did not confirm the number. It said it "continuously reviews its organization" to stay in line with its strategy. A deal with German workers protects jobs there until 2028, so most cuts may fall elsewhere.

Context

SAP had 111,397 employees at the end of 2025. Its last big restructuring, in 2024, covered 9,000–10,000 roles. SAP Labs India is one of its largest hubs, but no India numbers have been reported.

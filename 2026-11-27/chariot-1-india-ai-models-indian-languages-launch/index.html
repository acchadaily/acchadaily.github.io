India launches Chariot-1, AI models built from scratch for Indian languages under the IndiaAI Mission

The release includes a language model and a text-to-speech model covering English and 10 Indian languages.

accha? daily · 27 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
10Indian languages in the voice model
  • Launched: 26 November, New Delhi
  • LLM: reported 11 billion parameters
  • Tokeniser: up to 2.5x fewer tokens on Indian text (developer claim)

What launched

IT Secretary S. Krishnan launched the Chariot-1 models in New Delhi on 26 November. They were built by Chariot Artificial Intelligence, one of the first four teams picked under the IndiaAI Mission to build Indian foundation models.

There is a language model, reported at 11 billion parameters, and a text-to-speech model for English plus Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi and Odia. Both were trained from scratch in India.

The claims

Its open-source tokeniser, IndicShift, maps 1,012 characters across 12 Indian scripts to 350 letters. The developers say it uses up to 2.5 times fewer tokens than OpenAI's on Indian text, which makes it cheaper to run. All performance figures come from the developers and still need independent tests.

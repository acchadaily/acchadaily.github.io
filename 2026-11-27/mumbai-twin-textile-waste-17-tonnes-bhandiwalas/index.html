Mumbai startup TWIN has collected 17.5 tonnes of old clothes, giving rag collectors steady work

Free doorstep pickups from 650+ homes now supply 15–20 informal recyclers with sorted fabric.

accha? daily · 27 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
17,500 kgold clothes collected
  • 650+ households
  • 15–20 informal recyclers supported
  • WhatsApp bot got 300+ sign-ups in its first month

How it works

TWIN (Textile Waste Innovation Network), started by 29-year-old Vikram Shahi, collects unwanted clothes from homes across Mumbai for free, booked online or on WhatsApp, The Better India reported. It has collected about 17,500 kg from more than 650 households.

Wearable clothes go to second-hand markets and NGOs. Torn fabric becomes bags, mats and cleaning cloths.

Who it helps

TWIN works with 15–20 'bhandiwala' recyclers, mostly from the Waghari community. One of them, Anil Datanini, now earns ₹25,000–30,000 a month. "Now the material comes to us regularly," he said.

Urban Company starts selling its own air conditioners, with pre-bookings from ₹55,499 and a 5-year warranty that covers gas refills

The home-services company says it designed the AC around problems seen in 1.8 crore AC service jobs. Its shares rose 3.8%.

The launch

Urban Company has opened pre-bookings for two air conditioners, the Native AC Ultra and Ultra Air, sold only on its app, Inc42 reported on 26 November. Prices start at ₹55,499.

It claims the ACs cool a room to 24°C in 21 minutes and keep cooling fully at 47°C outside. Some models have a HEPA H13 air purifier. The warranty is 5 years unconditional, including gas refills, and 10 years on the compressor.

The pitch

Co-founder Varun Khaitan said the company wants to fix common AC problems "at the source": gas leaks, poor after-sales service and weak cooling on very hot days. It has already sold water purifiers under its Native brand.

The shares closed 3.77% higher at ₹170.80 on the BSE. In July–September, its net loss more than tripled to ₹182.5 crore, while revenue rose 41.4% to ₹537.3 crore.

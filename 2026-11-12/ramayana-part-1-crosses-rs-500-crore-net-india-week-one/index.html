Ramayana: Part 1 crosses ₹500 crore net in India in its first week; worldwide gross near ₹935 crore

Trackers estimate ₹70 crore-plus net on its first Wednesday, as Nitesh Tiwari's epic keeps up a strong run.

accha? daily · 12 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹511 CrIndia net in 7 days (Sacnilk)
  • India net, 7 days: ~₹511 crore
  • Day 7: ~₹70.65 crore net
  • Worldwide: ~₹934.55 crore gross

The numbers

Sacnilk estimates Ramayana: Part 1, starring Ranbir Kapoor and Yash, collected about ₹70.65 crore net on day seven (11 November), taking its India total to ₹511.10 crore net (₹611 crore gross). With about ₹323 crore from overseas, its worldwide gross is around ₹934.55 crore. Other trackers have slightly different figures, so treat these as estimates.

Records

Sacnilk says it is the first Hindi film to cross ₹100 crore net from its dubbed South Indian versions. It also posted the highest first-Monday gross for an Indian film in North America, at $1.37 million.

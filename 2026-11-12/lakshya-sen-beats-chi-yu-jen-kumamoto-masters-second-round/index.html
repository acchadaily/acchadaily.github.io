Lakshya Sen beats Chi Yu Jen 21-19, 21-18 to reach the Kumamoto Masters second round

The eighth seed came through a tight opener in Japan as he chases a place at the World Tour Finals.

accha? daily · 12 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp

21-19, 21-18Lakshya's first-round win
  • Score: 21-19, 21-18
  • Rankings: No. 13 vs No. 24
  • Event: Super 500, 10–15 Nov

The match

Lakshya Sen, the eighth seed and world No. 13, beat world No. 24 Chi Yu Jen of Chinese Taipei 21-19, 21-18 on 11 November at the Kumamoto Masters Japan, a Super 500 event running until 15 November. Sen had lost to Chi at last year's Malaysia Open before beating him at the Australian Open.

Other Indians

Sen recently won the Hylo Open in Germany and is chasing a spot at the BWF World Tour Finals. In mixed doubles, Dhruv Rawat and K. Maneesha lost in three games to the fourth seeds, while Gayatri Gopichand and Treesa Jolly reached the women's doubles pre-quarter-finals.

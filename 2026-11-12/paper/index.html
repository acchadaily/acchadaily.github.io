Amul and Mother Dairy raise milk prices by up to ₹4 a litre from today; Mother Dairy full cream now ₹76

India's two biggest milk brands have both raised prices from Thursday, 12 November, citing higher costs for fodder, feed, fuel and packaging.

Amul, sold by the Gujarat Cooperative Milk Marketing Federation (GCMMF), is raising fresh pouch milk prices across India from today. Amul Taaza (toned milk) goes up by ₹2 a litre and Amul Gold by ₹4 a litre, about 5.5%. Managing director Jayen Mehta blamed a steep rise in the cost of producing and processing milk.

In Delhi-NCR, Mother Dairy has made a similar move. Full cream milk in pouches now costs ₹76 a litre instead of ₹72. Toned milk goes from ₹60 to ₹62, bulk-vended toned milk from ₹58 to ₹60, double toned from ₹54 to ₹56, and cow milk from ₹62 to ₹65.

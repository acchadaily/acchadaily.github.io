Two fishermen pull a cruise ship pianist from the Pacific after about nine hours treading water

The 37-year-old went overboard in the night; the fishing boat spotted him about 20 miles off California around noon.

accha? daily · 12 November 2026 · 1 min read

In short
9 hrsin the water before rescue
  • Time in water: ~9 hours
  • Distance offshore: ~15–20 miles
  • Water temperature: 76°F

What happened

A 37-year-old pianist on Royal Caribbean's Ovation of the Seas, sailing from Long Beach to Ensenada, is believed to have gone overboard at about 2:30 am on 6 November. Around noon, Sean Norton and Grant Garrett, returning from a fishing trip, saw him roughly 15–20 miles offshore.

The outcome

They called for help, and Newport Beach lifeguards took him to hospital. The Coast Guard said he was expected to survive. Norton credited the warm 76°F water.

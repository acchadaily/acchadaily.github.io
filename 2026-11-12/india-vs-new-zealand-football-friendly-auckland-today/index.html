India's footballers take on New Zealand in Auckland today, only the third meeting between the sides

Khalid Jamil's team, ranked 138th, play the first of two friendlies; kick-off is around 11:30 am IST.

accha? daily · 12 November 2026 · 1 min read

  • India's ranking: 138

  • Second match: 15 Nov, Christchurch

The game

India face New Zealand's All Whites at Go Media Stadium, Auckland, on 12 November, with kick-off at 7 pm local time, about 11:30 am IST. A second friendly follows in Christchurch on 15 November at 8:30 am IST. The teams drew 0-0 in 1981 and New Zealand won 2-1 in 2018.

The squads

India arrive after defeats to Brazil and Uruguay. Coach Khalid Jamil named a 23-man squad and left out goalkeeper Gurpreet Singh Sandhu. For New Zealand, the series is coach Darren Bazeley's farewell.

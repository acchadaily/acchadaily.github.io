Stand-Up India extended to 2031 with a ₹4,111 crore guarantee fund to reach 2.5 lakh more entrepreneurs

The scheme backs bank loans for women, SC and ST entrepreneurs; the new fund guarantees 85% of each loan.

accha? daily · 12 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
2.5 lakhmore entrepreneurs targeted by 2031
  • Guarantee fund: ₹4,111 crore
  • Loan cover: 85%
  • Beneficiaries so far: 2.75 lakh+

The decision

The Union Cabinet on 11 November extended Stand-Up India by five years, to 31 March 2031, and approved a ₹4,111 crore credit guarantee corpus. Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said the guarantee covers 85% of the loan at a standard fee of 0.5%, with no extra risk premium.

Why it matters

Launched in April 2016, the scheme has helped more than 2.75 lakh people from women, SC and ST communities get bank loans to start businesses. A guarantee makes banks more willing to lend to first-time founders without collateral, which is the main hurdle for small entrepreneurs.

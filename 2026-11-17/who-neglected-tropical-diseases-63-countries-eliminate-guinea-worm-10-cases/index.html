Good News

63 countries have now eliminated at least one neglected tropical disease, and Guinea worm fell to a record-low 10 human cases

The number of people needing treatment for these diseases is down 36% since 2010, the WHO says.

The progress

The World Health Organization says 1.4 billion people needed treatment for neglected tropical diseases in 2024, down 36% since 2010, Positive News reports. Sleeping sickness cases have fallen 98% since 1999, and Guinea worm disease was down to just 10 human cases in 2025.

By June 2026, 63 countries had wiped out at least one of these diseases, up from 42 at the end of 2020. India was declared free of Guinea worm back in 2000.

Why it matters

These diseases mostly hit the world's poorest people, causing blindness, disability and lost income. Cheap medicines, clean water and community health workers are steadily pushing them back.

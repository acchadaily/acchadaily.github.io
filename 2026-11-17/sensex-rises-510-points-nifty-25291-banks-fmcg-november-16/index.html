Sensex jumps 510 points to 82,103 and Nifty ends at 25,291 as banks and FMCG shares lead; small caps fall 1.1%

Tata Motors PV was the biggest drag after its results, while broader markets stayed weak.

The day

The BSE Sensex rose 510.40 points, or 0.63%, to 82,102.85 on Monday, and the Nifty 50 gained 146.8 points, or 0.58%, to 25,291.10, Business Standard reports. Banking and FMCG shares did the heavy lifting, while auto, realty and pharma lagged.

The rally was narrow: the Nifty Midcap 100 lost 0.51% and the Smallcap 100 fell 1.10%.

What to watch

Investors are tracking Anthropic's US listing on Thursday, oil prices amid Gulf tensions and this week's IPOs, including Mahanadi Coalfields. Not investment advice.

Bombay Shaving Company's parent launches 'rE', a recovery brand selling massage guns and foam rollers from ₹349 to ₹9,999

It is aimed at desk workers and plans gym recovery corners and marathon recovery zones.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
14products at launch
  • Launch: 16 Nov
  • Price range: ₹349–₹9,999
  • Sub-brand: Republic

The launch

Visage Lines, the company behind Bombay Shaving Company, has moved into wellness with rE, Inc42 reports. The website went live on 16 November with 14 products, including massage guns, scalp massagers, foam rollers and hot and cold packs, priced from ₹349 for an eye mask to ₹9,999 for a foot massager.

The brand was co-founded by BSC's CMO Varun Gupta. Founder Shantanu Deshpande pitched it as a doctor-designed 'ten-minute reset' people can make a daily habit.

The plan

A sub-brand called Republic will build a network of gyms and fitness partners. rE also wants recovery zones at marathons and HYROX events and office wellness tie-ups. It faces premium players like Therabody and Hyperice, and cheaper Indian ones like Cult and AGARO.

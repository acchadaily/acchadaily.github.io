Sony brings back the headphones from its 1979 Walkman as the MDR-3R, now with USB-C, at $59.99

A Stranger Things edition with red and black ear pads costs $79.99. India pricing hasn't been announced.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$59.99US price of the MDR-3R
  • Original: 1979 Walkman
  • Stranger Things edition: $79.99
  • Connection: USB-C, wired

The product

Sony has revived the on-ear headphones that shipped with the original 1979 Walkman, afaqs reports. The MDR-3R keeps the slim metal headband and the famous orange-and-blue ear pads, but plugs into phones and laptops via USB-C. There's no Bluetooth or battery; it has an in-line mic and remote for calls and music.

The nostalgia play

A $79.99 Stranger Things edition adds red and black pads and an engraved logo, inspired by the character Max Mayfield. Sony hasn't said when or at what price it will come to India. It's a clear bet that retro design sells, especially to younger buyers who never owned a Walkman.

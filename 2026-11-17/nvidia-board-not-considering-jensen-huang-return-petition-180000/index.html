Nvidia's board is not considering bringing back Jensen Huang, CNBC reports, even as a petition for his return nears 180,000 signatures

The board is focused on a law-firm investigation into Huang's conduct; Trump says he is open to Huang returning.

What's new

Nvidia's board is not in talks to reinstate Jensen Huang, a person familiar with the matter told CNBC on Monday. Directors are concentrating on an investigation by law firm Wachtell Lipton into whether his conduct put national security or company secrets at risk.

Huang resigned last week after 33 years as CEO, days after acknowledging an affair. The board said on Friday it was choosing a search firm to find a new CEO.

The pressure

An online petition to bring him back has about 180,000 signatures, with backers including Elon Musk, Y Combinator's Garry Tan and Epic Games' Tim Sweeney. President Trump said on Monday he is open to Huang returning. OpenAI's Sam Altman, meanwhile, told TechCrunch the AI industry 'will be fine' without him.

17-year-old Taniya wins beach wrestling silver for India at the Youth Olympics in Dakar after learning the sport's rules from YouTube

She lost the 75kg final 2-1 to China's Zhao Min.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read

Silverin women's 75kg beach wrestling
  • Age: 17
  • Event: 75kg beach wrestling
  • Venue: Dakar, Senegal

The medal

Taniya, 17, took silver in the women's 75kg beach wrestling event at the Youth Olympic Games in Dakar, Senegal, losing the final 2-1 to China's Zhao Min, The Bridge reports.

Beach wrestling was new to her. She picked up the rules by watching videos on YouTube and from a guide the Wrestling Federation of India sent the team.

Why it's special

Beach wrestling is barely practised in India, so reaching a final at a global youth event shows how quickly Indian wrestlers can adapt. It adds to India's strong showing in Dakar, which includes boxing golds.

A Mumbai museum has taught more than 9,000 children about AI through games, 54% of them girls

The Museum of Solutions' AI Explorers programme works with government schools across six Maharashtra districts.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
9,087children in AI workshops
  • Teachers trained: 80
  • Schools: 36
  • Girls: 54%

What it does

The Museum of Solutions (MuSo) in Lower Parel runs AI Explorers with Microsoft Philanthropies India and the JSW Foundation, The Better India reports. Between July 2025 and June 2026, 9,087 children in Classes 5 to 10 attended hands-on workshops on AI, coding and staying safe online, and 80 teachers were trained.

It worked with 36 government and aided schools and 17 NGOs. Of the students it has records for, 54% were girls.

Why it matters

Nearly one in four schools in Maharashtra has no working computer. In the workshops, 81% of students tested showed more interest in AI afterwards, a head start as AI lessons begin from Class 3 next academic year.

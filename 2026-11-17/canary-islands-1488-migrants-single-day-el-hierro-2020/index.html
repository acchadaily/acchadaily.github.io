Nearly 1,500 migrants reach Spain's Canary Islands in a single day, the most since 2020

Most of the 14 boats landed on the small island of El Hierro, days after Spain declared a migration emergency there.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
1,488people rescued in one day
  • Boats: 14
  • Main landing point: El Hierro
  • Status: migration emergency declared

What happened

Spain's coastguard rescued 1,488 people from 14 boats on Saturday, 14 November, the highest one-day total for the Canary Islands since 2020, Reuters and EFE report. Most arrived on El Hierro, the smallest of the main islands.

Spain had declared a migration emergency on the islands days earlier after a rise in arrivals.

The pressure

Arrivals are lower so far this year than last year overall, but the recent surge has stretched reception centres and emergency services. Canary Islands President Fernando Clavijo has asked Madrid to deliver the help it promised straight away.

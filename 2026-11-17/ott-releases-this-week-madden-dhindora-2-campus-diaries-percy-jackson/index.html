OTT this week: Nicolas Cage's Madden and Campus Diaries 2 on Prime Video (18 Nov), Dhindora 2 on Netflix and Percy Jackson S3 on JioHotstar (20 Nov)

Your watchlist for the week, from a David O. Russell biopic to BB Ki Vines' comedy sequel.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
4big releases this week
  • Madden: Prime Video, 18 Nov
  • Dhindora 2: Netflix, 20 Nov
  • Percy Jackson S3: JioHotstar, 20 Nov

What's coming

Prime Video has two on 18 November: Madden, David O. Russell's biopic of American football coach and commentator John Madden, starring Nicolas Cage, and Campus Diaries Season 2, which also streams on Amazon MX Player.

On 20 November, Dhindora 2 lands on Netflix with eight episodes, and Percy Jackson and the Olympians Season 3 arrives on JioHotstar, India TV reports.

Also streaming

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has been on Prime Video since 12 November in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada and Hindi. Release dates can shift, so check the app before you plan a watch party.

Wholesale inflation climbs to 12.29% in October, the highest on the new series, as fuel and power prices jump 32.84%

Fuel and power drove almost all of the rise. Food inflation at the wholesale level eased slightly to 8.58%.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
12.29%WPI inflation in October
  • September WPI: 11.39%
  • Fuel & power: 32.84%
  • April–October average: 10.14%

The numbers

India's wholesale price index rose 12.29% from a year earlier in October, up from 11.39% in September, government data released on Monday shows. It is the highest reading since the index moved to its new 2022-23 base year, and above ICRA's forecast of 11.8%.

Fuel and power inflation shot up to 32.84% from 25.85%. ICRA estimates it explains 83 of the 90 basis-point increase. Manufactured goods rose 9.54% and primary articles 9.24%, while WPI food inflation dipped to 8.58% from 8.66%.

What it means for you

Wholesale prices feed into what shops and companies charge later, so a jump like this can keep retail prices high in the coming months. Economists think October may be the peak, but oil prices remain the big risk. November's data is due on 14 December.

Glenn Close finally gets an Oscar after eight nominations, receiving an honorary award alongside Ridley Scott

Disney's first Black animator, Floyd Norman, was also honoured at the Governors Awards in Hollywood.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read

8nominations without a win
  • Date: 15 November
  • Other honorees: Ridley Scott, Floyd Norman
  • Thalberg Award: Vachon & Koffler

The night

Glenn Close received an honorary Oscar at the Academy's Governors Awards in Hollywood on Sunday, 15 November, after eight competitive nominations without a win. Director Ridley Scott and animator Floyd Norman, who became Disney's first Black animator in 1956, also received honorary Oscars, Anadolu reports.

Killer Films founders Christine Vachon and Pamela Koffler got the Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award.

Why it matters

Close is one of Hollywood's most-nominated actors never to win, so the award was widely seen as overdue. The Governors Awards also mark the unofficial start of the Oscar campaign season.

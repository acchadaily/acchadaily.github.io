Urban Company opens a ₹2,500 crore share sale to big investors at a floor price of ₹161.03; ₹600 crore is for its InstaHelp maids service

The home-services firm wants money for acquisitions in India, the UAE or Singapore, its 15-minute help service and marketing.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹2,500 Crqualified institutional placement
  • Q2 revenue: ₹537 crore (+41%)
  • Q2 net loss: ₹182 crore
  • Cash on hand: ₹1,901 crore

The deal

Listed home-services company Urban Company opened a qualified institutional placement (QIP) of up to ₹2,500 crore on 16 November, Entrackr reports. The floor price is ₹161.03 a share, and the company can offer up to a 5% discount on that.

Planned uses: ₹1,025 crore for acquisitions and general purposes, ₹600 crore for InstaHelp's network of workers, ₹540 crore for marketing, ₹200 crore for technology, ₹85 crore for training centres and ₹50 crore for research on its Native products.

The numbers behind it

In the September quarter, revenue from operations grew 41.4% to ₹537.29 crore, but the net loss widened to ₹182.48 crore from ₹59.33 crore, mostly because of spending on InstaHelp. It had ₹1,901 crore in cash and investments at the end of September.

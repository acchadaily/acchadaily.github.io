ULA's Vulcan rocket fails about 10 minutes after launch, losing 40 Amazon Leo internet satellites

It is ULA's first full launch failure. Amazon says its plan to start commercial service in 2026 is unchanged.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
40Amazon Leo satellites lost
  • Launch date: 15 Nov
  • Vulcan launches booked by Amazon: 38
  • Cause: under investigation

What happened

United Launch Alliance's Vulcan rocket lifted off from Florida on 15 November carrying 40 satellites for Amazon Leo, Amazon's rival to SpaceX's Starlink. ULA said a problem about ten minutes into flight meant the satellites never reached orbit, Via Satellite reports. It is ULA's first complete launch failure, and the cause hasn't been explained.

What it means for Amazon

Amazon has booked 38 Vulcan launches to build its network, so the rocket's reliability matters a lot. The company says it still has enough satellites in orbit to start commercial service in some regions in 2026.

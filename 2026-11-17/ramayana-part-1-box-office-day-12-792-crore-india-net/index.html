Ramayana: Part 1 adds ₹32 crore on its second Monday, taking its India net total to about ₹792.6 crore in 12 days

The epic is closing in on the ₹800 crore mark in India, according to trade tracker Sacnilk.

The numbers

Ramayana: Part 1 collected about ₹32 crore net in India on Monday, its 12th day, according to Sacnilk. That's 57% below Sunday's ₹75 crore, a normal weekday drop, and takes the total to roughly ₹792.6 crore. The Hindi version made up about 74% of Monday's business.

These are trade estimates, not official figures from the makers.

What's next

The film should cross ₹800 crore in India within days. Its run has also been a boost for cinema advertising, as brands rush to buy screen time in packed halls.

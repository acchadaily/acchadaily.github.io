Cyclone Akvan to become a severe storm today; landfall expected near noon on 20 November between Nellore and Tuni in Andhra Pradesh

Winds of 120–130 km/h, gusting to 145 km/h, are forecast. Odisha and Tamil Nadu are also bracing for heavy rain.

Where it's heading

Cyclone Akvan formed over the Bay of Bengal on 16 November. The India Meteorological Department expects it to become a severe cyclonic storm by 17 November and a very severe cyclonic storm around the night of 19 November, The Federal reports.

Landfall is expected around noon on Friday, 20 November, between Nellore and Tuni on the Andhra coast, including Yanam, with sustained winds of 120–130 km/h. A pre-cyclone watch is in place for Andhra Pradesh and the south Odisha coast.

Who is preparing

Fishermen have been told not to go to sea. Southern Odisha districts such as Ganjam are lining up early evacuations from low-lying and landslide-prone areas, with heavy to very heavy rain likely from 19 to 21 November. Chennai and nearby districts expect heavy rain from today.

Andhra is still recovering from Cyclones Arnab and Murasu, which together caused more than ₹7,000 crore in losses.

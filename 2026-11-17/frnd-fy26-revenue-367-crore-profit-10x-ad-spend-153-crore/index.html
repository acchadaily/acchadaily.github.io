Social audio app FRND's revenue jumps 88% to ₹367 crore in FY26 and profit grows about 10x to ₹19 crore

The app now has about 45 lakh monthly users who spend roughly 30 minutes a day on it.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
10xjump in net profit
  • Monthly users: ~45 lakh
  • Creator payouts: ₹136 crore
  • Ad spend: ₹153 crore

The numbers

FRND, a voice-based social app founded in 2019 by Bhanu Pratap Singh Tanwar and Harshvardhan Chhangani, earned ₹367 crore in FY26, up from ₹195 crore, Entrackr reports. Net profit rose to ₹19.27 crore from ₹2 crore, its third profitable year in a row.

Based on October revenue, it says it is now running at ₹600 crore a year. The Middle East, North Africa and the US made up 7.2% of revenue.

Where the money goes

Payouts to creators on the app rose 72% to ₹136 crore. Advertising spend climbed to ₹153 crore from ₹84 crore, and FRND says it spent about ₹5 crore on moderation and AI safety tools.

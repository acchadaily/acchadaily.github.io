The series

After winning the ODI series 3-1, India play two Tests in New Zealand: in Wellington from 19 November and in Christchurch from 27 November. Shubman Gill is captain and KL Rahul vice-captain, with Jasprit Bumrah recalled for the Tests.

On 14 November, the BCCI added fast bowler Gurnoor Brar to the squad and released spinner Kuldeep Yadav to play domestic cricket. Rishabh Pant has been declared fit after time at the BCCI Centre of Excellence.

Why it matters

India are fourth in the World Test Championship table and need wins to stay in the race for the final. Winning Tests in New Zealand's seaming conditions has historically been tough.

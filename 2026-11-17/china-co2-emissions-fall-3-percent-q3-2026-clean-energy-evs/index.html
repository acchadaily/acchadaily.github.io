China's carbon emissions fall 3.1% in the third quarter, the biggest drop since Covid, as EVs hit two-thirds of car sales

Clean power grew twice as fast as electricity demand, pushing coal and oil use down.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
67%of new cars sold in China in September were EVs
  • Coal use: −3%
  • Oil use: −4%
  • EV share of trucks: 50%

The numbers

China's CO2 emissions fell 3.1% between July and September compared with a year earlier, according to an analysis by Lauri Myllyvirta for Carbon Brief. Coal use dropped 3% and oil 4%, as solar, wind and nuclear added 110 TWh, more than double the growth in power demand.

Electric vehicles made up a record 67% of passenger car sales and 50% of heavy truck sales in September.

Why it matters

China is the world's biggest emitter, and its emissions have now been flat or falling since March 2024. Slower growth and mild weather helped, but the main driver is the speed of its clean-energy and EV rollout.

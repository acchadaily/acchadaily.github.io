Anthropic set to list on Nasdaq on Thursday at $70–78 a share; Altimeter's Brad Gerstner plans a $5 billion bet

The Claude maker is offering 825 million shares at a valuation of about $1.4 trillion, with chip and cloud giants investing $26 billion.

The details

Anthropic is expected to start trading on Nasdaq on Thursday, CNBC reports, making it the first big AI lab to go public. The price range is $70–78 a share for 825 million shares, valuing the company at about $1.4 trillion.

AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC are together putting in $26 billion at the IPO price.

The big bet

Altimeter Capital founder Brad Gerstner says his firm aims to invest $5 billion, five times his largest IPO investment before this. He called Anthropic a long-term 'cornerstone compounder'. OpenAI is aiming to list in 2027.

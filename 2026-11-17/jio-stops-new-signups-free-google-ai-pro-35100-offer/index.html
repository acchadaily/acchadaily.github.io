Jio stops new sign-ups for its free Google AI Pro offer, an 18-month bundle it valued at ₹35,100 per user

People who already activated it keep Gemini Pro and 2 TB of storage for up to 18 months, as long as they stay on an eligible plan.

accha? daily · 17 November 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹35,100value Reliance put on the free bundle
  • Google AI Pro price: ₹1,950/month
  • Free period: up to 18 months
  • Cloud storage: 2 TB

What happened

Reliance Jio is no longer taking new claims for its free Google AI Pro subscription. Its support page now says the offer can't be activated on new recharges or plan upgrades, Inc42 reports. The claim window ran from 30 October 2025 to 29 October 2026.

Jio hasn't explained the decision. Inc42 says it has asked the company for comment.

What existing users get

If you already switched it on, nothing changes for now. The subscription runs for up to 18 months from activation, as long as you keep recharging with an eligible Jio plan; otherwise Jio can pause access.

Google prices AI Pro at ₹1,950 a month, which is how Reliance arrived at ₹35,100 for 18 months. The bundle includes Gemini Pro access, higher limits for image and video generation, more NotebookLM features and 2 TB of cloud storage.

Who could claim it

At launch the offer was for users over 18 on unlimited 5G plans of ₹349 or more. From February 2026, Jio temporarily opened it to ₹299-plus plans with 1.5 GB of data a day.

Why it matters

The freebie was the consumer face of Reliance's wider AI tie-up with Google, which also covers Gemini for businesses and access to Google's AI chips. Ending new sign-ups means one of India's biggest free AI giveaways is over, at least for now.

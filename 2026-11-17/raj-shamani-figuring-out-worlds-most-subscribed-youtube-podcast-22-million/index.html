Raj Shamani's 'Figuring Out' becomes the world's most-subscribed podcast on YouTube with 22 million subscribers, passing Diary of a CEO and Joe Rogan

The Indore creator's show added 8 million subscribers this year alone and now gets about 1.4 billion views a month across platforms.

What happened

Figuring Out With Raj Shamani is now the most-subscribed podcast on YouTube in the world, Inc42 reports. The show has about 22 million subscribers, just ahead of Steven Bartlett's The Diary Of A CEO at 21.9 million and The Joe Rogan Experience at 21.2 million.

Shamani started the show from Indore in 2021. It has grown fast in 2026, adding around 8 million subscribers so far this year.

How big it has become

The podcast pulls in roughly 1.4 billion views a month across platforms, and about 22% of its YouTube subscribers live outside India. The team records 220 to 230 interviews a year and publishes about 150 of them.

Guests have ranged from French President Emmanuel Macron and OpenAI's Sam Altman to Indian founders such as Eternal's Deepinder Goyal, Zerodha's Kamath brothers and CRED's Kunal Shah, plus the founders of boAt, Lenskart, Nykaa and OYO.

A business, not just a show

Behind it sits Figuring Out Media & Education, which has 120+ employees working on content, distribution, investments and education. It has signed up global talent agency WME to chase international deals, and counts Levi's and ASUS among its brand partners.

Why it matters

An Indian, Hindi-English interview show beating the biggest American podcasts is a big signal for the creator economy here. It shows how large India's YouTube audience has become, and why brands are moving more of their money to long-form creators.

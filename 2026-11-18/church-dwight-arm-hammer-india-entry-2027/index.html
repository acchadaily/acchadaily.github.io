Arm & Hammer maker Church & Dwight plans an India launch by February 2027 with Hero acne patches, TheraBreath and Batiste

The US consumer goods company will sell online and on quick-commerce apps first, betting on young buyers who try new beauty products.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$39BIndia beauty market by FY30 (1Lattice)
  • Launch target: February 2027
  • At least 4 products at launch
  • International business is ~18% of its $4.6 billion sales (9 months to Sept)

What happened

Church & Dwight, the US maker of Arm & Hammer and Trojan, plans to launch at least four products in India by February 2027, including Hero acne patches, TheraBreath mouthwash and Batiste dry shampoo.

Mumbai's Sawariya Group will handle sales. It will start with e-commerce and quick-commerce apps, then move into supermarkets and big stores.

Why India

India's beauty and personal care market was worth about $27 billion in FY25 and could hit $39 billion by FY30, according to 1Lattice. Quick commerce is about 14% of online beauty sales and may reach 30% by 2030, and Gen Z already makes up around 45% of spending.

The company thinks quick delivery apps will help people try products like acne patches.

Right now the company has a five-person office in Navi Mumbai that mostly handles West Asia. It plans to hire for sales and marketing in India from next year.

Ranveer Singh and boAt's Aman Gupta launch H.I.M, a men's grooming brand with 8 products priced ₹299–₹899

H.I.M stands for 'Handsome Indian Man'. Ranveer is a co-founder, not just the face, and the brand says its range is made for Indian skin, weather and hard water.

What happened

Actor Ranveer Singh and boAt co-founder Aman Gupta launched H.I.M (Handsome Indian Man) on 17 November. It starts with eight products across face, body and hair: face wash, moisturiser, sunscreen, body wash, body lotion, a soap bar, a 2-in-1 shampoo and a scalp serum.

Prices run from ₹299 to ₹899. You can buy them on the brand's own website, Amazon and Blinkit.

The pitch

The founders say most grooming products sold to Indian men were made for other markets. H.I.M claims its formulas are built for heat, humidity, pollution and hard water, and every product uses an in-house ingredient it calls IN·XT, meant to help the skincare soak in better.

Ranveer said he worked with scientists from the first samples to the final products. Aman said Indian men now read labels and build routines, and pointed to an 850% jump in searches for men's skincare routines over five years.

Why it matters

Unlike most celebrity brands, Ranveer is a co-founder here, not a paid ambassador. The launch is part of Aman's venture studio OFF/BEAT, which raised ₹100 crore in April from Bessemer Venture Partners.

The market is growing fast: IMARC Group expects India's men's grooming market to grow from $2.62 billion in 2026 to $4.46 billion by 2034. H.I.M walks into a busy aisle that already has Beardo, The Man Company, Bombay Shaving Company and Tiger Shroff's Prowl.

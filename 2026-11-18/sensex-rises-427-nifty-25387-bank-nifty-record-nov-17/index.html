Sensex rises 427 points to 82,532 and Nifty ends at 25,387 as Bank Nifty closes at a record 62,273; rupee gains 43 paise

Indian shares rose for a second day on Tuesday, helped by banks, cheaper crude and foreign buying. IT shares fell after a global tech sell-off.

In short
+0.52%Sensex on Tuesday
  • Sensex 82,532.19 (+427.40); Nifty 25,387.40 (+96.30)
  • Bank Nifty record close of 62,273.25 (+1.13%)
  • Rupee closes at 93.51 per dollar, up 43 paise

The numbers

The Sensex closed 427.40 points (0.52%) higher at 82,532.19 on Tuesday, 17 November. The Nifty 50 rose 96.30 points (0.38%) to 25,387.40, after touching about 25,500 during the day.

Bank Nifty jumped 1.13% to 62,273.25, a new all-time high, with PSU banks up 1.95%. The rupee rose 43 paise to 93.51 against the dollar (provisional).

Winners and losers

Adani Enterprises (+3.2%), Eicher Motors (+3%) and Axis Bank (+2%) were among the top Nifty gainers. HCL Tech fell 2.1% as IT stocks slipped after a sell-off in global AI stocks.

Brent crude eased to around $92 a barrel during Indian trading. Foreign investors bought shares worth about ₹4,865 crore, while domestic funds sold about ₹3,519 crore. Market breadth was slightly negative.

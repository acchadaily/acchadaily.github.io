MS Dhoni retires from IPL cricket after 278 matches and 5,439 runs; he will be Chennai Super Kings' Team Mentor from IPL 2027

CSK announced the news on retention day, ending a 19-year playing career that brought Chennai five titles. There will be no farewell match at Chepauk.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 2 min read

In short
5IPL titles as CSK captain
  • 278 IPL matches, 5,439 runs, 24 fifties
  • 5 titles as CSK captain (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023)
  • IPL 2027 mini-auction: 16 December, Goa

What happened

On Tuesday, 17 November, the deadline for IPL teams to submit their retention lists, Chennai Super Kings said on X that MS Dhoni 'takes charge as the Team Mentor'. He has stepped away as a player and will guide the team from IPL 2027.

The decision came quietly, only hours before the deadline. Cricbuzz reported that even people inside CSK learned of it only on Tuesday. CSK said he 'will always be a part of our identity' and that Chennai 'will always be his home'.

The numbers

Dhoni ends with 278 IPL matches, 5,439 runs and 24 fifties. As captain he led CSK to five titles: 2010, 2011, 2018, 2021 and 2023. His last IPL season was 2025, when CSK kept him as an 'uncapped' player for ₹4 crore. Harsha Bhogle called it 'the end of an era'.

Other retention-day moves

It was a busy day across the league. Mohammed Shami moved from Lucknow Super Giants to Rajasthan Royals for ₹8 crore. Arshdeep Singh went to Mumbai Indians for ₹18 crore. Liam Livingstone joined CSK for ₹6 crore. Kuldeep Yadav moved to Lucknow for ₹13.5 crore, and Kolkata now need a new captain after Ajinkya Rahane's exit.

What's next

The IPL 2027 mini-auction is in Goa on 16 December. As mentor, Dhoni is expected to have a big say in how CSK rebuild.

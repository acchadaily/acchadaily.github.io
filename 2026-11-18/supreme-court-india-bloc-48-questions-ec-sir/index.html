Rahul Gandhi and INDIA bloc want to put 48 questions to the CEC on voter-roll revision SIR; Supreme Court lists the plea for 9 December

The opposition wants to know whether all three election commissioners actually approved the nationwide SIR. The Election Commission says it has "serious reservations".

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 1 min read

In short
48questions for the poll body
  • 48 interrogatories filed
  • Bench led by CJI Surya Kant
  • Next hearing on 9 December 2026

What happened

On Tuesday, a bench led by Chief Justice Surya Kant heard a plea from Rahul Gandhi and other INDIA bloc leaders. They want to send 48 written questions, called interrogatories, to CEC Gyanesh Kumar, Election Commissioners Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi, and senior officials.

They ask for file records, meeting dates and any dissent, to check whether SIR decisions were taken by all three commissioners together, as the EC claimed in an October affidavit.

What's next

Harish Salve, for the EC, said the commission had "serious reservations" and opposed issuing any notice before a first hearing. The court adjourned the matter to 9 December and allowed the EC to file objections by then.

The CJI said the court would then hear the application and pass orders. Questions include whether officials could restore deleted voters and how fake-voter objections were handled.

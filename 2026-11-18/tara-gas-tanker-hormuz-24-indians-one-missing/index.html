LPG tanker with 24 Indians on board hit twice in the Strait of Hormuz; 21 rescued, one Indian seafarer missing and two in hospital

The Panama-flagged Tara Gas was struck near Oman on 16 November and hit again a day later, forcing the crew to abandon ship. India has condemned the attack.

What happened

The Tara Gas, a Panama-flagged tanker carrying LPG, was hit by a missile on 16 November about seven nautical miles north of Khasab in Oman. Shipping Minister Sarbananda Sonowal first said all 25 crew members, 24 of them Indian, were safe.

On Tuesday the ship was attacked again and the crew had to leave it. The foreign ministry said 21 Indians reached the shore at Khasab safely, two were admitted to hospital and one is still missing.

India's response

India condemned the attack and called the repeated attacks on sailors in West Asia "deeply worrisome". It said attacks on merchant ships must stop. No one has officially said who fired at the tanker.

Indian seafarers have been badly hit by the eight-month West Asia war: The Wire reports that 14 Indian mariners have died because of it.

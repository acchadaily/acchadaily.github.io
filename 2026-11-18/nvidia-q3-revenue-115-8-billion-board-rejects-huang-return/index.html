Nvidia's sales double to $115.8 billion in its first results without Jensen Huang, but the board says he won't be coming back

The AI chip giant beat Wall Street's estimates and raised its outlook. Huang said he would be 'honored' to return, but Nvidia's board quickly shut the door.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$115.8BNvidia revenue in one quarter, up 103%
  • Revenue: $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago
  • Next-quarter forecast: $127.4–132.6 billion
  • New share buyback: $150 billion

What happened

Nvidia reported results for the July–October quarter on Tuesday, 17 November. Revenue came in at $115.8 billion, more than double the $57 billion it made a year earlier, and well above the roughly $109 billion analysts expected.

Its data-centre business, which sells the chips that power AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude, made $107.6 billion of that, up 110% from a year ago.

The Huang question

This was Nvidia's first results call since co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as CEO last week. Hours earlier, Huang said in a post on X that he would be 'honored' to return if the board felt it was best for shareholders.

The board's answer came on the call itself. Chair Harvey Jones said the board accepted his resignation unanimously and 'does not intend to reinstate' him. It is now searching for a permanent CEO, while finance chief Colette Kress runs the company for now.

What's next

Nvidia expects $127.4–132.6 billion in revenue next quarter, again above forecasts, and announced a $150 billion share buyback. Kress called it 'business as usual' and said the company's cloud order backlog has grown to $2.5 trillion. Shares rose in after-hours trading.

Why it matters

Nvidia's numbers are a mood check for the whole AI boom. Strong results ease fears that AI spending is slowing, just a day before Anthropic is set to list on Nasdaq.

A two-month lull ends: Iran fires drones at a US radar in Bahrain for the third time in 5 days, and a US jet strikes a missile site on Qeshm Island

Bahrain says it stopped the attack and the US reports no damage, but the tit-for-tat raises fears of a wider flare-up as talks stall.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
3Iranian attacks on Bahrain in 5 days
  • War began on 28 Feb 2026, now in its 8th month
  • US strike hit a Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm Island
  • Egypt, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the UAE condemned the Bahrain attack

What happened

Iran sent drones at a US radar site in Bahrain on Tuesday, its third such attack in five days. Bahrain said its air defences stopped them, and the US military said nothing was damaged and no one was hurt.

Hours later, a US fighter jet hit a Revolutionary Guard missile launch site on Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, a US official said. A local official said no homes or businesses were damaged.

Why it matters

The war began on 28 February and is now in its eighth month. Qatar warned the attacks hurt mediation efforts, and Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the UAE condemned the strike on Bahrain.

Iran calls the situation "no war, no peace". The US has sent a third aircraft carrier to the region.

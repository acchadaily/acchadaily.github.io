Cyclone Akvan: Odisha lists 2.04 lakh people for possible evacuation, and red alerts cover 7 Andhra districts ahead of a 20 November landfall

IMD expects Akvan to become a very severe cyclone and hit the Andhra coast between Nellore and Tuni around noon on Friday, with winds of 120–130 km/h.

In short
2.04Lpeople listed for evacuation in Odisha
  • Landfall expected around noon on 20 Nov, between Nellore and Tuni
  • Winds of 120–130 km/h, gusting to 145 km/h
  • 74 rescue teams deployed in Odisha

Where it's headed

Early on 17 November, the storm sat about 670 km south-southeast of Visakhapatnam. IMD expects it to become a very severe cyclone by 19 November and cross the coast between Nellore and Tuni around noon on 20 November.

Winds could reach 120–130 km/h, with gusts up to 145 km/h. Kakinada, Konaseema, East and West Godavari, Eluru, Krishna and Bapatla are on red alert for very heavy rain on 19 November.

Getting ready

Odisha has identified 2,04,070 people who may need to move, including over 1.2 lakh in Ganjam, and ordered evacuations in six districts. It has deployed 74 rescue teams.

Andhra CM Chandrababu Naidu reviewed preparations on Tuesday. Fishermen have been told to stay off the sea, and IMD may still adjust the landfall spot.

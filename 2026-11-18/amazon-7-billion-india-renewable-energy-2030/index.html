Amazon will invest $7 billion more in Indian wind and solar projects by 2030, on top of $4 billion already committed

It also announced four new projects in Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan with 934 MW of capacity.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 1 min read

In short
$7Bnew green-energy investment in India
  • $7 billion new + $4 billion already invested
  • 4 new projects, 934 MW
  • 15,000+ EVs across 500+ Indian cities

What happened

Amazon says it will put $7 billion into renewable energy projects in India by 2030, on top of the $4 billion it has already invested here. It also named four new wind and solar projects totalling 934 MW: two in Karnataka, a 300 MW solar farm in Madhya Pradesh and a 200 MW solar farm in Rajasthan.

These four are part of the existing $4 billion of contracted projects.

Why it matters

Amazon wants to cut the carbon intensity of the power it uses by 65% by 2030. In India it already has 14 big wind and solar projects, 43 rooftop-style solar sites and over 15,000 electric delivery vehicles in 500+ cities.

This comes on top of Amazon's earlier promise to invest $35 billion across its India businesses by 2030.

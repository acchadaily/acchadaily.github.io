First Quad leaders' summit in 2 years: Modi, Trump, Takaichi and Albanese plan a first-ever joint P-8 patrol aircraft exercise in 2027

Meeting in Manila on Tuesday, the four leaders spoke out against changing the Indo-Pacific status quo by force and agreed to cut reliance on single suppliers of critical minerals.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 1 min read

In short
2027first Quad patrol-aircraft drill
  • First Quad leaders' summit in over 2 years
  • Joint P-8/P-3C exercise planned for 2027
  • Quad Business Leaders' Forum set for early 2027

What was agreed

Leaders of India, the US, Japan and Australia met on the sidelines of the ASEAN summit. It was their first leaders' summit in over two years and the first of Trump's second term.

They plan a joint maritime exercise in 2027 using P-8 and P-3C patrol planes, the first of its kind under the Quad. They also want to rely less on single suppliers of minerals used in chips, EVs, batteries and defence.

What else

The statement opposed changing the status quo by force or coercion and raised concerns over arbitrary export curbs, without naming China. A first Quad Business Leaders' Forum is planned for early 2027.

Modi called the Quad a "constructive and trusted partnership" for India and also met Philippine President Marcos.

Grammys 2027 nominations: Raye leads with 11, Rosalía has 9, and Beyoncé gets her 100th nomination ever

Taylor Swift, Olivia Rodrigo and Bruno Mars are among eight acts up for Album of the Year. The show is on 7 February.

  • Ceremony: 7 February 2027, Los Angeles

What happened

The Recording Academy announced nominations for the 69th Grammy Awards on 16 November. British singer Raye leads with 11 nods, followed by Rosalía with 9. Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo, Phoebe Bridgers and Ella Langley have 7 each.

Beyoncé picked up her 100th career nomination, extending her record as the most-nominated artist in Grammy history.

Album of the Year race

The eight nominees include Taylor Swift's The Life of a Showgirl, Bruno Mars's The Romantic, Olivia Rodrigo's you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love, Rosalía's LUX and Raye's This Music May Contain Hope. Tame Impala's track with Blackpink's JENNIE is up for Record of the Year. The ceremony is on 7 February 2027 in Los Angeles.

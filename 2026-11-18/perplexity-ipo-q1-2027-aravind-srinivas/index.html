Perplexity could file for a US IPO as early as early 2027, says Aravind Srinivas, as its yearly revenue run rate hits $1 billion

The Chennai-born CEO had earlier said Perplexity would not go public before 2028. Faster growth has changed the plan, with India its biggest market by users.

What happened

Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas told CNBC that the AI search company could file for a US stock market listing as soon as the first quarter of 2027. 'I wouldn't rule out filing something' in early 2027, he said, because the business is growing faster than expected.

That is a big change. Earlier, Srinivas had said Perplexity would not list before 2028.

The numbers

Perplexity's annual recurring revenue, the yearly value of its subscriptions, has reached $1 billion. The company had expected about $750 million by the time of an IPO.

The app has been downloaded more than 80 million times.

Srinivas co-founded Perplexity in 2022 with Denis Yarats, Johnny Ho and Andy Konwinski. In June 2026, Tech Mahindra rolled out Perplexity's enterprise product across its sales teams.

The India angle

India is Perplexity's largest market. A deal with Airtel gave its 360 million users a free year of Perplexity Pro, and downloads peaked at 22 million in October 2025. After the offer ended, downloads fell to about 14 million by July 2026.

Indians have not paid much yet: estimated monthly revenue from India rose from $34,000 in January 2025 to $156,000 in July 2026, according to TechCrunch figures quoted by Inc42. The IPO talk comes as AI companies line up to list, with Anthropic set to debut on Nasdaq this week.

Ukraine knocks out a road span of Russia's Kerch Bridge to Crimea, as Russia says it downed a record 1,441 drones in one night

Zelensky called the strike "unprecedented" and the last step of a seven-stage operation. Road traffic to Crimea is halted, while rail services restarted by Tuesday afternoon.

What happened

Ukraine hit the Kerch Bridge, which links Russia to occupied Crimea, for a second day running on Tuesday. Its military said an arch and a span of the road section collapsed. The rail section was damaged but still standing.

Zelensky said drones hit the road section, Neptune missiles went for the rail line and Flamingo missiles struck the final blow. Russia's investigators confirmed drone damage.

Why it matters

The bridge is Putin's prized link to Crimea, and Ukraine's army called it "the symbol of the occupation". The Kremlin says it will be rebuilt "even better".

Russia's defence ministry said it shot down 1,441 Ukrainian drones overnight, after 1,336 the night before. Russian strikes on Kyiv killed at least two people on the same day.

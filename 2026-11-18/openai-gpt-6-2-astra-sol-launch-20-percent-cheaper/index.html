OpenAI launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol; Astra is 20% cheaper for developers and is its first model to hit a 'High' self-improvement risk level

The two new models are live in ChatGPT and Codex for paying users. Astra is for the hardest work, Sol for everyday tasks.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
20%price cut on GPT-6.2 Astra's API
  • Astra API price: $8 input / $40 output per million tokens
  • Sol API price unchanged at $2 / $10
  • Available for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users

What happened

OpenAI released two new models on 17 November: GPT-6.2 Astra, its top model for complex projects, and GPT-6.2 Sol, a faster, cheaper model for daily work. Both are rolling out to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT and Codex, and to developers through the API.

Astra's API price drops 20%, to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. Sol stays at $2 and $10.

The safety flag

OpenAI says Astra is the first model it has released that meets the 'High' level for AI self-improvement under its own safety rules. Both models also hit its 'Critical' level for cybersecurity, so they come with tighter limits on hacking-related help.

How good is it?

On one third-party leaderboard for 3D design tasks, Astra scored 1,318, ahead of Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 at 1,271. The launch lands two days before Anthropic's stock market debut.

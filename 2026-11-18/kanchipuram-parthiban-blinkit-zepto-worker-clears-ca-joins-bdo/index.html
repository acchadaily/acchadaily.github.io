He packed Zepto orders and rode a rented ₹600 cycle for Blinkit deliveries, studying between drops; now Parthiban is a CA finalist at BDO

After failing CA Intermediate twice, a first-generation graduate from Kanchipuram cleared it while working night shifts in Bengaluru.

The hard months

Parthiban, from Kanchipuram in Tamil Nadu, is the first graduate in his family; his father is a nadaswaram player. He cleared the CA Foundation exam on his first try with 305 out of 400, but then failed CA Intermediate twice.

In September 2025 he moved to Bengaluru to support himself. He packed orders at a Zepto store in Koramangala on night shifts that sometimes ran till 7 am, working in freezer rooms as cold as -15°C. Later he became a Blinkit delivery partner on a rented cycle, sometimes riding 90–100 km a day on weekends and studying in the 20-minute gaps between orders.

The turnaround

He cleared one group of CA Intermediate in November 2025 and the second in March 2026, becoming a CA Finalist. He went to four firm interviews and got picked by three.

On 2 April 2026 he joined global accounting firm BDO's Bengaluru office as an Article Assistant, in the same area where he had once packed groceries. Colleagues there praise his hard work and humility.

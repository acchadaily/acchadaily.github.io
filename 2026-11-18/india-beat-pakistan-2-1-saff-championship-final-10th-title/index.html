India come from behind to beat Pakistan 2-1 in the SAFF Championship final and win a record 10th title; Sandip Mandi scores in the 88th minute

Pakistan led at half-time in Dhaka, but Suhail Bhat equalised and Mandi headed in a late winner for India's third SAFF title in a row.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
10thSAFF title for India, a record
  • Score: India 2, Pakistan 1
  • Winning goal: Sandip Mandi, 88th minute
  • India's 10th SAFF title, third in a row

What happened

India beat Pakistan 2-1 in the SAFF Championship final at Dhaka's National Stadium on Tuesday, 17 November. Pakistan, playing their first-ever SAFF final, went ahead through Alamgir Ghazi in first-half stoppage time.

The game turned in the 59th minute when Pakistan's Junaid Shah was sent off after a VAR review. About a minute later, 21-year-old Suhail Ahmad Bhat equalised. In the 88th minute, Sandip Mandi headed in a corner from Abdul Rabeeh to win it.

Why it matters

It is India's 10th SAFF title, extending their record, and their third in a row under coach Naushad Moosa. Bhat, from Kashmir, also took home both the Golden Boot and the Golden Ball for the tournament.

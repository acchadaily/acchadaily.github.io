Mahindra Last Mile Mobility wins the £1 million Earthshot Prize in Mumbai for putting 4 lakh electric three-wheelers on India's roads

Prince William's climate prize came to India for the first time, and an Indian e-auto maker took home the 'Clean our Air' award.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
4 lakh+electric three-wheelers sold
  • £1 million prize for each of 5 winners
  • 500 women trained to drive and fix e-autos
  • Target: 10 lakh EVs by 2031

What happened

The Earthshot Prize, started by Prince William, held its 2026 awards night in Mumbai on 17 November, the first time it has come to India. Five winners were picked from 15 finalists, and each gets £1 million to grow their work.

Mahindra Last Mile Mobility won the 'Clean our Air' category. The company has sold more than 4 lakh electric last-mile vehicles, the e-autos and cargo three-wheelers you see delivering parcels and ferrying passengers, and says they have avoided over 1.8 lakh tonnes of CO2.

Why it matters

Electric models now make up more than half of all new three-wheelers sold in India, the first vehicle segment in the country to cross that mark. Lower running costs can lift a driver's take-home pay by 25–30%, according to the Earthshot profile.

The company has also trained 500 women to drive and repair e-autos. It plans to use the prize money for more women drivers, easier loans and charging points in places that lack them, and is aiming for 10 lakh EVs on Indian roads by 2031.

Warren Buffett gives away about $5.5 billion in Berkshire shares for Thanksgiving, roughly four times last year's gift

The 96-year-old investor donated 92.25 lakh shares to four family foundations and repeated his message that kindness costs nothing but 'compounds'.

What happened

On 16 November, Berkshire Hathaway said its chairman emeritus Warren Buffett was converting 6,150 Class A shares into 9,225,000 Class B shares and giving them all away. The Susan Thompson Buffett Foundation gets 36.9 lakh shares, and the Sherwood, Howard G. Buffett and NoVo foundations get 18.45 lakh each.

Forbes valued the gift at about $5.5 billion, around four times the roughly $1.3 billion he gave the same foundations last Thanksgiving. These charities fund scholarships, food security, education and community work.

His message

Buffett says all of his Berkshire shares will eventually go to the four foundations, which are run by his children.

In a short note, he said that kindness is 'costless but also priceless', and added that it compounds 'at a better rate than anything' he and his late partner Charlie Munger ever bought. His advice for the holiday: thank a selfless person in your life, or better still, be one for someone else.

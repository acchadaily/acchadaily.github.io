Milk keeps getting costlier: Himachal's Vyasdhenu adds ₹4 a litre, after Amul and Mother Dairy's ₹2–4 hikes and Nandini's ₹8 jump

Dairies across India have raised prices in November, blaming costlier fodder, feed, fuel and packaging.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹2–8per litre hikes in November
  • Vyasdhenu: ₹70 a litre, up ₹4 (from 17 Nov)
  • Mother Dairy double-toned: ₹54 → ₹56
  • Nandini toned milk: up ₹8 a litre from 1 Nov

Who raised prices

Himachal's Vyasdhenu raised its milk price by ₹4 a litre to ₹70 from Tuesday, 17 November. Its paneer is up ₹50 to ₹400 a kg and curd up ₹20 to ₹120 a kg.

Amul and Mother Dairy raised milk prices by ₹2–4 a litre from 12 November. Mother Dairy's double-toned milk went from ₹54 to ₹56, and cow milk from ₹62 to ₹65. Karnataka's Nandini went up ₹8 a litre from 1 November.

Why

Dairies blame higher costs for fodder, cattle feed, fuel and packaging. Amul and Mother Dairy say about 80% of the increase will go to farmers.

For a family buying a litre a day, a ₹4 hike adds about ₹120 to the monthly bill. Sanchi in Madhya Pradesh also raised its prices from 14 November.

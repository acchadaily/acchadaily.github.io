Ramayana: Part 1 crosses ₹800 crore net in India, reaching ₹821 crore in 13 days and about ₹1,483 crore worldwide

The Ranbir Kapoor–Yash epic made ₹28.7 crore on its second Tuesday, a dip of about 10% from Monday.

What happened

Ramayana: Part 1 earned about ₹28.7 crore net in India on Tuesday, 17 November, its 13th day, according to trade tracker Sacnilk. That takes its India net total to about ₹821 crore, past the ₹800 crore mark.

Hindi shows brought in about ₹21 crore of Tuesday's total. The film has made about ₹502 crore overseas, for a worldwide gross of about ₹1,483 crore.

What's next

The film has entered the top 5 highest-grossing Indian films. Its next test is this Friday, when Dhanush and Mammootty's OM and Godzilla Minus Zero open in theatres.

Anthropic's IPO is likely to be priced above its $70–78 range as orders pile up; shares start trading on Nasdaq Thursday as 'ANT'

The Claude maker closed its order book on Tuesday and sets its final price on Wednesday, a day before its stock market debut.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 1 min read

In short
~7xorders vs shares on offer (by Sunday)
  • Price range: $70–78 a share
  • Valuation at $78: about $1.4 trillion
  • Trading starts Thursday, 19 Nov, as ANT

What happened

Anthropic is likely to sell its shares above the $70–78 range it set on 10 November, CNBC reported on 17 November, citing sources. Bloomberg reported that investors were told to expect the top of the range or higher.

Orders had reached nearly seven times the shares on offer by Sunday, with several orders of $10 billion or more, according to The Next Web.

What's next

The order book closed at 4 pm New York time on Tuesday. The final price comes Wednesday, and trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT starts Thursday, 19 November. At $78 a share, Anthropic would be valued at about $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis.

Amazon and Google will own about 15% and 14% of the company after the listing.

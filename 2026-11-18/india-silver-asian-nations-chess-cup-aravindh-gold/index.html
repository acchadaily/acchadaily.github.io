India win silver at the Asian Nations Chess Cup in Abu Dhabi, as Aravindh Chithambaram takes individual gold with 8 points from 9 games

India finished just one point behind champions Russia, and Indian players won gold, silver and bronze on their boards.

accha? daily · 18 November 2026 · 1 min read

In short
8/9Aravindh's score on Board 4
  • India: 15 match points, silver behind Russia's 16
  • Aravindh Chithambaram: gold, 8/9 on Board 4
  • Individual silver for Pranesh, bronze for Pranav

What happened

India's men's team of Nihal Sarin, Pranav Venkatesh, Pranesh M and Aravindh Chithambaram finished second in the Open section with 15 match points. Their only loss was to Russia, who won the title with 16 points. India beat Syria 4-0 in the last round.

China also had 15 points, but India edged them on tie-break to take silver.

Medals on the boards

Aravindh won individual gold on Board 4 with 8 points from 9 games. Pranesh took silver on Board 3 and Pranav bronze on Board 2. In the women's section, India finished seventh, but Shubhi Gupta won a bronze on Board 3.

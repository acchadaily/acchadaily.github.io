Nike plans to triple its India business by 2031, with a full cricket shoe range and a running shoe built for Indian runners

India is one of the bright spots in Nike's new turnaround plan, even as the company warns that its global sales will shrink next year.

accha? daily · 21 November 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp
In short
3xNike's India growth target by 2031
  • India sportswear market: ~$9B now, ~$14B by 2031
  • Cricket shoe range before LA 2028
  • Global FY27 revenue to fall high single digits

The plan

Nike wants to more than triple the size of its India business by 2031. The goal is part of its "Full Stride Ahead" strategy, laid out at its first investor day since 2017 earlier this week.

It is betting on India's fast-growing sportswear market, which is worth about $9 billion today and is growing more than 10% a year, to about $14 billion by 2031.

Cricket and running

Nike plans a full range of cricket footwear before the LA 2028 Olympics, where cricket returns to the Games. It is also building on its partnership with Mumbai Indians across the men's and women's leagues.

For runners, it is launching Vegam Run, a shoe designed using insights from Indian runners — a sign that global brands now see Indian feet, roads and weather as a market worth designing for.

Why India matters to Nike

Globally, Nike is going through a tough patch. It has guided for revenue to fall by a high single-digit percentage in FY27, and its shares fell sharply after the investor day this week.

Its long-term targets are about $5 billion of extra revenue by FY31 and an operating margin of 12–14%. Fast-growing markets like India are central to getting there, especially as Indian rivals and global brands like Adidas, Puma and Asics fight for the same young buyers.

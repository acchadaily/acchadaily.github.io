COP31 climate talks in Antalya run into overtime after a draft deal drops all mention of moving away from fossil fuels

A third draft is due on Saturday morning, as many countries push back against the weaker text.

Where things stand

The UN climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, did not finish on time on Friday. A revised draft removed all references to transitioning away from fossil fuels and dropped a proposed $120 billion target for adaptation finance, after objections from Saudi Arabia and other oil producers, according to Reuters.

Panama threatened to formally object, and a bloc of countries from Europe, Africa, Latin America and the Pacific demanded stronger language. Delegates approved only technical items late on Friday.

What's next

COP31 President Murat Kurum promised a third draft on Saturday morning. Separately, Turkey says it has signed deals worth more than $26 billion during the summit, including a preliminary $15 billion renewable energy deal with China.

Russian missiles and a drone land in Poland during a massive strike on Ukraine; NATO calls it a 'grave violation'

No one was hurt in Poland, but PM Donald Tusk said it looked like "an intentional provocation".

accha? daily · 21 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
3objects crossed into Poland
  • One object was a Kh-101 cruise missile
  • NATO jets scrambled
  • Rutte visits Warsaw on Saturday

What happened

Poland's defence minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz said one of the objects that fell on Polish territory on Friday was a Kh-101 cruise missile, a second was likely a missile, and the third was most likely a jet-powered drone. NATO jets were scrambled. No one was injured.

Tusk linked the incident to military drills being held in Poland by the "Coalition of the Willing" countries backing Ukraine. NATO chief Mark Rutte is due in Warsaw on Saturday.

In Ukraine

It happened during a huge Russian missile and drone attack on Ukraine. Two people were killed at a railway station in Lviv, near the Polish border, and three were reported killed in Kyiv, with emergency power cuts in several regions.

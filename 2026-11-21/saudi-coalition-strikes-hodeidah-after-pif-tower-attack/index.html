Saudi-led coalition strikes Yemen's Hodeidah port after the Houthi hit on Riyadh's PIF tower; Houthis threaten Red Sea ports

The tit-for-tat raises fears for shipping and for an aid lifeline that millions of Yemenis depend on.

accha? daily · 21 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • PIF tower hit on 19 Nov, no injuries
  • Hodeidah: key port for aid
  • War reignited in July 2026

The strikes

The Saudi-led coalition said it destroyed facilities at Hodeidah port used to smuggle weapons and missiles. Yemen's Houthi-run information ministry said the port was still working. The coalition also said it intercepted Houthi missiles on Friday.

The strikes followed Thursday's Houthi missile hit on the Public Investment Fund's tower, Riyadh's tallest building, which caused damage but no injuries.

Threats and warnings

The Houthis threatened Jeddah and other Saudi Red Sea ports and the Shuqaiq power station, later claimed attacks on Shuqaiq and a port in Jazan, and warned airlines to avoid Saudi airspace. The UN warned that strikes on Hodeidah put a vital aid route at risk. The war reignited in July.

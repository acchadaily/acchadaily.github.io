Election Commission postpones West Bengal's voter-list revision that was due to start on 23 November; new dates to come later

The state's Chief Electoral Officer cited "some unavoidable circumstances" and gave no other reason.

accha? daily · 21 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Was due to start 23 Nov
  • Qualifying date: 1 Jan 2027
  • ~91 lakh names removed earlier this year

What was postponed

The Special Summary Revision of the photo electoral roll for 2027, which was due to begin in West Bengal on 23 November, has been put off. The revised schedule will be shared later, the Chief Electoral Officer's office said. The qualifying date for new voters stays 1 January 2027.

A summary revision is the routine yearly update when people can add their names, correct details or flag entries for removal.

The context

Bengal's rolls have been in the spotlight all year. After an earlier special intensive revision, about 91 lakh names, around 11.9% of the electorate, had been removed by 6 April.

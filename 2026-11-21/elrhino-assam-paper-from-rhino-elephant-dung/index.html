An Assam father and daughter turn rhino and elephant dung into notebooks, giving villagers a reason to protect wildlife

Elrhino's paper is made from washed and boiled dung mixed with recycled fibres.

accha? daily · 21 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Founded 2014
  • Uses banana-stem fibre and cotton waste
  • Villagers paid to collect dung

How it began

Retired mining engineer Mahesh Chandra Bora started Elrhino in 2014, experimenting at home with his wife's kitchen blender and window screens. His daughter Nisha later left a 17-year corporate career to join him.

The dung is washed, boiled and pulped, then mixed with recycled paper, cotton waste and banana-stem fibre.

Why it helps

Villagers near wildlife areas earn money by collecting the dung, which turns rhinos and elephants into a source of income rather than a nuisance. Elrhino now makes notebooks, journals, bags, lampshades and even playing cards.

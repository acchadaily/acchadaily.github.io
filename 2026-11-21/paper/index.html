Airtel is India's most valuable brand at $43.2 billion in Kantar BrandZ 2026; Blinkit enters at No. 27 and Meesho grows fastest, up 64%

Kantar's yearly list of India's 100 most valuable brands is out. Telecom and banks still rule the top, but quick commerce and value shopping are climbing fast.

Bharti Airtel keeps the No. 1 spot in the Kantar BrandZ India 2026 ranking with a brand value of $43.2 billion. That is 5% more than last year and about 210% more than in 2020, Kantar says.

The rest of the top five is a wall of banks and IT: HDFC Bank ($35.6 billion), TCS ($24.1 billion), SBI ($23.6 billion) and ICICI Bank ($20.1 billion). Financial services brands together make up 29% of the value of the whole list.

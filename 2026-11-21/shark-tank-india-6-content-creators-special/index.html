Shark Tank India Season 6 opens a 'Content Creators Special': influencers can now pitch their brands to the Sharks

"Don't just post your ideas. Pitch them!" says the new call for entries on Sony LIV.

accha? daily · 21 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Registrations open on Sony LIV
  • Separate Young Founders Special also open
  • ChatGPT co-presents Season 6

What's new

Shark Tank India has opened registrations for a special segment only for content creators in Season 6, under the line "Don't just post your ideas. Pitch them!" Sign-ups are open on the show's website on Sony LIV. A separate Young Founders Special is also taking entries.

The promo poster shows ChatGPT as a co-presenter of the season.

Why creators

Creator-led brands have become one of the hottest corners of Indian D2C. Coverage of the new segment points to examples like comedian Kusha Kapila's innerwear label Underneat and Bhuvan Bam's House of Sarkkar.

Creators bring a ready audience, which cuts marketing costs — the very thing most Shark Tank pitches struggle with. The special lets the show tap that trend, and gives its own social media a boost from creators who will promote their episodes.

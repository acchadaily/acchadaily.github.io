A cancer vaccine cuts the risk of death by 41% for leukaemia patients in a late-stage trial

Patients on the vaccine lived a median of 24 months, against 14.7 months on standard care.

accha? daily · 21 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
41%lower risk of death
  • 126 patients in Phase 3
  • 24.0 vs 14.7 months median survival
  • US filing planned H1 2027

The trial

Sellas Life Sciences says its vaccine galinpepimut-S helped patients with acute myeloid leukaemia (AML) live longer in its Phase 3 REGAL trial of 126 people. Median survival was 24.0 months on the vaccine versus 14.7 months on the best available treatment.

The patients were in a second or later remission and could not have a stem cell transplant, a group with few options.

What's next

No new safety problems were seen. The results are early and not yet peer-reviewed, and the company plans to seek US approval in the first half of 2027.

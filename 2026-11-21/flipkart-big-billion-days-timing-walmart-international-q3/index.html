Flipkart's Big Billion Days timing knocks about 5 points off Walmart International's growth; the unit still grows 7% to $35.9 billion

Because this year's sale fell in a different quarter, Walmart's Indian business showed up weaker than usual in its latest results.

accha? daily · 21 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$35.9BWalmart International quarterly sales, up 7%
  • Big Billion Days shift cut growth ~500 bps
  • Walmart total revenue $188.8 billion, +5.2%
  • Walmart raised its full-year forecast

What Walmart reported

Walmart International's net sales rose 7% to $35.9 billion in the third quarter, or 3.3% if you strip out currency moves. Walmart said the shift in timing of Flipkart's Big Billion Days sale cut the unit's growth by about 500 basis points and the whole company's growth by about 100 basis points.

Walmart expects to get roughly that 100 basis points back in the fourth quarter, when the festive sales are counted.

The bigger picture

Walmart's total revenue rose 5.2% to $188.8 billion, and it raised its forecast for the full year. Last year's numbers included a one-off $722 million non-cash charge related to PhonePe, which makes comparisons messier.

Flipkart is Walmart's biggest bet outside the US and is preparing for a stock market listing in India.

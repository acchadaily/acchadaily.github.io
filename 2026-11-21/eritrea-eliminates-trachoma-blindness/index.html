Eritrea beats trachoma, the world's leading infectious cause of blindness, the WHO confirms

It is the 34th country to do so, and the 11th in Africa.

accha? daily · 21 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
94%fall in people at risk worldwide since 2002
  • 34th country, 11th in Africa
  • All 58 districts surveyed
  • Free antibiotics donated

The win

The WHO has confirmed that trachoma is no longer a public health problem in Eritrea after surveys in all 58 districts. The country used the WHO's SAFE strategy — surgery, antibiotics, face washing and a cleaner environment — with the antibiotic azithromycin donated through the International Trachoma Initiative.

Worldwide progress

The number of people at risk of trachoma globally has fallen 94% since 2002. Elimination does not mean zero cases, so Eritrea will keep watching for new infections.

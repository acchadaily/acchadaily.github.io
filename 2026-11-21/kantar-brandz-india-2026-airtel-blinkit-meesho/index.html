Airtel is India's most valuable brand at $43.2 billion in Kantar BrandZ 2026; Blinkit enters at No. 27 and Meesho grows fastest, up 64%

Kantar's yearly list of India's 100 most valuable brands is out. Telecom and banks still rule the top, but quick commerce and value shopping are climbing fast.

  • Top 100 worth $460.3 billion, down 12%

Who is on top

Bharti Airtel keeps the No. 1 spot in the Kantar BrandZ India 2026 ranking with a brand value of $43.2 billion. That is 5% more than last year and about 210% more than in 2020, Kantar says.

The rest of the top five is a wall of banks and IT: HDFC Bank ($35.6 billion), TCS ($24.1 billion), SBI ($23.6 billion) and ICICI Bank ($20.1 billion). Financial services brands together make up 29% of the value of the whole list.

The fast risers

Meesho is the fastest-growing brand, with its value up 64% to $1.9 billion (rank 59). Royal Enfield is close behind, up 60% to $3.8 billion (rank 30).

Other big gainers include TVS (up 33%), PhonePe (31%), SBI (26%), CRED and Paytm (both 23%) and Flipkart (19%). Many of the risers sell to price-conscious buyers in smaller towns, or sell everyday digital payments.

Blinkit's big debut

Nine brands are new to the Top 100 this year. The highest new entry is Blinkit, which comes straight in at No. 27 with a value of $4.3 billion, ahead of some brands that have been around for decades.

It is a sign of how quickly 10-minute delivery has become a habit in Indian cities. Blinkit, owned by Eternal (formerly Zomato), has spent the year expanding into everything from electronics to ambulances.

The bigger picture

The combined value of all 100 brands is $460.3 billion, which Kantar puts at about 11% of India's GDP. That total is down 12% from last year, so the big gains of a few brands came while the overall pool shrank.

BrandZ measures how much of a company's value comes from its brand alone, based on its finances and on surveys of what consumers think and feel about it.

💡 Explained

What is Kantar BrandZ, and what does "brand value" mean?

BrandZ is a yearly ranking by research firm Kantar. It works out how much of a company's worth comes from its brand alone — the extra that people pay, or the loyalty they show, because of the name. It mixes the company's financial results with surveys of thousands of consumers about how they feel about the brand. So a brand can rise even when the company's profits don't, if people start to trust it more.

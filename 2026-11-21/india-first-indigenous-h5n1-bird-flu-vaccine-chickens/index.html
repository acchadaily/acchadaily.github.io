India unveils its first home-grown bird flu vaccine for chickens; one dose protects for about six months

The H5N1 vaccine was developed by ICAR's high-security animal disease lab in Bhopal.

accha? daily · 21 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
500+bird flu outbreaks in India since 2006
  • Developed by ICAR-NIHSAD, Bhopal
  • Single dose, ~6 months protection
  • Targets clade 2.3.2.1a

The vaccine

Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan unveiled the vaccine, developed by ICAR-NIHSAD in Bhopal, on Friday. It targets clade 2.3.2.1a of the H5N1 virus, the strain circulating in India, and a single dose gives about six months of protection, the institute says.

Why it matters

India has had more than 500 outbreaks of H5N1 and H5N8 bird flu since 2006. Each one usually means culling every bird in a wide area around the farm, which wipes out poultry farmers' income and pushes up egg and chicken prices.

A local vaccine could protect flocks before outbreaks, rather than relying only on culling after them.

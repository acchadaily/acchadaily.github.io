European Commission confirms a cyberattack that used AI tools; payroll and medical claims systems were down for over 10 days

The first signs of the attack were spotted on 22 October.

accha? daily · 21 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
10+ daysstaff systems disrupted
  • First signs: 22 October
  • Payroll, medical claims, translation hit
  • No sign of staff-wide data breach

What was hit

The European Commission confirmed it was hit by attackers using AI tools. Its payroll system Sysper, its medical reimbursement system MyPMO and translation services were disrupted for more than ten days. It said there is no evidence so far of a breach of all staff data.

The EU's cyber response team CERT-EU links the attack to ARTEX, a penetration-testing tool turned to criminal use, though the Commission has not confirmed that.

Why it matters

Security experts have warned for months that AI makes attacks faster and cheaper to run. A successful hit on the EU's executive body is one of the clearest public examples yet.

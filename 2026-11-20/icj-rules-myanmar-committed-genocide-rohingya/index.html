UN's top court rules 9–6 that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, the first time it has found a state guilty of genocide

The International Court of Justice ordered Myanmar to prosecute those responsible and allow displaced Rohingya to return safely.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
9–6judges' vote in The Gambia v. Myanmar
  • Vote: 9–6
  • Case filed by The Gambia in 2019
  • Myanmar must report to the court within 2 years

The ruling

In a case brought by The Gambia in 2019, the International Court of Justice found that the only reasonable conclusion from Myanmar's actions was an intent to destroy the Rohingya in northern Rakhine State. It also found Myanmar failed to prevent and punish genocide. It rejected a separate claim that Myanmar publicly incited genocide.

It is the first time the court has held a country responsible for genocide.

What Myanmar must do

Myanmar must investigate and prosecute those responsible, including military commanders, pass laws banning genocide, and allow Rohingya to return safely. Compensation will be decided later, and Myanmar must report back within two years. The ruling is binding, but the court has no way to enforce it.

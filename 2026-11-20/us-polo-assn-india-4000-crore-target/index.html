U.S. Polo Assn. targets ₹4,000 crore of sales in India this year; India is now its fastest-growing market

India already makes up about 20% of the brand's global sales, through its partnership with Arvind Fashions.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹4,000 crIndia consumer sales target, FY27
  • India: ~20% of global sales
  • Global retail sales 2025: $2.7 billion
  • Operates in 190 countries

The target

U.S. Polo Assn. aims for ₹4,000 crore in consumer sales in India in FY27, after high-teens growth. India is its third-largest market after the US and Turkey, its fastest-growing, and about 20% of global sales. Worldwide, the brand sold $2.7 billion at retail in 2025 across 190 countries.

How it plans to grow

The brand is run in India by Arvind Fashions, which now gets 53% of its sales from its own stores and website and wants 65%. It started in menswear but now sells womenswear, kidswear, footwear and innerwear, and is using brand ambassador Padmanabh Singh, the polo-playing Jaipur royal, to reach younger shoppers.

India's women come from behind to beat Malaysia 4–1 at the Asian Champions Trophy in Bhopal

The defending champions trailed after 19 minutes but scored four times, with captain Salima Tete among the scorers.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
4–1India's opening win in Bhopal
  • Score: India 4–1 Malaysia
  • Scorers: Mumtaz, Salima, Puja, Lalremsiami
  • Next: vs South Korea, 21 November

The match

Malaysia's Nuramirah Zulkifli scored in the 19th minute, but Mumtaz Khan equalised a minute later and it was 1–1 at half-time. Captain Salima Tete then put India ahead, Puja Sahoo scored from a penalty corner, and Lalremsiami made it 4–1 in the 49th minute. Puja Sahoo was named player of the match.

Next up

India play South Korea on 21 November in the six-team tournament at Bhopal's International Sports Complex in Nathu Barkheda. Entry is free.

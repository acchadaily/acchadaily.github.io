A pocket-sized ECG built in Dehradun has reached over 20,000 villages, giving heart reports in about two minutes

Sunfox's Spandan plugs into a smartphone and needs no internet or extra battery.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
60,000+Spandan devices in use
  • Report in about 2 minutes
  • 20,000+ villages in 28 states
  • Price from ₹6,900

The device

Rajat Jain started Sunfox Technologies after a close friend died of a heart attack. With four co-founders, he built Spandan, a 12-lead ECG that runs off a smartphone and gives a report in about two minutes. Prices range from ₹6,900 to ₹75,000; small clinics pay about ₹16,000–17,000.

The reach

More than 60,000 devices are deployed across 28 states and over 20,000 villages, and about 60% of users are in rural or small-town India, the company says. On Uttarakhand's Char Dham route, it screened over 13,100 pilgrims and flagged 463 critical cases. More than 400 devices are also used in Africa. Sunfox raised a $7 million Series A in September.

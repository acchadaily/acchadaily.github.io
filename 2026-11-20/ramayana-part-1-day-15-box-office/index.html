Ramayana: Part 1 earns ₹19.35 crore on day 15; its worldwide total crosses ₹1,547 crore

The Ranbir Kapoor–Yash epic's Hindi version alone has now passed ₹650 crore net in India.

  • Day 15 India net: ~₹19.35 crore

  • Worldwide gross: ~₹1,547 crore

Day 15

On its second Thursday, Ramayana: Part 1 earned about ₹19.35 crore net in India, down 16.6% from Wednesday's ₹23.2 crore, according to Sacnilk's figures. Its worldwide gross is now about ₹1,547 crore.

The Hindi version has collected about ₹657.5 crore net, making it only the fourth film to pass ₹650 crore in Hindi, The Week reports.

What's next

Nitesh Tiwari's film faces new weekend releases from today, but its second-week total of nearly ₹294 crore net suggests it will keep a big share of screens.

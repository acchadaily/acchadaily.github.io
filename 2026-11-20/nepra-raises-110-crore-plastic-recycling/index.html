Ahmedabad's NEPRA raises ₹110 crore to triple its plastic recycling to 18,000 tonnes a year

London-based PIDG led the round, its first equity bet on waste and recycling in Asia.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
3xplanned jump in plastic recycling capacity
  • Raised ₹110 crore
  • Waste processing: 900 → 1,880 tonnes/day
  • 1,500+ registered waste pickers

The round

NEPRA, which runs the 'Let's Recycle' brand, raised ₹110 crore led by PIDG through its InfraCo arm, with existing investors Aavishkaar Capital, Circulate Capital, Blue Earth Capital and Asha Impact. It will build new sorting centres and raise working capital.

The plan

NEPRA wants to raise waste processing from about 900 to 1,880 tonnes a day, and plastic recycling from 6,200 to 18,000 tonnes a year. Founded in 2006, it has facilities in Ahmedabad, Indore, Pune and Bengaluru, works with over 1,500 registered waste pickers, and helps brands meet their plastic-recycling obligations.

Mahanadi Coalfields' IPO is subscribed 0.38 times on day one; big institutions bid 1.26 times their share, retail just 0.14 times

The Coal India arm's issue, priced at ₹129–136 a share, stays open until 23 November.

In short
0.38xsubscription after day 1
  • Price band: ₹129–136; lot of 110 shares
  • Anchor book: ₹2,295 crore
  • Closes 23 Nov; listing 27 Nov

Day one

By the end of Thursday, the IPO had received bids for 0.38 times the shares on offer. Qualified institutional buyers bid 1.26 times their portion, while non-institutional investors were at 0.08 times and retail investors at 0.14 times. The company raised ₹2,295 crore from anchor investors including ADIA and LIC before the issue opened.

Key details

The price band is ₹129–136, and one lot is 110 shares, or ₹14,960 at the top end. Bidding closes on 23 November, and the shares are due to list on 27 November. IPOs often see most retail bids on the last day.

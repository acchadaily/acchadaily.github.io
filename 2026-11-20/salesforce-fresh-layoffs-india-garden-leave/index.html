Salesforce starts a fresh round of layoffs; India employees say they've been put on garden leave until February

The company has not confirmed how many people are affected. Posts on Blind describe packages worth about 21 weeks of pay in India.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Number of job cuts: not disclosed
  • India garden leave reported until 1 Feb 2027
  • US severance: up to 30 weeks' base pay

What we know

Salesforce has reportedly begun another round of job cuts, Storyboard18 and Business Insider report, citing employee posts and internal Slack chats. Managers are among those affected. The company has not confirmed the number of jobs, the teams or the countries involved.

One person who said they work at Salesforce India wrote on Blind that they were told in a meeting and will stay on garden leave until 1 February 2027.

Pay-outs

Unverified Blind posts describe India packages of about two months of garden leave plus extra weeks based on tenure, adding up to about 21 weeks. In the US, Business Insider says some staff could get up to 30 weeks of base pay. The cuts are linked to the company's push into AI and cost control.

McDonald's brings back Home Alone's Kevin, now grown up, locked inside a McDonald's for its global holiday ad

Macaulay Culkin returns as Kevin McCallister in a 60-second film where he runs the drive-thru and builds a 'Big McCallister'. A Home Alone Meal arrives on 24 November.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
60 secanchor ad, made in Canada for the world
  • Home Alone Meal from 24 November
  • Ad by Wieden+Kennedy Toronto
  • Fan event in Toronto: 20–21 November

The ad

In the new film, a storm traps a grown-up Kevin inside a McDonald's, alone again. He makes his own McFlurry, works the drive-thru and stacks a burger he calls the 'Big McCallister'. 'I made everyone disappear. Again,' he says.

It is set to Chuck Berry's 1958 song 'Run Rudolph Run', which also plays early in the 1990 film. Wieden+Kennedy Toronto made it, with Ramy Youssef and Amir Farhang directing.

The meal and merch

From 24 November, a Home Alone Meal comes with an entrée, fries, a drink and collectible socks, one pair with McDonaldland characters and one with Kevin's famous hands-on-cheeks scream.

There are also pizza-flavoured shaker fries, a nod to the film's Little Nero's pizza, and an 'Everything Sundae' McFlurry inspired by Kevin's sundae.

Why it works

The campaign runs on TV, billboards, TikTok and YouTube, with creators worldwide and a fan event in Toronto on 20–21 November. Nostalgia ads keep getting shared, and Home Alone is one of the most rewatched Christmas films.

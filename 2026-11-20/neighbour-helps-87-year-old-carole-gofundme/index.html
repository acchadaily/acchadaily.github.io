She went upstairs to complain about the noise and found an 87-year-old with Parkinson's living alone; strangers have now given over $109,000

Heidi Widmer's videos about her neighbour Carole in Los Angeles have been watched nearly 30 million times.

From annoyed to 'grandma'

Heidi Widmer was losing sleep over the thumping from the flat above hers in Culver City. Then she met the cause: Carole, 87, who has Parkinson's, lives alone and takes about 20 minutes to get down the stairs with a walker.

Widmer started taking out Carole's trash twice a week, cooking for her and sorting her medical paperwork, and got the landlord to replace her AC, carpet, stove and fridge. 'Now I got a grandma,' she says.

Where the money goes

After her posts went viral, a GoFundMe raised more than $109,000. It will pay for a stair lift so Carole can stay in her home, and for private care after her rehab from a broken wrist. Widmer's advice: 'Lead with kindness.'

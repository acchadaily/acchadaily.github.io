A Houthi missile attack damages Saudi wealth fund PIF's tower, the tallest in Riyadh; no injuries reported

It is the second strike on Riyadh's financial district this month, after an Aramco building was hit on 8 November.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Second strike on the district in November
  • No injuries reported
  • An Aramco building was damaged on 8 November

What happened

Saudi civil defence said an attack on Thursday damaged several floors of a building and nearby buildings in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District, with no injuries. A photo released by the Saudi Press Agency showed the Public Investment Fund (PIF) Tower, the capital's tallest building and a symbol of the Vision 2030 plan.

Reuters, citing three sources, said it was hit by a Houthi missile; it was not yet clear whether it was a direct hit or debris from an interception.

The context

The Houthis did not claim this specific strike, but their spokesman said they had targeted Riyadh's airport and financial centre. The attack comes as President Trump weighs another offensive against Iran, which backs the group.

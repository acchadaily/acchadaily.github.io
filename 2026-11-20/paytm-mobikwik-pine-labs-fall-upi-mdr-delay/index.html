Paytm closes 8.5% lower and MobiKwik and Pine Labs fall up to 7% on reports that the UPI merchant fee may wait until after 2027 polls

A planned 0.4% fee on some big UPI payments to merchants was meant to start in October. Now it could slip again, hurting the payment companies counting on it.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp

−8.5%Paytm's fall on BSE on Thursday
  • Proposed fee: 0.4% on UPI merchant payments above ₹2,000
  • Paytm closed at ₹1,727.70, down 8.51%
  • Original start date was 15 October 2026

What happened

Shares of payment companies fell sharply on Thursday after reports said the government may hold off on charging a merchant discount rate (MDR) on UPI until after the 2027 state elections in Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Uttarakhand, Goa and Manipur.

Paytm closed 8.51% lower at ₹1,727.70 on BSE. MobiKwik and Pine Labs fell as much as about 7% and 6% in early trade. The government has not officially confirmed a new delay.

What the fee is

The proposal is a 0.4% MDR on certain UPI payments to merchants above ₹2,000. Ordinary person-to-person UPI stays free. It was supposed to start on 15 October, then was pushed to after Diwali.

Fintechs have been waiting for it because UPI is free today and costly to run. NPCI has already notified a scheme to fund QR codes and soundboxes for small shops out of MDR money.

Why it matters to these firms

Pine Labs says more than 70% of its digital checkout payments are UPI, worth about ₹20,000 crore a month, and over 65% of that value comes from payments above ₹2,000. Razorpay has also said it is exploring the fee.

For shoppers, nothing changes yet: paying by UPI stays free.

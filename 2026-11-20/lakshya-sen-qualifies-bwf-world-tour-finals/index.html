Lakshya Sen qualifies for the BWF World Tour Finals in Hangzhou, five years after his semi-final debut

He edged Japan's Koki Watanabe by just 560 points in the race, after Kidambi Srikanth knocked Watanabe out in Hong Kong.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
560points between Sen and Watanabe in the race
  • Finals: 9–13 December, Hangzhou
  • Sen 74,180 vs Watanabe 73,620 points
  • PV Sindhu also qualified

How he got in

Sen had 74,180 points in the Race to Finals standings to Watanabe's 73,620. When Srikanth beat Watanabe in the first round of the Hong Kong Open, the last qualifying event, Sen's place was sealed. He had won back-to-back titles at the Hylo Open and Kumamoto Masters to climb into contention.

What's next

The Finals run from 9 to 13 December in Hangzhou, China. Sen joins PV Sindhu as India's second qualifier. In 2021, he reached the semi-finals on debut.

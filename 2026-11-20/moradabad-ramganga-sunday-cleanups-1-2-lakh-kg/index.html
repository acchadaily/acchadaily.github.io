An engineer's Sunday clean-ups have pulled over 1.2 lakh kg of waste out of Moradabad's Ramganga river

More than 5,000 volunteers have joined Kapil Kumar's 218-plus drives since 2018.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
1.2 lakh kgwaste removed from the Ramganga
  • Started 22 April 2018
  • 218+ clean-up drives
  • 5,000+ volunteers

Every Sunday

Kapil Kumar, a 35-year-old mechanical engineer, started the Clean Up Ram Ganga campaign at Lalbagh Ghat on Earth Day 2018 with his group Parivartan. Since June 2021, volunteers have shown up every Sunday morning. A typical drive collects 300–700 kg.

Flowers and offerings go to a compost pit, idols are kept out of the river and other waste goes to the city or to recyclers.

Changing habits

More than 5,000 people have taken part, and Parivartan has over 3,000 registered volunteers. The group pays its way by selling donated old newspapers, books and plastic. 'The greatest achievement has never been the kilograms of waste removed,' Kumar says; it is getting people to change how they dispose of things.

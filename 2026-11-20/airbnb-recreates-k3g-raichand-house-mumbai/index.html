Airbnb and Dharma recreate K3G's Raichand house in Mumbai for 25 years of the film; 180 fans can visit free from 1–6 December

Airbnb is turning the most famous mansion in Hindi cinema into a free two-hour experience. Bookings open on 23 November at noon.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp
In short
180free guest spots at the Raichand house
  • Visits: 1–6 December 2026, about 2 hours each
  • Bookings open 23 November, 12 pm IST
  • 180 spots; each booking covers 2 people

What fans get

Airbnb has teamed up with Karan Johar's Dharma Productions to rebuild the Raichand family home from Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham, which turns 25 this year. The set in Mumbai recreates the grand staircase, the living room, the study and, of course, Poo's wardrobe.

Visits run from 1 to 6 December and last about two hours. Guests get a traditional welcome into the house, an audio tour packed with film references, a tongue-twister challenge, a chance to write a note in the house and a keepsake to take home.

How to book

There are 180 spots, and each booking covers one guest plus a companion. Bookings open on 23 November at 12 pm IST on Airbnb's special page. The visit itself costs nothing, but guests pay their own way to Mumbai.

You need an active Airbnb profile with good reviews and a government ID. Airbnb says it is first come, first served, not a contest.

Why brands love nostalgia

Karan Johar, Kareena Kapoor and Hrithik Roshan are promoting the experience online, though Airbnb says none of them will be there in person. Johar called the film a world that felt 'larger than life'.

It is part of Airbnb's 'Only on Airbnb' series of one-off stays and experiences. For the brand, it is a cheap way to get millions of K3G fans talking about Airbnb in India, using a film many families still rewatch every festive season.

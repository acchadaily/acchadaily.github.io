GTA 6 breaks the all-time streaming record with 7.9 million people watching live at once, within three hours of launch

That is more than four times the old record for a game launch, set by Valorant's beta in 2020.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
7.9Mpeak live viewers across Twitch, YouTube, Kick
  • Peak: 7,886,176 viewers
  • Old record: ~1.74 million (Valorant, 2020)
  • Twitch peak: ~3.29 million

The record

Streams Charts' early estimates show GTA VI peaked at 7,886,176 concurrent viewers across Twitch, YouTube and Kick within three hours of release. The previous game-launch record was about 1.74 million for Valorant's closed beta in April 2020. Hogwarts Legacy peaked at about 1.36 million and Cyberpunk 2077 at about 1.32 million.

Twitch alone

On Twitch, GTA VI hit about 3.29 million viewers, beating the roughly 3.11 million who watched League of Legends' 2022 world final, a record that stood for nearly four years. Rockstar's game launched in stages, with New Zealand players getting in first on 18 November.

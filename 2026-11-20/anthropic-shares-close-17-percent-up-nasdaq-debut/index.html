Anthropic opens at $111.50 on Nasdaq and closes at $97.17, 17% above its IPO price, after the biggest IPO ever at $81.3 billion

The Claude maker's stock jumped as much as 34% in its first trade under the ticker 'ANT' before giving back some of the gain on a weak day for tech stocks.

accha? daily · 20 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
+17%first-day gain over the $83 IPO price
  • IPO price: $83; opening trade: $111.50; close: $97.17
  • Raised $81.3 billion, plus $26 billion in a private placement
  • Revenue Jan–Sep 2026: $33.2 billion

A record first day

Anthropic sold 980 million shares at $83 each, above its planned $70–78 range, raising $81.3 billion. It raised another $26 billion through a private placement. That makes it the largest IPO in history, ahead of SpaceX's $75 billion listing in June.

The shares started trading just before noon in New York at $111.50, about 34% above the offer price. They then slipped back and closed at $97.17, about 17% higher, on a day when the Nasdaq 100 fell more than 2%. Around 830 million shares changed hands.

The numbers behind it

At the IPO price, Anthropic was valued at about $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis. Its revenue in the first nine months of 2026 was $33.2 billion, up more than 12 times from a year earlier, according to its filings.

The India angle

India is the second-biggest market for Claude.ai, with about 7.2% of global usage. Anthropic opened a Bengaluru office in February, named Irina Ghose as India managing director and, in October, started letting Indian organisations run its models on servers inside India.

In India, the listing has restarted a debate about why the country still has no frontier AI lab of its own, Inc42 notes.

US to mediate Russia–Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi on 10–11 November, with low hopes

The working-level round is the first direct negotiation between the two sides in about nine months.

accha? daily · 9 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
10–11 NovAbu Dhabi, US-mediated
  • Dates: 10–11 November
  • Venue: Abu Dhabi
  • First direct talks in about 9 months

What is planned

US envoy Steve Witkoff said the US will mediate working-level negotiations between Ukraine and Russia in Abu Dhabi on 10 and 11 November. Witkoff and Jared Kushner are expected to lead for the US, and the agenda covers security guarantees for Ukraine, Europe's post-war security structure and reconstruction economics.

Expectations

Reuters reported this would be the first direct negotiation in about nine months. The last US-mediated talks were held in February, before the US and Israel launched a war against Iran. As of 8 November, experts cautioned that a major breakthrough was unlikely, as the two sides remain far apart on terms. Witkoff said US negotiators would also meet European partners in Brussels later in the week.

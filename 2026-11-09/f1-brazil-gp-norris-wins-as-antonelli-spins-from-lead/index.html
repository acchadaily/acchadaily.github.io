Norris wins a wet Brazilian GP after Antonelli spins from the lead; Mercedes clinch constructors' title

Verstappen was second and Hadjar third. Antonelli's lead over Verstappen is 111 points with 75 left.

The race

Lando Norris won the Brazilian Grand Prix at a rain-hit Interlagos after Kimi Antonelli spun out of the lead at Turn 1 on lap 20. Max Verstappen, who said he 'did 85%' of the race with a damaged car, was second and Isack Hadjar third. Russell, Antonelli, Piastri, Lawson, Leclerc, Hamilton and Hülkenberg completed the top 10. Leclerc was handed a five-second penalty for a collision with Lawson.

Championship picture

After 20 of 23 rounds, Antonelli has 390 points, Verstappen 279, Russell 262, Hamilton 261, Leclerc 249 and Norris 246, so Antonelli leads by 111 points with 75 available. Mercedes lead the constructors' standings on 652 points, 142 clear of Ferrari with 129 available.

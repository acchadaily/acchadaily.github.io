IIT Kharagpur student wins ₹5 lakh Viasat Space for Good India Challenge for AI to dodge space debris

Adeetya Uppal's idea helps satellites detect threatening debris and plan avoidance moves in seconds.

accha? daily · 9 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹5 lakhprize to develop the innovation
  • Winner: Adeetya Uppal, IIT Kharagpur
  • 1st prize: ₹5 lakh; pool ₹11.5 lakh
  • Runners-up: IIT Gandhinagar, IIT Roorkee

The win

Adeetya Uppal, a fourth-year aerospace dual-degree student at IIT Kharagpur from Navi Mumbai, was named winner of the 2026 Viasat: Space for Good India Challenge. His AI concept detects threatening space debris and plans satellite avoidance manoeuvres in seconds. The first prize is ₹5 lakh to develop the idea further.

The field

Shashank Kumar Singh of IIT Gandhinagar and Aryan Gupta of IIT Roorkee placed second and third. Six finalists were announced ahead of the final on 27 October, with a prize pool of ₹11.5 lakh. The challenge is a STEM innovation competition run by Viasat in partnership with India's space agency.

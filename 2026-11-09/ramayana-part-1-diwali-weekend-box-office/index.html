Ramayana: Part 1's Diwali weekend: trackers put it at ₹220–239 crore net in India, ₹500 crore worldwide

Early day-4 estimates vary widely, from about ₹37 crore to ₹56 crore net.

accha? daily · 9 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
~₹500 crfour-day worldwide gross, per DNA
  • Day 1 India net: ₹64.85 crore (Sacnilk)
  • 4-day India net: roughly ₹220–239 crore
  • 4-day worldwide: ₹500.2 crore (DNA)

The numbers so far

Sacnilk estimated Day 1 at ₹64.85 crore net in India and about ₹127.5 crore worldwide. After four days, cumulative India net is reported between about ₹220 crore and ₹239 crore, depending on the outlet, and DNA reports ₹500.20 crore worldwide for the opening weekend.

CineTalkers says its Day 4 figure of about ₹56 crore net beats the previous Diwali-day record of ₹44.50 crore held by Tiger 3 (2023). Other outlets show lower Day 4 estimates, so treat that record claim as unconfirmed.

Caveat

These are early trade estimates, not final figures. Wait for Sacnilk or Box Office India updates for confirmed totals.

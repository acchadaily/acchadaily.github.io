Mamaearth parent Honasa's Q2 profit jumps 87% to ₹73 crore as revenue grows 36%

Honasa Consumer posted a profit of about ₹73 crore for July–September, up from about ₹39 crore a year ago, though profit and revenue slipped from the June quarter.

accha? daily · 9 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹73 crQ2 FY27 profit, up 87% year on year
  • Q2 FY27 profit: ₹73.2 crore vs ₹39.23 crore
  • Revenue: about ₹731 crore, up 36%
  • Q1 FY27 profit was ₹90.45 crore

The results

Honasa Consumer, which owns Mamaearth, The Derma Co and other beauty brands, reported a consolidated net profit of about ₹73.2 crore for the quarter ended 30 September 2026. A year earlier it made ₹39.23 crore, which puts the rise at roughly 87%.

Revenue from operations rose about 36% to roughly ₹731 crore, against ₹538 crore in Q2 FY26. The company said its focus categories grew more than 40%.

The caveat

The numbers are lower than the previous quarter. In Q1 FY27 (April–June) Honasa had posted a profit of ₹90.45 crore, more than double the year before, so both profit and revenue fell sequentially in Q2.

Why it matters

Honasa has been one of the most-watched listed D2C brands since its 2023 IPO, because it shows whether online-first beauty labels can keep growing while staying profitable. A 36% revenue jump with a bigger profit is a good sign, but the sequential dip means investors will watch whether the Q1 level can be matched in the festive quarter.

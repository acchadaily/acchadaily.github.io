UN report: 252 million hectares of habitat are being actively restored across 149 countries

The Green Climate Fund also had a record year, allocating $3.3 billion to developing countries.

accha? daily · 9 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
252 M haunder active restoration
  • Area under restoration: 252M hectares
  • Countries: 149
  • Investment needed: $64B → $296B a year by 2030

The numbers

A UN Environment Programme report estimates that 252 million hectares of land, marine and freshwater habitat in 149 countries are under active restoration, supporting over a million green jobs. The Green Climate Fund reported a record year, allocating $3.3 billion to climate projects in developing countries.

The gap

The report says restoration investment would need to rise from $64 billion to $296 billion a year by 2030, so the progress is real but far from enough.

Man Utd come from two down twice to draw 4-4 with Aston Villa as Dorgu levels in the 96th minute

United are still in the relegation zone on 8 points from 10 games.

accha? daily · 9 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
4-4Old Trafford, 8 November
  • Score: Man Utd 4-4 Aston Villa
  • Man Utd: 8 points from 10, 8 games without a win
  • Villa: first 4-goal non-win since Dec 2007

The match

Manchester United drew 4-4 with Aston Villa at Old Trafford after trailing 0-2 and 2-4. Villa's early goals gave United their fastest-ever Premier League 2-0 deficit. Bruno Fernandes, Youri Tielemans, Joshua Zirkzee and Patrick Dorgu scored for United, with Zirkzee and Dorgu both scoring in stoppage time. Alysson, Nicolas Jackson, John McGinn (penalty) and Leon Goretzka scored for Villa. Sky Sports puts Dorgu's equaliser in the 96th minute.

The table

United have one win, five draws and four losses and sit in the relegation zone on 8 points. It equals their worst-ever Premier League run without a win, at eight games. Opta notes Villa scored four and failed to win for the first time since December 2007.

Coal India's Mahanadi Coalfields opens its ₹8,981 crore IPO today at ₹129–136 a share; one lot costs ₹14,960

MCL at a glance

Mahanadi Coalfields, a Coal India subsidiary, is open for bids from 19 to 23 November. The price band is ₹129–136 and retail investors must apply for at least 110 shares, or ₹14,960 at the top of the band.

The entire issue is an offer for sale, with Coal India selling up to 66.18 crore shares. Allotment is likely on 25 November and listing on BSE and NSE on 27 November.

Also open: Crystal Crop

Agrochemicals maker Crystal Crop Protection's ₹455 crore IPO, priced at ₹259–272, closes on 20 November. It was subscribed about 1.36 times by the end of day 1, with institutional buyers bidding 2.07 times their quota, according to Groww data. Listing is expected on 26 November.

As always, read the offer documents and don't invest based on grey-market chatter.

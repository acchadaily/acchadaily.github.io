Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen today for their first three-way talks, with nuclear arms and the Iran war on the agenda

Xi broke with protocol to greet Trump on the tarmac at the APEC summit. Few expect a concrete deal.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • First Trump–Xi–Putin three-way meeting
  • Held on the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen
  • Agenda: nuclear arms, Iran war, trade

The setup

US President Donald Trump landed in Shenzhen on Wednesday for the APEC summit and was met on the tarmac by Chinese President Xi Jinping, a rare gesture. The three-way meeting with Russia's Vladimir Putin is set for Thursday, with separate Trump–Xi talks expected to focus on trade.

Nuclear arms control and the Iran war are expected to top the agenda. Putin said he sees considerable potential benefits, while US officials have said they don't expect a deal.

Who's watching

Ukraine has asked that no decisions about it be made without its participation. Trump has said that talking to both leaders at once is an advantage because they work so closely together.

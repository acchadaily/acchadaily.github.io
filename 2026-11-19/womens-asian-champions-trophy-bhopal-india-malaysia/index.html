Women's Asian Champions Trophy starts in Bhopal tonight: defending champions India face Malaysia at 7 pm, with free entry

It is India's first tournament since winning gold at the Asian Games in Japan last month.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
6teams in Bhopal, 19–28 November
  • Dates: 19–28 November
  • Venue: Nathu Barkheda, Bhopal
  • Free entry for all matches

The tournament

Six teams, India, Japan, China, Malaysia, Thailand and South Korea, play at Bhopal's new hockey stadium at the International Sports Complex in Nathu Barkheda from 19 to 28 November. The bronze medal match and final are on 28 November, with rest days on 20, 23 and 26 November.

Hockey India has made entry free for all matches.

India's challenge

India won the last edition in Rajgir in 2024 and arrive as Asian Games champions. They open against Malaysia tonight after the opening ceremony.

Geopolitics

Iran fires drones at a US air base in Kuwait, its first attack on Kuwait since September, after a US strike on Qeshm Island

Kuwait says its air defences stopped the drones and calls the attacks 'criminal'. Qatar is holding fresh talks with Iran.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Target: Ali Al Salem Air Base, Kuwait
  • Followed a US strike on Qeshm Island
  • Qatar–Iran talks held on Tuesday

What happened

Iran's army said it targeted Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait, which hosts US forces, with drones early on 18 November. Kuwait's army said it intercepted the attack and did not report damage. Iran also fired at targets in Bahrain.

The attack came after US jets struck an Iranian missile launch site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday evening, which the US said was a response to attacks on its personnel.

Diplomacy and oil

Qatari and Iranian officials met on Tuesday for talks aimed at 'narrowing the gap' between proposals, Qatar's spokesman Majed al-Ansari said. Kuwait's foreign ministry condemned the repeated attacks.

Oil markets have stayed fairly calm: Brent crude traded in the low $90s a barrel this week, well below its 30 October peak of about $117.

US homelessness falls about 6% in a year, the biggest single-year drop since 2007

Official and independent counts both show tens of thousands fewer people without a home, the second yearly fall in a row.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
-6.7%drop in US homelessness (Alliance estimate)
  • About 49,000 fewer homeless people
  • Veteran homelessness: down nearly 8%
  • Second yearly fall in a row

The numbers

The National Alliance to End Homelessness estimates about 693,000 people were homeless on a single night in 2026, roughly 49,000 fewer than a year before, a 6.7% fall. HUD's own 2026 count found 698,425 people, a 6.3% drop.

Sheltered homelessness fell 7.6% and homelessness among veterans fell nearly 8%, the Alliance said.

Why it matters

It is the second straight yearly decline and the biggest since 2007. Numbers are still above pre-Covid levels, and chronic homelessness rose about 1%, but advocates credit local housing programmes and front-line workers for the progress.

Bengaluru's Humyn Labs raises $47 million to record everyday human work for training robots

Started this year by ex-Nazara CEO Manish Agarwal and Ishank Gupta, it says it already has over $50 million in annual revenue.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp

$47MSeries A, equity plus debt
  • $26M equity + $21M debt
  • Data collected in 20+ countries
  • Claimed revenue: $50M+ annualised

The round

Humyn Labs raised $26 million in equity led by Accel, with Prosus Ventures, Bajaj Finance's Finserv Intelligence and Jump Trading among others, plus $21 million in venture debt led by Lighthouse Canton and Trifecta Capital.

What it does

The startup films people doing everyday tasks in factories, warehouses, farms and homes, cleans up that data and sells it to AI labs and robotics companies that need real-world examples to train robots. It runs data collection in more than 20 countries and claims over $50 million in annualised revenue within six months.

It will expand in Brazil, South Korea and India, and set up teams in San Francisco, London and Seoul.

The 'Dhoni discount': CSK's sponsorship value could fall 10–15% after his IPL exit, say marketers, as 17 brands post farewell tributes

From Zomato to Netflix, brands lined up to say goodbye to MS Dhoni, while ad experts debate what Chennai Super Kings loses without him on the field.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
10–15%possible hit to CSK's sponsorship value
  • CSK sponsorship income FY25: ₹116.35 crore
  • 21 brand partners in the 2025 season
  • 17 brands posted Dhoni farewells

What marketers expect

Parle Products CMO Mayank Shah told Storyboard18 he expects CSK's brand valuation to drop 10–15% after Dhoni's retirement as a player, with viewership of CSK matches falling by less than 10%. A former FMCG marketer said sponsorship rate cards could also dip 10–15%.

Not everyone agrees. Baseline Ventures' Ramakrishnan Ram does not expect a big hit, pointing to a gradual handover with Ruturaj Gaikwad as captain and Dhoni staying on as Team Mentor.

What's at stake

CSK earned ₹116.35 crore from sponsorships in FY2024-25, up 7.2% from ₹108.52 crore, and had 21 brand partners in the 2025 season, including Etihad Airways, FedEx and Gulf Oil. A 2026 Houlihan Lokey study valued the CSK brand at $244 million; Kroll valued Dhoni's personal brand at $115.3 million in 2025.

The IPL 2027 mini-auction is on 16 December.

The tribute wave

Social Samosa counted 17 brands posting farewells. Zomato asked 'why does goodbye have 7 letters', Netflix India called him 'GOAT for a reason', Prime Video said 'game recognises game' and JioSaavn said no song makes this goodbye easier. Swiggy, Flipkart, Domino's, KitKat and BookMyShow also joined in.

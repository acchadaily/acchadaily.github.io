Siblings in China build a 430-step 'sky ladder' up a mountain so their 86-year-old mother can visit her old home

The family spent three months hauling stones and cement, and the new path now helps hikers and herb gatherers too.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
430steps built by one family
  • Mother's age: 86
  • Built August–October 2026
  • Mother's contribution: 50,000 yuan

A gift for Mum

Huang Zhulian, 58, and her siblings in Zhejiang province built the stairway for their mother, Mrs Chen, who grew up on the mountain and never settled into town life. The old path she used each April for the Qingming festival had become overgrown and full of snakes.

Brothers and cousins carried stones and mixed cement, other relatives cooked and cleared rubble, and a grandson used a heavy-lift drone to move stones. Some sections are fixed to a sheer cliff.

Worth it

Work ran from August to October. Mrs Chen put in 50,000 yuan (about $7,500) from her savings. Huang says she lost weight, got tanned and lost hair, but has no regrets.

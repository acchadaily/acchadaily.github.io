Ramayana: Part 1 reaches ₹844 crore net in India in 14 days, adding ₹23.2 crore on its second Wednesday

Worldwide earnings are now around ₹1,500 crore, according to trade trackers.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹844 CrIndia net in 14 days
  • Day 14: about ₹23.2 crore net
  • India net: ₹844.4 crore
  • Overseas gross: about ₹510 crore

The numbers

Ranbir Kapoor and Yash's Ramayana: Part 1 earned about ₹23.2 crore net on day 14, down about 19% from day 13, taking its India net total to ₹844.4 crore, according to Sacnilk. Overseas gross is about ₹510 crore.

DNA India reports the worldwide gross has crossed ₹1,500 crore; other estimates put it just under. Either way, it is one of the biggest Indian releases ever and is still adding ₹20 crore-plus on weekdays.

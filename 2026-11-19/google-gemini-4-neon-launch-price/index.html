Google launches Gemini 4 Neon at $3 per million input tokens, its fourth new Gemini model in a month

Neon is live for paid AI Pro and Ultra subscribers and on the API, priced at twice the cheaper Gemini 4 Hydrogen.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$3per million input tokens
  • Output: $15 per million tokens
  • Cached input: $0.15 per million tokens
  • Available to AI Pro and Ultra users

What's new

Google released Gemini 4 Neon on 18 November. Developers pay $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, falling to $0.15 per million for cached input. There is no free API tier. In the Gemini app, it is available to AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

It is the fourth Gemini model in about a month, and costs twice as much as Gemini 4 Hydrogen ($1.50/$7.50).

The fine print

Google says Neon brings 'Carbon quality at Flash speeds' and claims a lower cost per task than Carbon. Independent testing data is mixed so far, so the real-world value will depend on the job.

The SS United States, once the world's fastest ocean liner, is sunk off Florida to become the world's largest artificial reef

The 1952 record-breaker now rests about 180 feet down off Destin, where it will shelter fish and draw divers.

A new life underwater

The SS United States was sunk on 11 November about 22–25 miles off Destin, Florida, after months of cleaning to remove harmful materials. It is now the world's largest artificial reef, beating the aircraft carrier USS Oriskany, sunk off Pensacola in 2006.

Okaloosa County bought the liner in 2024 for $1 million; the reef project is budgeted at $13.5 million.

A record-holder

In 1952 the ship crossed the Atlantic in 3 days, 10 hours and 40 minutes, still the fastest by a passenger liner. Instead of being scrapped, it will now support corals, fish and a dive-tourism economy.

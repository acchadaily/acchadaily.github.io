Palo Alto Networks buys AI startup Applied Compute for $4.5 billion in stock

Applied Compute helps companies train and run their own AI models; the cybersecurity giant wants security built in from the start.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$4.5Ball-stock deal
  • Deal value: $4.5 billion (all stock)
  • Expected close: Palo Alto's fiscal Q3 2027
  • Earlier deal: Chronosphere, $3.35 billion

The deal

Palo Alto Networks said in a filing that it will buy Applied Compute in an all-stock deal valued at $4.5 billion. It expects to close in its fiscal third quarter of 2027, subject to regulatory approval.

Applied Compute co-founder and CEO Yash Patil said the startup was built to make it easy for any company to train, serve and improve its own AI models.

Why it matters

Big security companies are buying AI firms as businesses roll out AI agents that need guarding. Palo Alto bought observability firm Chronosphere for $3.35 billion a year ago.

Boba Bhai raises $18 million to grow from 120 to as many as 300 outlets; its revenue jumped 145% to about ₹70 crore in FY26

The bubble tea chain, founded in 2023, plans to open in Kolkata, Ahmedabad and Chandigarh within three to six months.

The round

Boba Bhai has raised $18 million (about ₹168 crore) in a Series B round led by US-based Left Lane Capital, with BIG Capital and existing backer Titan Capital joining. Most of it is fresh money, with a small part going to earlier investors. Its Series A, about ₹40 crore, came only in March.

How big it is

Founded by Dhruv Kohli in 2023, Boba Bhai runs 120 outlets and serves about 3.5 lakh orders a month. Bengaluru is its biggest market, with Mumbai and Karnal also doing well, and it is in cities like Lucknow, Jaipur and Kanpur.

Net revenue rose 145% to about ₹70 crore in FY26 from ₹28 crore, while the loss widened 32% to ₹12.5 crore. The company says it already beat its full-year FY26 revenue in the first half of FY27 and now makes about ₹12.5 crore a month.

What's next

The plan is 250–300 outlets in the next 12 months and 1,000 in the long run, with Kolkata, Ahmedabad and Chandigarh next. It is testing a second brand, Oreco Mexican Kitchen, in Bengaluru, and selling Korean chips on Blinkit in five cities.

Quick commerce already brings about 1,500 orders a day, and online orders make up about 60% of revenue.

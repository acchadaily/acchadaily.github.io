Antarctic blue whales have grown from about 418 to 4,018 since illegal whaling ended in 1973

Researchers led by Zoe Rand of the University of Washington combined old whaling catch records, photo-identification of individual whales and decades of ship surveys across the Southern Ocean. They estimate the population grew from about 418 animals in 1973 to 4,018, an average of about 4.5% a year.

A brain-only CAR-T therapy from UCSF wiped out a deadly childhood brain cancer in every mouse tested; human trials could start in April 2027

Scientists at the University of California, San Francisco engineered immune cells, called B-SYNC CAR-T, that switch on only inside the brain. Delivered directly into the brain, they eliminated diffuse midline glioma in every mouse tested across two models, while standard CAR-T cells could not control the tumours.

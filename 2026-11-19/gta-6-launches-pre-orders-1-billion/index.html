GTA 6 is finally out on PS5 and Xbox after a 13-year wait; pre-orders topped $1 billion, Sensor Tower estimates

Rockstar's game went live at midnight local time around the world, starting in New Zealand. It is not on PC yet.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$1B+estimated pre-order sales
  • Out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; no PC yet
  • India price: ₹5,999 (Standard Edition)
  • GTA V's launch: $815.7 million (2013)

It's here

Grand Theft Auto VI went on sale at midnight local time on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, rolling out country by country from New Zealand. In India it unlocked at midnight, with the Standard Edition priced at ₹5,999. There is no PC version at launch.

The game returns to Vice City, the series' take on Miami, 13 years after GTA V.

How big a launch

App and game tracker Sensor Tower estimates pre-orders passed $1 billion and about 10.3 million copies after returns, with PlayStation making up about 75% of sales. These are estimates; Rockstar's owner Take-Two has not shared figures, though its CEO called pre-orders 'unprecedented' in August.

For comparison, Guinness World Records lists GTA V's launch-day sales at $815.7 million in 2013.

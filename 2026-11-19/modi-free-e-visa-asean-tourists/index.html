India

Modi proposes free e-visas for tourists from ASEAN countries, one of nine ideas he put forward at the India–ASEAN summit in Manila

Travellers would still apply online, but the fee would be waived. No start date has been announced.

accha? daily · 19 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
9initiatives proposed to ASEAN
  • Summit: 23rd ASEAN–India, Manila, 17 November
  • E-visa fee waiver proposed, not visa-free entry
  • No launch date yet

The proposal

At the 23rd ASEAN–India Summit in Manila on 17 November, PM Narendra Modi proposed free e-tourist visas for visitors from ASEAN member countries. Visitors would still need to apply through India's e-visa portal; only the fee would go.

India already offers a free 30-day e-tourist visa to Thai nationals and announced one for Filipinos in 2025.

The rest of the package

The visa offer was one of nine ideas, including a maritime cooperation plan, nuclear energy exchanges, youth programmes and a joint cyber-forensics training programme. ASEAN countries are among India's fastest-growing tourism and trade partners.

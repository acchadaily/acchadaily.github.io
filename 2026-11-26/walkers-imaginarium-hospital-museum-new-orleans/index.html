A dying 9-year-old's wish comes true: a 15,000 sq ft children's museum opens inside a New Orleans hospital

Walker's Imaginarium, built only with donations, gives young patients a place to play, dance and even golf between treatments.

Walker's wish

Walker Beery was diagnosed with brain cancer in 2019 and spent about two years in treatment at the children's hospital in New Orleans. He wished for a place to escape the hospital rooms. Before he died, his family started a charity, Kids Join The Fight, to make it happen.

On 17 November, after five years of planning and building, Walker's Imaginarium opened at Manning Family Children's hospital. It was paid for entirely by donations.

What's inside

The museum has Louisiana-themed rooms, like a bayou with a pretend airboat, a Mardi Gras parade, a dance floor and a swing that sets off butterfly lights. Outside there is a nine-hole mini-golf course.

A classroom helps children who miss a lot of school. It is open every day for patients, and it is the first hospital to join the Association of Children's Museums.

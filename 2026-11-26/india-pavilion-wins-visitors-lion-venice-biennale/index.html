India's pavilion wins the Visitors' Lion at the Venice Art Biennale, on its first return in seven years

Visitors voted India's show 'Geographies of Distance: Remembering Home' the best national pavilion. PM Modi called it 'a proud moment'.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
5Indian artists in the winning pavilion
  • 61st Venice Art Biennale, 9 May–22 Nov 2026
  • India's first art pavilion since 2019
  • Winner chosen by public vote

The win

La Biennale di Venezia announced on Wednesday that India won the Visitors' Lion for Best National Participation at the 61st art exhibition, which ran from 9 May to 22 November. The prize was decided by a public vote among people who visited both main venues, the Giardini and the Arsenale.

American artist Nick Cave won the Visitors' Lion for best participant.

What was on show

The India Pavilion, in Venice's historic Arsenale, was curated by Amin Jaffer and built around the idea of home and memory. It featured five artists: Alwar Balasubramaniam, Sumakshi Singh, Ranjani Shettar, Asim Waqif and Skarma Sonam Tashi, many working with thread, bamboo and earth.

It was India's first pavilion at the art Biennale since 2019. Culture Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat said the award shows how traditional forms can find new expression.

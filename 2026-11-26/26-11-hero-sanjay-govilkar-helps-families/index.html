Shot while capturing Kasab alive on 26/11, retired cop Sanjay Govilkar now helps grieving families in 21 villages

Eighteen years after the Mumbai attacks, the officer who took a bullet at Girgaum Chowpatty spends his time helping others through loss and legal trouble.

That night

On 26 November 2008, Sanjay Govilkar was part of the police team at Girgaum Chowpatty that caught terrorist Ajmal Kasab. He was shot in the abdomen. He also stopped a colleague from shooting Kasab, saying he was more useful alive as a witness.

He spent five to six days in hospital and was given counselling by a team of doctors. He retired from the police in July 2026.

A life of helping

With friends, he runs Jeevarang, a group that gives cash or other help to families who have lost a loved one in 21 villages of Maharashtra, including with last rites.

He has also started a legal advisory firm to guide people stuck in tricky situations, like fraud victims, and has written five books on health and relationships.

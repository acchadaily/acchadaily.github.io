Bayern's unbeaten run ends in Lille

Bayern Munich lost for the first time this season on Wednesday night, beaten 2-1 by Lille in the Champions League. Bayern were without Harry Kane, Michael Olise and Manuel Neuer.

Dayot Upamecano gave the German side an early lead with a header. Lille equalised through Japanese striker Ayase Ueda in the 62nd minute, and a few minutes later Bayern midfielder Aleksandar Pavlović turned the ball into his own net. It was Lille's first win in this season's league phase.

Liverpool cruise, United held

Liverpool bounced back from their loss to Fenerbahçe with a 3-0 win at Club Brugge. Alexander Isak scored twice and Florian Wirtz added the other. It was Liverpool's first clean sheet in the competition this season.

Manchester United drew 2-2 at Sporting in Lisbon. Bruno Fernandes scored twice early in the second half, but Luis Suárez also struck twice for Sporting, his equaliser coming after a mistake by United goalkeeper Senne Lammens.

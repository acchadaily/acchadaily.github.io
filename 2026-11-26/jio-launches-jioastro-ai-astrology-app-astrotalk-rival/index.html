Reliance Jio launches JioAstro, an AI astrology app, to take on Astrotalk in India's $390 million digital astrology market

The app reads your birth chart and lets you chat with an AI 'astrologer' in four languages. Tech Twitter has been quick to mock it.

What JioAstro does

Jio Platforms has launched JioAstro, an AI-powered Vedic astrology service. It makes birth charts (kundali) using a standard astronomy method called Swiss Ephemeris, and gives personal readings from an AI that has been trained on Vedic astrology. Users can ask things like whether it is a good time for marriage or what their chart says about their career.

It works in English, Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati. For now it is available on a website and has a Google Play listing, though its terms describe the mobile app as an invite-only test version. Jio has not said which AI models power it, and has not shared pricing.

Going after Astrotalk

The launch puts Reliance up against Astrotalk and AstroSage. Astrotalk became a unicorn earlier this year at a $1 billion valuation and says its annual revenue run rate has crossed ₹2,500 crore. Apple removed Astrotalk from its App Store on 4 November, and the app is still not available there.

According to MarkNtel Advisors, India's digital astrology market is worth about $390 million in 2026 and could reach $2.32 billion by 2032.

The reaction

Not everyone is impressed. Many on tech Twitter mocked India's biggest telecom company for building an astrology bot while rivals abroad race to build frontier AI models. Jio says birth details are encrypted and are not used to target ads.

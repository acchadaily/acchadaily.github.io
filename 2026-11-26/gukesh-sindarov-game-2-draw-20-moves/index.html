Gukesh draws Game 2 in just 20 moves with white; World Chess Championship against Sindarov level at 1-1

The champion tried the London System, but Sindarov was well prepared and the game ended in a repetition in under two hours. Gukesh has black in Game 3 tonight.

A quick game in Geneva

D Gukesh had the white pieces in Game 2 of his World Chess Championship defence on Wednesday, but the game was over in only 20 moves. He opened with the London System, a calm setup, and his challenger Javokhir Sindarov of Uzbekistan looked ready for it.

Gukesh had to think hard several times. He spent about 25 minutes on one reply and around 30 minutes on his 16th move, falling well behind on the clock. The players then repeated moves three times, which ends the game in a draw.

Gukesh said the London was a nice, cool way to start the match, though he admitted he would have liked to win both of the first two games.

What comes next

After two draws the match is tied 1-1. It is a 14-game match, and the first player to 7.5 points wins. Each win is worth $200,000 from the $2.5 million prize fund.

Game 3 starts at 7:30 pm IST on Thursday, 26 November, with Sindarov playing white. Friday is the first rest day.

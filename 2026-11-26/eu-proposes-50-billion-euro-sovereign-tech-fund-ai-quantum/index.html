EU proposes a €50 billion fund to buy stakes in European AI and quantum companies so they stay out of US and Chinese hands

The Sovereign Tech Equity Fund comes with a draft Quantum Act that aims to make Europe a quantum leader by 2030.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
€50 bnproposed EU tech equity fund
  • Fund size: €50 billion
  • 10x the €5 billion Scaleup Europe Fund
  • EU quantum startups get ~5% of global private funding

The plan

The European Commission on 25 November proposed a €50 billion Sovereign Tech Equity Fund. It would put equity money into companies working on quantum, AI and other technologies Europe sees as key to its independence. Politico reported the EU could take stakes worth hundreds of millions of euros in firms to keep them European.

That is ten times the size of the EU's existing €5 billion Scaleup Europe Fund. EU tech chief Henna Virkkunen called €50 billion a "sufficient starting point".

The Quantum Act

The fund sits inside a draft Quantum Act unveiled the same day. It aims to make Europe a world leader in quantum technology by 2030, with shared quantum computers, help for European firms in public tenders and a push towards encryption that can resist quantum attacks.

Virkkunen said European quantum startups get only about 5% of global private funding, against 50% in the US. The plan still needs approval from EU member states and the European Parliament, and more details are due in February.

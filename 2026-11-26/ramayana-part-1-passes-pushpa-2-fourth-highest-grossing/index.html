Ramayana: Part 1 passes Pushpa 2 to become India's 4th highest-grossing film, at ₹1,755.6 crore worldwide

The Ranbir Kapoor and Yash epic made about ₹9.6 crore net in India on day 21 and now stands at ₹982.55 crore, just short of ₹1,000 crore.

Into the all-time top four

Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana: Part 1 has moved past the lifetime worldwide total of Allu Arjun's Pushpa 2: The Rule, which trade reports put at about ₹1,742 crore. After 21 days, the film stands at ₹1,755.6 crore gross worldwide, according to Sacnilk, including ₹582 crore from overseas.

That makes it the fourth highest-grossing Indian film ever. Only Dangal (about ₹2,070 crore), Dhurandhar 2 (about ₹1,813 crore) and Baahubali 2 (about ₹1,788 crore) are ahead, per DNA.

₹1,000 crore net is close

After the big Guru Nanak Jayanti holiday, collections dropped on Wednesday. The film made about ₹9.6 crore net in India on day 21, down roughly 45% from ₹17.5 crore the day before. Most of it, ₹6.75 crore, came from the Hindi version.

Its India net total is now ₹982.55 crore, so it needs under ₹18 crore more to join the ₹1,000 crore net club. Two new Hindi films arrive on Friday, which may cut into its shows.

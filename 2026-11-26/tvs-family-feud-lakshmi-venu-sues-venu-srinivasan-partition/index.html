TVS family feud goes to court: Lakshmi Venu sues father Venu Srinivasan in Madras High Court seeking her share of family assets

Backed by her mother Mallika Srinivasan, Lakshmi Venu wants the joint family wealth of her father's branch of the TVS group divided, saying talks after her 16 November notice went nowhere.

The case

Lakshmi Venu, daughter of TVS Motor chairman emeritus Venu Srinivasan, has filed a suit in the Madras High Court asking for the joint family assets of her father's branch to be split, and for her share to be given to her, news agencies reported on 25 November.

She sent her father a partition notice on 16 November. She says the reply did not deal with her claims or move towards a settlement. The suit also asks for access to documents and information about the family's assets, which she says were not shared despite repeated requests.

Who is on which side

Her mother, Mallika Srinivasan, has publicly backed her. She said daughters and sons should have equal rights in the family, and that Lakshmi's share should be no less than that of her brother, Sudarshan Venu. Reports describe the dispute as Lakshmi and Mallika on one side and Venu Srinivasan and Sudarshan Venu on the other. No statement from Venu Srinivasan or Sudarshan Venu has been reported.

What it means for TVS

Lakshmi says the case is only about who owns what, not about how any TVS company is run, and that she remains committed to the family businesses. The fight is limited to the Venu Srinivasan branch of the wider TVS family. Still, it is one of the most public family business disputes in India this year, and investors in TVS group companies will be watching closely.

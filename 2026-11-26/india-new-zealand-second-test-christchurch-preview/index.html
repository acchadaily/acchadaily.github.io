India and New Zealand play for the series in Christchurch from 3:30 am IST Friday after the 720-run Wellington draw

The second and final Test at Hagley Oval decides the series. New Zealand are without spinner Mitchell Santner and pacer Blair Tickner.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
1Test left to decide the series
  • 2nd Test starts Friday, 27 November, at 3:30 am IST
  • India made 720/9 in the drawn 1st Test
  • New Zealand miss Santner (broken hand) and Tickner (hamstring)

All to play for

After the record-breaking but drawn first Test in Wellington, India and New Zealand meet again at Hagley Oval in Christchurch for the second and final Test. Play starts at 11 am local time on Friday, which is 3:30 am in India.

The series is level, so whoever wins this match wins the series. Another draw means the trophy is shared. India also need wins to stay in the race for a World Test Championship final spot.

Team news

Captain Shubman Gill has hinted that India will keep most of the side that piled up 720 for 9 in Wellington, where KL Rahul made 136 and Rishabh Pant 128. He called leaving out Mohammed Siraj a tough decision and said players can force their way back by performing.

New Zealand are hit by injuries. Blair Tickner has a hamstring problem and Matt Fisher has come in as cover, while Mitchell Santner broke his right hand in the one-day series and is out of the tour, leaving the hosts with no specialist spinner. The Christchurch pitch is expected to help seam bowlers more than Wellington did.

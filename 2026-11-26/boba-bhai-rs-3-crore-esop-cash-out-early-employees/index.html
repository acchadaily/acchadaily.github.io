Boba Bhai pays ₹3 crore to six early employees in an ESOP cash-out, a week after its $18 million Series B

The bubble tea chain let its first staff sell a quarter of their vested stock options, about ₹50 lakh each on average, even though it is still loss-making.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp

  • About ₹50 lakh per employee on average
  • FY26 revenue ₹68 crore, up 139%; loss ₹21.8 crore
  • 120+ outlets; target 250–300 in 12 months

The payout

Bengaluru-based Boba Bhai has paid about ₹3 crore to six of its earliest employees, letting each of them sell up to 25% of their vested ESOPs. That works out to about ₹50 lakh per person. It comes about a week after the brand raised an $18 million Series B led by Left Lane Capital, with BIG Capital and Titan Capital.

Founder Dhruv Kohli said the company plans to offer similar cash-outs in future funding rounds. Few Indian startups do this as early as Series B.

The business

Started in October 2023, Boba Bhai has more than 120 outlets and sells over 3 lakh orders a month. It wants 250–300 outlets within a year. Revenue grew 139% to ₹68 crore in FY26, but its loss more than doubled to ₹21.8 crore.

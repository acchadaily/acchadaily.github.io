IT Secretary S Krishnan backs Sarvam AI, says hosting Chinese models on Token Factory doesn't break IndiaAI Mission rules

After days of criticism over its sovereign-AI image, Sarvam gets support from the government, which says it sees 'no misuse' of IndiaAI support.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
105Bparameters in Sarvam's own model on Token Factory
  • Token Factory hosts models from Google, DeepSeek, GLM and Kimi
  • Sarvam also released Sarvam-105B-1711 and a dictation API for 23 languages
  • Government plans a scheme to support AI inference

The new development

MeitY Secretary S Krishnan has defended Sarvam AI's new Token Factory service, saying that offering foreign AI models does not go against the IndiaAI Mission, Business Standard and PTI reported on 26 November. He said AI companies "also require a revenue model" and that the ministry, after taking feedback, does not see any misuse of the support Sarvam gets.

What the row was about

Token Factory lets businesses use Sarvam's own 105-billion-parameter model as well as open models from Google, DeepSeek, GLM and Kimi, all running on GPUs in India. Critics asked why a company that calls itself a champion of sovereign Indian AI was hosting Chinese models. Co-founder Pratyush Kumar had already hit back, saying the service runs on privately bought GPUs, not subsidised ones.

Krishnan also said the government plans a scheme to support AI inference and will back Sarvam's work towards a trillion-parameter model.

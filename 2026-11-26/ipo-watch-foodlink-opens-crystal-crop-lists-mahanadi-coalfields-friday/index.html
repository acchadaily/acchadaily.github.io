IPO watch: Foodlink F&B opens at ₹181–191, Crystal Crop lists today, and Mahanadi Coalfields debuts Friday with grey-market premium at zero

A busy week for new issues: one ₹361 crore IPO opens Thursday, Integris Medtech follows on Friday, and Coal India's arm lists after a lukewarm response.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹0Mahanadi Coalfields GMP on 25 Nov
  • Foodlink F&B: ₹360.76 crore, ₹181–191 band
  • Integris Medtech: ₹600 crore fresh issue
  • Mahanadi Coalfields lists 27 Nov at ₹136 issue price

Opening now

Foodlink F&B Holdings, a luxury catering, banquet and restaurant company, opens its ₹360.76 crore IPO on Thursday, 26 November, and closes on 30 November. The price band is ₹181–191 and one lot is 78 shares, or ₹14,898 at the top price. Money from the fresh issue will fund a new central kitchen, two banquets and four restaurants. Goodreturns put its grey-market premium at ₹27 on 25 November. Tentative listing is 3 December.

Medical devices maker Integris Medtech, backed by Everstone, opens on 27 November with a ₹600 crore fresh issue plus an offer for sale of about 1.08 crore shares. It closes 1 December.

Listings

Crystal Crop Protection, which sold shares at ₹259–272 and was subscribed about 17 times, lists on NSE and BSE on Thursday.

Mahanadi Coalfields, Coal India's subsidiary, lists on Friday, 27 November. Its ₹8,981 crore offer for sale was priced at ₹129–136 and was subscribed 1.52 times. Its grey-market premium had fallen to ₹0 by 25 November from a high of ₹25, hinting at a flat start. GMP is unofficial and can change quickly.

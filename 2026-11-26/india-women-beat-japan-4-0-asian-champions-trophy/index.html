India's women blank Japan 4-0 to win all 5 group games at the Asian Champions Trophy and rise to world No. 4

Deepika scored twice as the defending champions finished top of the table in Bhopal. India now face Malaysia in the semi-finals.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
5/5group games won by India
  • India beat Japan 4-0 on 25 November
  • Deepika scored 2 goals, both from penalty corners
  • India rise to No. 4 in the FIH rankings, their best ever

Another clean win in Bhopal

India's women stayed perfect at the Asian Champions Trophy on Wednesday night, beating Japan 4-0 in their last group match in Bhopal. That makes it five wins from five games and 15 points, putting the hosts and defending champions on top of the table.

Lalremsiami opened the scoring in the 12th minute. Deepika then turned a penalty corner into a goal in the 16th minute, and Neha made it 3-0 before half-time by pouncing on a rebound from another penalty corner. Deepika added her second, again from a penalty corner, in the 48th minute.

Best-ever world ranking

The win came a day after Deepika's hat-trick in the 6-2 rout of China, and it has lifted India to No. 4 in the FIH world rankings, up from fifth. It is the highest the Indian women's team has ever been ranked.

India face Malaysia in the semi-finals, while Japan play China in the other semi. The final is on Saturday, 28 November. India are chasing a third straight title and a record fourth overall.

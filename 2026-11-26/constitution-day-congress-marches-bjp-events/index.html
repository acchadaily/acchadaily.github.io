Constitution Day: Congress holds 'Samvidhan Raksha' marches in every state over voter lists, as BJP runs its own events

India marks 77 years since the Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949. Both big parties are using the day to make their political case.

The official day

Constitution Day, or Samvidhan Divas, marks the day the Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution in 1949. It has been observed since 2015. This year the government is running an online reading of the Preamble on MyGov in 23 languages, along with quizzes and events in schools and offices.

Congress and BJP

The Congress said every state unit will hold a 'Samvidhan Raksha March' today, starting at statues of Ambedkar or Gandhi, with party chief Mallikarjun Kharge marching in Lucknow. The party links the marches to its 'vote chori' charge about the revision of voter lists. The Election Commission has rejected those claims.

The BJP has lined up its own Constitution Day programmes led by senior leaders. On Wednesday, Defence Minister Rajnath Singh accused the Congress of undermining democracy and criticised its 'Vote Chor, Gaddi Chor' slogan.

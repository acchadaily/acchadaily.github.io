Sensex jumps 1,502 points to 83,938 and Nifty gains 1.85% as foreign buying returns; rupee hits its best level this year

Markets won back all of Monday's crash in one session on Wednesday, led by private banks and FMCG stocks, while the rupee rose 69 paise to about 92.16 a dollar.

A full recovery

After Monday's 1,484-point fall and a market holiday on Tuesday, Indian shares bounced back on Wednesday, 25 November. The Sensex rose 1,502.29 points, or 1.82%, to close at 83,938.28. The Nifty 50 added 469.60 points, or 1.85%, to 25,819.25.

The gains were mostly in big companies. The Nifty Midcap 100 rose only 0.30%, while the Nifty Smallcap 100 slipped 0.44%.

Who led the rally

Private banks did the heavy lifting, with the Nifty Private Bank index up 2.21% and Nifty Bank up 1.79%. HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and Reliance were among the top Sensex gainers. FMCG stocks such as Hindustan Unilever, Tata Consumer and Nestle also rallied as palm oil and crude prices eased, and Nifty IT rose 1.12%.

Metal stocks fell, with Nifty Metal down 2.04%, and media dropped 4.44%. Tata Steel, Hindalco and JSW Steel were among the Nifty's biggest losers.

Why investors came back

Foreign investors kept buying. Religare Broking's Ajit Mishra said they have put more than $14 billion into Indian shares so far in November. A softer oil price during Indian trading hours also helped.

Geojit's Vinod Nair said bulls returned "with renewed vigour" but flagged that hawkish US Fed comments have raised bets on a December rate hike there.

Rupee at its strongest this year

The rupee rose 69 paise to a provisional 92.16 against the US dollar, its best level of 2026 so far, helped by the stock rally, foreign inflows and RBI steps to improve dollar supply. Mirae Asset Sharekhan's Anuj Choudhary expects it to trade between 91.95 and 92.55.

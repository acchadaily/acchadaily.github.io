Hackers break into an unclassified intelligence system shared by the CIA and the US spy chief's office

Officials say classified networks were not touched. It is not yet known who did it or whether any data was taken.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
3US spy agencies reported affected
  • Breach first reported on 24 Nov
  • Classified systems not reached, officials say
  • Attacker not yet identified

What we know

Hackers breached an unclassified computer system used by the CIA and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) to analyse public and commercially available information, The New York Times first reported. The National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency was also affected, according to the Times.

An ODNI official told CNN the incident did not disrupt critical capabilities or classified systems, and one source said the problem has been fixed. The CIA declined to comment.

Why it still matters

Experts say even unclassified data can reveal what US intelligence is watching and where its blind spots are. Nextgov reported that an AI-driven hacking tool called ARTEX is believed to have been used, but this has not been officially confirmed.

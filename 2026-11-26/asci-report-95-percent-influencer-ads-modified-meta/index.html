ASCI says 95% of influencer ads it checked had to be changed; Meta accounts for 73% of all ad violations

The ad watchdog's half-yearly report finds personal care brands broke influencer rules most often, while illegal betting ads topped the overall list.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
95%of influencer ads checked by ASCI needed changes
  • 3,951 complaints about 2,308 ads reviewed
  • Betting: 590 violations; personal care: 456
  • Voluntary compliance up to 86%

Influencers under the lens

The Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) released its complaints report for April to September 2026 on 25 November. It looked at 659 influencer ads, and nearly 95% of them had to be changed. Personal care brands had the most influencer violations at 263, followed by food and drinks at 181.

ASCI flagged health claims about PCOD, anaemia, cancer risk, heart health and fast weight loss that were not properly backed up.

Digital is where the problems are

Digital media made up 98% of all ad violations. Meta alone had 1,563 ads that broke the code, or 73.4% of the total, and 90% of those were paid, sponsored ads. Illegal and offshore betting ads were the biggest category with 590 cases, despite the 2025 law banning online money games. ASCI sent those to the government's cybercrime centre.

Brands are fixing faster

Overall, ASCI reviewed 3,951 complaints about 2,308 ads, and 94% needed changes. But more brands are fixing ads without a fight: voluntary compliance rose to 86%, and the average time to settle complaints fell to 15 days.

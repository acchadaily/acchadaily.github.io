Judges in Netanyahu's 6-year corruption trial suggest dropping or settling cases; he says the trial 'must end immediately'

The judges said proving the bribery charge faces 'considerable difficulties'. Israel's attorney general says she will consider their suggestions.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
3corruption cases against Netanyahu
  • Trial began in May 2020
  • Election held on 27 October
  • Eisenkot's deadline: 6 December

In court

At a hearing in Jerusalem on Wednesday, judges recommended that prosecutors drop or change several charges against Benjamin Netanyahu. In Case 4000, the most serious, Judge Rivka Friedman-Feldman said proving bribery faced 'considerable difficulties'. For Case 1000, about luxury gifts, they suggested reaching an agreement, and they questioned whether the conduct in Case 2000 was a crime at all.

Attorney General Gali Baharav-Miara said she would weigh the recommendations and was ready for either a verdict or a plea deal. Netanyahu, the first sitting Israeli PM to stand trial, denies wrongdoing.

Politics

Netanyahu, now caretaker PM after an inconclusive 27 October election, said the trial must end. Opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot has until 6 December to try to form a government.

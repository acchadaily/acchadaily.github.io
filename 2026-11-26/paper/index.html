Flipkart CEO Kalyan Krishnamurthy steps down after 11 years as Walmart probe and IPO delays hang over the company

Flipkart has confirmed that its group CEO is leaving. Chief product and technology officer Balaji Thiagarajan will run the company until a new CEO is named in the coming weeks.

Flipkart said on Wednesday, 25 November, that group CEO Kalyan Krishnamurthy is stepping down after more than 11 years at the company. It is the biggest leadership change at India's largest homegrown e-commerce company since Walmart bought it in 2018.

Balaji Thiagarajan, Flipkart's chief product and technology officer, will lead the business for now. Flipkart said it will name a permanent successor "in the coming weeks". The company did not give a last working day, but Bloomberg reported that he will leave on 4 December.

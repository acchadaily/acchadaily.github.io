Friday clash: Emraan Hashmi's Gunmaaster G9 takes on Sooraj Barjatya's Yeh Prem Mol Liya on 27 November

An action romance and a family drama open on the same day. Early ticket sales are steady, but neither is near a record.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
2big Hindi releases this Friday
  • Both films release on Friday, 27 November
  • Gunmaaster G9: about 20,800 day-1 tickets sold by Wednesday
  • Yeh Prem Mol Liya is Sooraj Barjatya's new family film

Two very different films

Two Hindi films open in cinemas this Friday, 27 November. Gunmaaster G9 is a romantic action thriller directed by Aditya Datt, starring Emraan Hashmi, Genelia Deshmukh and Aparshakti Khurana, with music by Himesh Reshammiya. Hashmi met fans at Mumbai's Gaiety Galaxy cinema on Tuesday when bookings opened.

Yeh Prem Mol Liya marks the return of Sooraj Barjatya, the director behind family hits like Vivah. It stars Ayushmann Khurrana and Sharvari.

How bookings look

As of Wednesday afternoon, Gunmaaster G9 had sold about 20,800 tickets for its first day across 2,005 shows, worth around ₹33 lakh net, Koimoi reported. That already puts it among the top 10 advance sales for romantic films this year.

Yeh Prem Mol Liya's bookings opened only on 24 November, giving it a short runway. Trade reports call the early interest real but not record-breaking. Both films will also have to share screens with Ramayana: Part 1, which is still drawing crowds in its fourth week.

Poland plans a no-fly zone over 10 km deep along the Ukraine border, and asks NATO to shoot down Russian missiles over Ukraine

Warsaw wants the zone in place by early 2027, after Russian missiles and drones again strayed into Polish airspace this week.

accha? daily · 26 November 2026 · 1 min read

10+ kmwidth of Poland's planned no-fly zone
  • Zone planned by early 2027
  • Airspace breaches on 23 and 24 Nov
  • Poland first shot down Russian drones in Sept 2025

The plan

Poland's Deputy Defence Minister Cezary Tomczyk said the country plans a no-fly zone stretching more than 10 km from its eastern border with Ukraine, ideally in force by late December or early January. It would back a new rule letting Polish pilots identify and engage aerial targets using radar, allied intelligence, speed and altitude, rather than only by sight.

Russian missiles and drones entered Polish airspace during attacks on Ukraine on 23 and 24 November.

NATO question

Poland also raised the idea of NATO intercepting Russian missiles over Ukraine at a meeting of alliance ambassadors on 23 November. Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski said doing so at Kyiv's request could count as self-defence. Some allies backed it, but Tomczyk said NATO was not supportive and no decision has been made.

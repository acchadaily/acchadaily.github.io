O.M: Operation Mole crosses ₹100 crore worldwide in 5 days, Mammootty's first ₹100 crore hit from tickets alone

The Dhanush–Mammootty spy drama has made ₹106.25 crore worldwide in six days, per Sacnilk. Mammootty's earlier best was about ₹88 crore.

A milestone for Mammootty

Rajkumar Periasamy's O.M: Operation Mole, starring Mammootty and Dhanush with Sai Pallavi, crossed ₹100 crore worldwide within five days of its 20 November release. By day six it had reached ₹106.25 crore gross, according to Sacnilk.

The Week notes that this is Mammootty's first film to cross ₹100 crore from cinema tickets alone. Some of his earlier films were promoted as ₹100 crore hits, but those totals included TV, streaming and music rights. His previous best in theatres was Bheeshma Parvam (2022), at about ₹88 crore worldwide.

Slowing on weekdays

The film opened with about ₹18.1 crore net in India and stayed strong over its first weekend. Like most films, it has slowed on weekdays. It made about ₹4.1 crore net on Wednesday, its sixth day, taking its India net total to ₹65.55 crore. The Tamil version brings in the most money, and overseas markets have added close to ₹30 crore.

Trump, Xi and Putin will hold their first-ever three-way meeting in Shenzhen on 19 November

The Kremlin confirmed the meeting on the sidelines of APEC; nuclear weapons and the Iran war are on the agenda, Ukraine is not.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
1stever meeting of the three leaders together
  • Date: 19 November
  • Venue: APEC summit, Shenzhen
  • Topics: nuclear arms, Iran

What's confirmed

Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov told the state news agency TASS on Saturday that Vladimir Putin, Xi Jinping and Donald Trump will meet on 19 November in Shenzhen, China. The White House had announced it a day earlier.

The meeting will take place on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen, which runs on 18 and 19 November. The White House says it will be the first three-way meeting between the leaders of the US, Russia and China.

What they'll discuss

Trump wants to talk about cutting nuclear arsenals; a White House spokeswoman said he considers nuclear proliferation "very dangerous". A senior US official said he will also raise the Iran war.

US officials say Ukraine is not expected to be on the table, because peace talks with Kyiv and Moscow run on a separate track. Chip export controls are also not part of it.

The trip

Trump leaves Washington on Monday for an ASEAN summit in the Philippines, then heads to Shenzhen, where he will also hold a working lunch and meeting with Xi.

Some commentators have compared the plan to the 1945 Yalta conference. Peskov rejected that, saying any agreements reached in Shenzhen could not be imposed on other countries.

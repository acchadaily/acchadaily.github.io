OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet so its AI agents can shop and check out for you

It starts with some US Pro users, works with Visa, Mastercard and Amex, and needs your approval for every purchase.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
3card networks on board: Visa, Mastercard, Amex
  • Launch: US Pro users
  • Approval: every purchase
  • Card details: tokenised

How it works

ChatGPT Wallet lets OpenAI's AI agents, called "dots", pick products and complete checkout. Users add a card once, review the items and total, and approve each purchase with Face ID or a fingerprint.

Payments use a token rather than the real card number, so neither the agent nor the shop sees card details. Card rewards and dispute rights still apply.

Who gets it

The first rollout is limited to some US ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18+ with US-issued cards. Spending limits and pre-approved purchases are planned, with no timeline for other countries. Some retailers block AI agents; Amazon is the biggest.

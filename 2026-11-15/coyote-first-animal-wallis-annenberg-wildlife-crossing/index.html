A coyote is the first animal to cross the world's largest wildlife bridge in Los Angeles, before it even opens

Trail cameras caught it walking over 10 lanes of the 101 Freeway on the $114 million crossing.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$114Mcost of the wildlife crossing
  • Opens: 2 December
  • Lanes crossed: 10
  • Habitat restored: 13 acres

The first crossing

Trail cameras filmed a coyote walking across the unfinished Wallis Annenberg Wildlife Crossing near Agoura Hills, and a second coyote crossed days later. The bridge spans 10 lanes of the 101 Freeway.

It officially opens to wildlife on 2 December.

Why it was built

The crossing links the Simi Hills with the Santa Monica Mountains. Since 2002, at least a dozen mountain lions have been killed on the 101, and lions cut off by the highway have become inbred.

Sensex logs a third straight weekly gain, up 564 points, as Bank Nifty jumps 3.7% and SBI rallies 6.3%

Despite Friday's fall on high inflation, the Diwali-shortened week ended in the green, led by banks.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
+0.7%Sensex for the week
  • Sensex: 81,592
  • Nifty 50: 25,144
  • Bank Nifty: +3.71%

The week

The Sensex rose 564 points, or about 0.7%, over the week to close at 81,592 on Friday, while the Nifty 50 gained about 0.5% to 25,144. It was the markets' third weekly gain in a row.

Bank Nifty did much better, rising 3.71% to 61,034. SBI was the top Nifty gainer at 6.3%, while Grasim, HCL Tech and ONGC fell the most.

What's next

Foreign investors were big buyers through the four sessions. Next week, traders will watch Nvidia's results, US retail sales and Fed minutes; analysts see Nifty support around 24,750–25,000.

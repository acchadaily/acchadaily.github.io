Centre cracks down on rice hoarding: traders must report stocks every Friday, and govt doubles cheap rice sales to 50 lakh tonnes

With rice prices up about 12% in a year, the food ministry is pushing more grain into the market and watching who holds it.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read

The new rules

The food ministry said on Saturday that its online portal for declaring rice and paddy stocks is now working. Traders, wholesalers, retailers, big retail chains and millers in every state must report their stocks on it every Friday.

Units that don't register or that break stock limits can face action under the Essential Commodities Act.

More rice for the market

The Centre has also doubled its target for selling government rice through open-market e-auctions this year, from 25 lakh tonnes to 50 lakh tonnes. More rice is being given to NAFED, NCCF and Kendriya Bhandar to sell as 'Bharat Rice'.

Retail rice inflation rose to 8.25% in September from 5.89% in August, with the average retail price around ₹48 a kg. The kharif paddy area fell about 4% this year.

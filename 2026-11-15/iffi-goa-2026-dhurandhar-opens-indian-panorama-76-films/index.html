IFFI Goa 2026: Dhurandhar opens the Indian Panorama of 76 films in 18 languages; Ramayana opens a new BRICS section

The 57th film festival runs from 20 to 28 November with films from 86 countries.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
76Indian Panorama films in 18 languages
  • Dates: 20–28 November
  • Submissions: 1,900+
  • Countries: 86

The line-up

The 57th International Film Festival of India runs in Goa from 20 to 28 November under the theme "Stories Without Borders". It received more than 1,900 submissions from 137 countries and will show over 150 international films.

Dhurandhar opens the Indian Panorama, and Ramayana: Part 1 opens a new section for films from BRICS countries. Paweł Pawlikowski's Fatherland is the festival's opening film.

Also at IFFI

Spain is the country of honour and Russia the partner country. Nana Patekar's Natsamrat will be screened open-air at Miramar Beach.

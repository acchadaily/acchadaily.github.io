Noel Tata warns a Tata Sons listing could put 'profit ahead of philanthropy' as friction in the group widens

The Tata Trusts chairman spoke out as a Voltas chairman change reportedly went ahead without Chandrasekaran being told first.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
66%of Tata Sons owned by Tata Trusts
  • Trusts' stake: 66%
  • Next board meet: 26 Nov
  • New Voltas chair: Sunil D'Souza

What he said

Speaking at the HT Leadership Summit, Tata Trusts chairman Noel Tata said his worry is that listing Tata Sons, as some people are proposing, "will change the Tata way". With tens of thousands of shareholders, he said, profit could one day take precedence over philanthropy.

He said he doesn't question the group's direction, but sometimes questions "the delivery", and called for discipline in executing big bets like chips and EV batteries.

Growing friction

Business Today, citing Business Standard, reports Tata Consumer CEO Sunil D'Souza replaced Noel Tata as Voltas chairman, reportedly without N Chandrasekaran being told in advance. Tata Sons also turned down Noel's request for a recording of the September board meeting. The next Tata Sons board meeting is on 26 November.

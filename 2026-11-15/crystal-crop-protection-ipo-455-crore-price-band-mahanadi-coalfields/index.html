IPO week: Crystal Crop Protection's ₹455 crore issue opens Wednesday at ₹259–272; Coal India's Mahanadi Coalfields is lined up

It's the first mainboard IPO after a gap of more than two weeks.

Crystal Crop

Agrochemicals and seeds maker Crystal Crop Protection opens its ₹455.48 crore IPO from 18 to 20 November, with a price band of ₹259–272. It is a ₹354.77 crore fresh issue plus a ₹100.71 crore offer for sale by the promoter.

A lot is 55 shares. Fresh money will mainly repay debt. Listing is expected on 26 November.

Mahanadi Coalfields

Coal India's subsidiary Mahanadi Coalfields, India's largest coal producer, has filed its red herring prospectus for a pure offer for sale of about 10% of its shares, tentatively open from 19 to 23 November. The price band isn't out yet.

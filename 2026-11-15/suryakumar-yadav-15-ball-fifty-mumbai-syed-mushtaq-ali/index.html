Suryakumar Yadav smashes a 15-ball fifty as Mumbai chase 173 in 13.1 overs in the Syed Mushtaq Ali opener

Out of the India T20 side, the Mumbai captain answered with 60 not out off 21 balls in Kolkata.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
60*off 21 balls, with 5 sixes
  • Target: 173
  • Chased in: 13.1 overs
  • Fifty: 15 balls

The match

Mumbai beat Hyderabad by 7 wickets with 41 balls to spare at Kolkata's Jadavpur University ground. Hyderabad made 172, and Mumbai raced to 174/3.

Suryakumar came in at No. 4 with Mumbai on 90/2, reached his fifty in 15 balls and finished on 60 not out off 21, with 6 fours and 5 sixes. He was Player of the Match.

Why it matters

He was named Mumbai captain for the tournament on 9 November and hasn't played for India's T20 side since the 2026 T20 World Cup.

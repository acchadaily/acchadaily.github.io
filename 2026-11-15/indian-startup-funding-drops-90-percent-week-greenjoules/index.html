Startups

Indian startup funding falls about 90% in a week to $25–31 million; Greenjoules' ₹150 crore round is the biggest

A Diwali-shortened week made for one of the quietest funding stretches in months.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
-90%weekly startup funding, per Inc42
  • Deals (Inc42): 9
  • Biggest: Greenjoules, ₹150 crore
  • Previous week: ~$255 million

The numbers

Inc42 counts $25.2 million raised across 9 deals from 9 to 13 November, down 90.1% from $255 million the week before. Entrackr, which counts through 14 November, puts it at $31.3 million across 12 startups, down nearly 88%.

Bengaluru had the most deals, with six.

Who raised

The biggest round went to Pune clean-fuel startup Greenjoules, which raised ₹150 crore in a Series B co-led by Amazon's Smbhav Venture Fund and Climate Pledge Fund. Salad brand Olyv raised ₹57.62 crore, and Desible.ai raised ₹32 crore led by Prime Venture Partners.

Separately, Norwest closed a $4 billion fund.

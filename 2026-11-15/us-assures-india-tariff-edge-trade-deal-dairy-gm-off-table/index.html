US assures India of a tariff edge over rivals in the trade deal; dairy and GM foods stay off the table

A senior official says Washington promised India a 'comparative advantage over our competitors', but the final US tariff structure isn't settled.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
18%US tariff on Indian goods since February's interim deal
  • Interim tariff: 18% (from 50%)
  • Services: not in first tranche
  • Dairy and GM: excluded

The assurance

A senior Indian official said US negotiators have assured India of a "comparative advantage over our competitors" under the planned trade deal, while adding that India will have to see how the US tariff structure unfolds.

India keeps the right to change its own concessions if the US later puts tariffs above what it commits to. The first part of the deal will not cover services.

Red lines

India has again ruled out opening its dairy sector or allowing genetically modified imports. The February interim deal cut US tariffs on Indian goods from 50% to 18%.

The final US structure should get clearer after the US trade office publishes findings from an investigation into industrial overcapacity.

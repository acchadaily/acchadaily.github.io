Infosys Prize 2026: six researchers under 40 win $100,000 each, from quantum machines to mitochondrial disease

The winners work in India and abroad, across six fields.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp

The winners

The Infosys Science Foundation named six winners: economist Karthik Sastry (Princeton), mathematician Riddhipratim Basu (ICTS-TIFR, Bengaluru), physicist Manoj Kumar Joshi (Singapore University of Technology and Design), engineer Swarun Kumar (Carnegie Mellon), legal scholar Gautam Bhatia and life scientist Isha Jain (Gladstone Institutes and UCSF).

Each gets a gold medal, a citation and $100,000.

Why it matters

Basu solved a long-standing problem in probability, Joshi builds programmable quantum machines from trapped ions, and Jain studies mitochondrial disease. The prize has been honouring young researchers since 2009.

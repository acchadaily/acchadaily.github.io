Ramayana: Part 1 earns ₹72.90 crore on its second Saturday, a record, and reaches ₹1,228 crore worldwide in 10 days

The epic's collections jumped about 52% from Friday, according to Sacnilk.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹1,228 crworldwide gross in 10 days (Sacnilk)
  • Day 10 net: ₹72.90 crore
  • India net: ₹685.60 crore
  • Overseas: ₹408.50 crore

The numbers

According to Sacnilk, Ramayana: Part 1 made ₹72.90 crore net in India on Day 10 (Saturday), up 51.6% from ₹48.10 crore on Friday. Hindi brought in ₹53.75 crore of that.

Its 10-day total stands at ₹685.60 crore net in India and about ₹1,228 crore gross worldwide, with ₹408.50 crore from overseas.

Records

Bollywood Hungama calls it the biggest second Saturday ever for a film in India, ahead of Dhurandhar The Revenge. Advance bookings for Sunday were strong too. The film will also open the new BRICS section at IFFI Goa.

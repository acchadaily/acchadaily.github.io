Ajinkya Rahane invests in Italian-made cookware brand Ember, the latest cricketer to back a startup

The D2C brand sells ceramic and steel cookware made in Italy, and is on quick-commerce apps in four big cities.

The deal

Cricketer Ajinkya Rahane has picked up an undisclosed stake in Ember, a direct-to-consumer cookware brand founded in 2024 by Siddharth Gadodia, Himanshi Tandon and Bret Recor.

Ember sells ceramic cookware, stainless steel pans, cast-iron pots and Dutch ovens made in Italy. It sells online and on quick-commerce apps in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad, and raised a $3.2 million seed round last year.

Cricketers as investors

Rahane has also backed Chupps, Hudle, Proxgy and Oatey. He joins a growing list of cricketers putting money into startups, after Sachin Tendulkar backed Recykal and KL Rahul joined PlayBlue.

A new pink-flowered begonia has been found beside a mountain stream in Arunachal's Upper Siang

Named Begonia siangensis, it is known from just one spot so far.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
600 maltitude where it grows
  • Name: Begonia siangensis
  • Altitude: ~600 m
  • Status: Data Deficient

The find

Researchers from the Mumbai-based Thackeray Wildlife Foundation found the plant in a shaded, damp stream valley between Bomdo and Jengging during a survey. It is a creeping herb with pale pink flowers and dark green leaves.

The study was published in the Edinburgh Journal of Botany.

What's next

Because it is known from a single site, it has been provisionally listed as 'Data Deficient'. The team wants more surveys of shaded streams between 500 and 1,000 m.

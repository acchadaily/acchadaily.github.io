Raj Shamani's 'Figuring Out' becomes the world's most-subscribed YouTube podcast with 22 million subscribers

The show that started in a small room in Indore has moved past The Diary of a CEO and The Joe Rogan Experience.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp

22MYouTube subscribers, more than any other podcast
  • Started: 2021, in Indore
  • Added in 2026: about 8 million subscribers
  • Overseas share: about 22% of subscribers

What happened

Figuring Out with Raj Shamani has crossed 22 million subscribers on YouTube, making it the most-subscribed podcast channel on the platform, according to Storyboard18.

That puts the Indian show ahead of two global giants: Steven Bartlett's The Diary of a CEO, at about 21.9 million, and The Joe Rogan Experience, at about 21.2 million.

From Indore to the top

Shamani started the podcast in 2021 from a small room in Indore. The growth has sped up sharply: the channel added about 8 million subscribers in 2026 alone.

The show now pulls about 1.4 billion views a month across platforms, and roughly 22% of its YouTube subscribers live outside India, a sign that Indian long-form interviews are finding a global audience.

Why it matters

Podcasts have become one of the most powerful marketing channels in India. Founders, ministers, actors and creators now use long-form conversations to reach young audiences directly, and brands follow that attention with sponsorships.

Shamani says he wants to turn Figuring Out into a "global media institution", adding that where you come from shouldn't decide what you get to learn.

What's next

The race at the top is close: the gap to The Diary of a CEO is only around 100,000 subscribers, so expect the rivals and their fans to keep an eye on the counter.

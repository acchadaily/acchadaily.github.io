Portugal lose 4-1 to Denmark in their first game since Cristiano Ronaldo quit international football

Rasmus Højlund scored twice in Braga on a night that began with tributes to Ronaldo.

The match

Denmark beat Portugal 4-1 in the Nations League in Braga, with goals from Pierre-Emile Højbjerg (penalty), Rasmus Højlund (two) and Joachim Andersen. Bernardo Silva scored a late consolation.

Before kick-off, Portugal's players and mascots wore Ronaldo's No. 7 and fans held up a "Thank you, captain" banner. Ronaldo, 41, retired with a record 146 goals in 234 games.

Elsewhere

Portugal had already qualified for the quarter-finals. Norway beat Wales 3-1 with a late Erling Haaland penalty, while Sweden and Austria won promotion to League A.

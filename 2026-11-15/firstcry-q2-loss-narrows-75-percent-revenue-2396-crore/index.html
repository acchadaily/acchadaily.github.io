FirstCry's Q2 loss shrinks 75% to ₹12.6 crore as revenue rises 14% to ₹2,396 crore

The kids' products platform is closing in on profit, though rising raw-material costs squeezed its India margins.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
-75%drop in quarterly loss
  • Q2 loss: ₹12.6 crore
  • Revenue: ₹2,395.8 crore
  • Share price: ₹160.40

The results

Brainbees Solutions, which runs FirstCry, posted a consolidated loss of ₹12.6 crore for July–September, down from ₹50.5 crore a year ago. Operating revenue rose 14.1% to ₹2,395.8 crore.

India revenue grew 18.6% to ₹1,637.9 crore, but India adjusted EBITDA fell 10.5% to ₹112.2 crore, which the company blamed on crude-linked raw-material inflation.

The rest

The international business grew 5.8% to ₹249.3 crore, and house-of-brands GlobalBees grew 7.7% to ₹530.6 crore. The shares closed 1.84% lower at ₹160.40.

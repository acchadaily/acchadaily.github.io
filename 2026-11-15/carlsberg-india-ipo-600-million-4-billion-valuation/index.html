Carlsberg plans a $600 million India IPO at a $4 billion valuation; the brewer could list as early as January

India's second-largest brewer is lining up investor meetings, Bloomberg reports, with the parent selling a 15% stake.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read

$600MIPO size, all an offer for sale
  • Valuation: ~$4 billion
  • FY26 revenue: ₹8,709 crore
  • Market share: 21.9%

The IPO

Danish brewer Carlsberg may start investor meetings as early as next week for the IPO of Carlsberg India, according to a Bloomberg report carried by Storyboard18. It could raise up to $600 million at a valuation of about $4 billion and list as early as January.

It is entirely an offer for sale: subsidiary South Asian Breweries would sell up to 86.42 million shares, about 15% of the company. Kotak, Citi and JPMorgan are the bankers.

The business

Carlsberg India runs 7 breweries of its own plus 9 third-party plants and had a 21.9% market share across 16 key states and union territories in FY26, when revenue was ₹8,709 crore.

In the June quarter, revenue rose 21.5% to ₹1,366 crore while profit slipped 3.9% to ₹158 crore.

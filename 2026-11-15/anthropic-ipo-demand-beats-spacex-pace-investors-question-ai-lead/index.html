Anthropic's IPO draws orders faster than SpaceX's did, but some investors question how long its AI lead will last

Reuters says the $64 billion offering was about six times covered by day three, ahead of pricing on 18 November.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read

  • Pricing: 18 Nov

  • Ticker: ANT

The demand

Reuters reports Anthropic's roughly $64 billion IPO had orders for about twice the shares on offer by the end of its first day, and about six times by Thursday. SpaceX was about 2x covered after two days of its roadshow.

Pricing is expected on 18 November, with trading on Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT" set for 19 November.

The doubts

Sell-side analysts forecast 2026 revenue of $52–53 billion. One investor who passed expects 2028 revenue about 30% below bank estimates, and others asked whether Claude can stay ahead of rivals such as Google beyond coding and keep charging premium prices. Anthropic declined to comment.

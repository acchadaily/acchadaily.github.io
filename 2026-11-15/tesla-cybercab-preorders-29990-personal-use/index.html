Tesla opens Cybercab pre-orders for personal use at $29,990, plus $299 a month

Deliveries start in December, but only in a handful of Texas cities, and the car drives only inside mapped zones.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$29,990Cybercab price, plus $299 a month
  • Monthly fee: $299
  • Delivery: from Dec 2026
  • Where: Texas only for now

The offer

Tesla is letting select customers pre-order its steering-wheel-free Cybercab for personal use at $29,990, plus $299 a month to operate it. Elon Musk said the fee is "too high" and should match what Tesla charges for self-driving on its other cars.

Estimated delivery is December 2026, with buyers taking delivery in a few Texas cities.

The catch

The car only works inside geofenced areas, the same zones as Tesla's robotaxi service, and Musk said wider availability depends on regulators. Tesla plans to stop making the Cybertruck in 2027 and use that space for more Cybercabs.

'The best founders are not on Instagram': Bombay Shaving Company's Shantanu Deshpande calls himself a 'mediocre founder'

One of India's most visible founders told other founders to stop copying reel-making CEOs, and pointed to the leaders of Meesho, Groww, Ather and Lenskart instead.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Examples named: 4 founders
  • Companies: Meesho, Groww, Ather, Lenskart
  • His brand: Bombay Shaving Company

What he said

Shantanu Deshpande, founder of Bombay Shaving Company, posted a video questioning the trend of founders building personal brands on Instagram, from dance reels to lifestyle content.

He said some founders see how much he posts and assume they must do the same. He apologised if his content had given that impression and called himself a "mediocre founder".

Who he pointed to

His examples of top founders who don't need to be on Instagram: Vidit Aatrey of Meesho, Lalit Keshre of Groww, Tarun Mehta of Ather Energy and Peyush Bansal of Lenskart.

He applied the same logic to investors, saying that sounding smart online is not the same as good judgement on deals.

Why it's trending

Founder-led content has become a marketing playbook in Indian startups, with CEOs posting daily on Instagram and LinkedIn. Deshpande, who is himself one of the most active founder-creators, is now arguing that online visibility and validation don't automatically turn into business success.

The clip has reopened a familiar debate: is a founder's personal brand a cheap growth channel, or a distraction from building the company?

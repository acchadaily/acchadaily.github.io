Iran says at least three oil tankers, including a supertanker, hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz

Iran says the ships had switched off their tracking and used unauthorised routes; the US says it has cleared the main lanes of mines.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
3+tankers hit mines, according to Iran
  • Ships hit: at least 3
  • One: a supertanker
  • Brent (Fri): $95.84

What Iran says

Iran's Revolutionary Guards navy said two oil tankers caught fire after hitting a naval mine in the south of the strait, claiming both had turned off their radar and positioning systems. Iran's regular navy said a third tanker in a "prohibited zone" east of the strait also struck a mine and stopped.

Iran said one of the ships was a supertanker but did not name the vessels. Counts differ between outlets, and some reports put the number of ships hit higher.

The backdrop

Since the war with the US began in February, Iran has demanded that ships seek its permission to cross the strait. The US is blockading Iranian ports and escorting tankers, and says it has cleared the main shipping lanes of mines. Brent crude settled at $95.84 a barrel on Friday.

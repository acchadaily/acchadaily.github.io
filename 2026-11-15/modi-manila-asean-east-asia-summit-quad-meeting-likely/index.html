India

PM Modi heads to Manila on Monday for the ASEAN-India and East Asia summits; a Quad leaders' meeting is likely on 17 November

It would be the first Quad leaders' summit in about two years; a Modi–Trump meeting is being discussed but isn't confirmed.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$122BIndia–ASEAN trade, says MEA
  • Dates: 16–18 November
  • Summits: ASEAN-India, East Asia
  • Quad: likely 17 Nov

The trip

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will travel to Manila, Philippines, on 16 November for three days. He will attend the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit and the 21st East Asia Summit, returning on 18 November.

A Quad leaders' meeting of India, the US, Japan and Australia is likely in Manila on Tuesday 17 November, though India has not officially confirmed it.

What's on the table

Expected topics include freedom of navigation, attacks on merchant ships, critical minerals and supply chains. A bilateral meeting between Modi and Donald Trump has been discussed; an MEA official said it was "all up in the air" for now.

The MEA puts India's trade with ASEAN at about $122 billion.

A surprise right whale calf takes this season's births to 24, the most since 2009 for a species of about 380

Researchers spotted the unknown calf swimming beside its mother off Quebec.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
24calves this season
  • Whales left: ~380
  • Breeding females: ~70
  • Mother's calves: 6

The surprise

Researchers photographed an unknown North Atlantic right whale calf next to its mother, Silt, near Anticosti Island in Quebec. The New England Aquarium confirmed it was a new calf that had been missed in winter surveys.

That makes 24 calves this season, the most since 2009.

Why it matters

Only about 380 North Atlantic right whales are left, with about 70 breeding-age females, so every calf counts. Silt is 38 and has had six known calves.

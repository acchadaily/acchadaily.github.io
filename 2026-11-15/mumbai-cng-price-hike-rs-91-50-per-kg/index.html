Mumbai CNG goes up ₹1.50 to ₹91.50 a kg from today, the second hike this month

Mahanagar Gas blamed costlier imported gas; autos, taxis and private cars across the Mumbai region will feel it.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹91.50per kg of CNG in Mumbai from 15 Nov
  • New price: ₹91.50/kg
  • Hike: ₹1.50/kg
  • Vehicles affected: ~12.93 lakh

The hike

Mahanagar Gas Ltd (MGL) has raised the price of CNG by ₹1.50 per kg in Mumbai and nearby areas, taking it to ₹91.50 a kg from Sunday 15 November.

It comes just two weeks after a ₹1 hike on 1 November that took the price to ₹90.

Why, and who pays

MGL said it is paying more for gas during the Middle East crisis, is getting less cheap domestic gas, and has to rely more on expensive imported LNG.

About 12.93 lakh CNG vehicles in the region are affected, including roughly 4.7 lakh autos and 2 lakh taxis, according to the Times of India. Auto unions want the minimum fare raised from ₹27 to ₹29.

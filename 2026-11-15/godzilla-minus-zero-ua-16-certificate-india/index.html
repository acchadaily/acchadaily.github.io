Godzilla Minus Zero gets a U/A 16+ certificate in India despite an R rating abroad; it opens on 20 November

The censor board passed the 137-minute monster film without an adults-only rating.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
U/A 16+CBFC rating in India
  • Release: 20 November
  • Runtime: ~137 minutes
  • Abroad: R-rated

The rating

The CBFC has given Godzilla Minus Zero a U/A 16+ certificate rather than an 'A' rating, Bollywood Hungama reports, even though it carries an R rating overseas. Its runtime is about 137 minutes.

The film releases in Indian cinemas, including IMAX screens, on 20 November.

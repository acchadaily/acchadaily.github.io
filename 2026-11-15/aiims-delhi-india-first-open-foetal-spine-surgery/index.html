AIIMS Delhi performs India's first open surgery on an unborn baby's spine, at 25 weeks of pregnancy

A team of 30+ doctors and nurses repaired a severe spinal defect while the baby was still in the womb.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
6 hrssurgery, after 5+ weeks of planning
  • Team: 30+ clinicians
  • Pregnancy: 25 weeks
  • Shunt need: 40% vs 82%

What they did

Doctors at AIIMS Delhi repaired a severe form of spina bifida, where the spinal cord is exposed, in a baby at 25 weeks of pregnancy. AIIMS says it is India's first open foetal surgery of this kind.

The six-hour operation involved more than 30 specialists from obstetrics, neurosurgery, anaesthesia, plastic surgery and neonatology, after more than five weeks of planning and rehearsal.

Why it matters

Repairing the defect before birth can protect the spinal cord from further damage. In an international trial, 40% of babies operated on before birth needed a brain-fluid shunt by 12 months, against 82% of those treated after birth. Doctors stress it isn't a cure, and mother and baby will need long-term care.

Outlook outage turns into a meme fest: Zomato, KitKat, Air India, Canva and Myntra roast Microsoft

While Outlook and Teams were down for a second workday, brands raced to turn the outage into topical posts.

accha? daily · 15 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
6+big brands joined in with outage jokes
  • Brands: KitKat, Air India, Myntra, Canva, Zomato, Yahoo
  • Services hit: Outlook, Teams, Copilot
  • Format: topical social posts

The posts

KitKat played on its "Have a break" line with a mock office memo announcing an "Unscheduled Global Break" that had been "reviewed and approved".

Air India posted a view from a window seat, suggesting a "change of Outlook". Myntra told users that if their inbox wouldn't refresh, they could refresh their wardrobe instead.

Rivals had fun too

Canva posted a Microsoft-style error screen saying it was still up and running, and pushed Canva Docs as a way to open .docx files. Zomato told whoever was fixing the outage to first decide on lunch and dinner, with "no need to rush".

Yahoo joked that with work email down, it was finally time to read thousands of unread personal emails.

Why brands do this

Moment marketing is a staple for Indian brands: a big shared event, from a cricket match to a server crash, is a cheap way to get attention with a single clever post. The risk is looking like you're mocking people stuck at work, so most brands kept the tone light.

The Microsoft 365 outage hit Outlook, Teams and Copilot in dozens of countries.

Government caps trade margins on 137 anti-cancer drugs; steepest price cut is 96%

The cap of 30% on trade margins took effect on 9 November and is expected to save about ₹3,557 crore a year.

accha? daily · 10 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
96%steepest reported price cut
  • 137 medicines covered
  • Trade margin cap: 30%
  • Estimated savings: ₹3,557 crore a year

What changed

The government has capped trade margins at 30% on 137 non-scheduled anti-cancer medicines, effective 9 November. The aim is to curb excessive mark-ups by manufacturers, distributors and pharmacies.

How much will you save?

Reported price cuts range from about 20% to 70% on average, with the steepest at 96.45%. The estimated saving is about ₹3,557 crore a year, based on FY26 sales volumes. The source does not list the specific drugs, so patients should ask their pharmacist or hospital which products are covered.

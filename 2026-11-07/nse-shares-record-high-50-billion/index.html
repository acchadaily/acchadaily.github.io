NSE shares hit a record; exchange now worth over $50 billion

India's biggest stock exchange touched ₹1,953.95 a share after strong September-quarter profits.

accha? daily · 7 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$50B+NSE's market value
  • Record: ₹1,953.95
  • Q2 profit: ₹2,578 crore (+23%)
  • Market cap ~₹4.83 lakh crore

What happened

Shares of the National Stock Exchange of India rose up to 3.15% on Friday to a record ₹1,953.95, the fourth straight day of gains. That puts its market value at about ₹4.83 lakh crore, or more than $50 billion.

In the September quarter, NSE's consolidated profit rose 23% to ₹2,578 crore and total income rose 9% to ₹4,554 crore.

Operating profit (EBITDA) more than doubled to ₹3,079 crore, cash-market trading values rose 24% and listing fees jumped 70% — helped by a busy IPO season.

What brokerages say

JPMorgan said results beat its estimates and kept a Buy with a ₹2,130 target; Morgan Stanley, Jefferies and Macquarie also stayed positive. This is information, not investment advice.

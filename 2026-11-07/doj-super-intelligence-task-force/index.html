US Justice Department sets up a 'Super Intelligence' task force

The new team will go after foreign states, criminal groups and fraudsters who misuse advanced AI.

accha? daily · 7 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
  • Led by the Attorney General's office
  • Includes the FBI
  • Replaces January's AI Task Force

What happened

The US Department of Justice has created the Super Intelligence Task Force (DOJ-SITF) to coordinate criminal and civil cases against people who misuse advanced AI, including foreign governments, criminal organisations and fraudsters. It brings together the FBI and the department's antitrust, criminal, civil and national security teams.

It takes over the work of an earlier AI task force set up in January.

Why it matters

It is a sign that governments now treat AI misuse as a law-enforcement priority, not just a policy debate. Legal experts note it creates no new crime by itself; it uses existing laws.

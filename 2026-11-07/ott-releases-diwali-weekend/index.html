Diwali weekend on OTT: Haiwaan, The Vvaan and Last Man in Tower

Akshay Kumar and Saif Ali Khan, Sidharth Malhotra and Manoj Bajpayee lead this weekend's streaming picks.

accha? daily · 7 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
5big new releases this weekend
  • Haiwaan – SonyLIV
  • The Vvaan – Prime Video
  • Last Man in Tower – ZEE5

What's new

Haiwaan (SonyLIV): Priyadarshan's thriller with Akshay Kumar and Saif Ali Khan, about a visually impaired lift operator suspected of murder.

The Vvaan – Force of the Forrest (Prime Video): Sidharth Malhotra and Tamannaah Bhatia in a folklore thriller set in Central India's forests.

Last Man in Tower (ZEE5): Manoj Bajpayee as a retired teacher who refuses to sell his Mumbai flat for redevelopment, based on Aravind Adiga's novel.

Also streaming

I'm Game (JioHotstar), a Malayalam action thriller with Dulquer Salmaan set around cricket, and Under Siege (Netflix), a Spanish drama about the 1985 Bogotá Palace of Justice siege. All dropped on 6 November.

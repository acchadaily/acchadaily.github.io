Hotel brand Bloom raises $75 million led by Kedaara Capital; AI startup AidenAI raises $32 million

Two of the week's biggest rounds: Bloom wants to grow to 12,000 rooms, while AidenAI plans to train 1,000+ AI engineers in Hyderabad.

Bloom checks in big money

Bloom, a tech-led hotel brand, raised $75 million in a round led by private equity firm Kedaara Capital. It says it has grown about 30% a year for five years while staying profitable.

It expects to run more than 4,700 rooms by the end of this financial year and aims for 12,000 rooms in the next few years. Part of the money will go into AI and dynamic pricing for its in-house platform.

AidenAI's Series A

AidenAI, founded in 2023 by former Infosys executive Srinivas Kamadi, raised $32 million (about ₹297 crore) in a Series A round led by A91 Partners. Based in New Jersey with most of its team in Hyderabad, it helps companies build AI agents and update old software. It serves 30+ enterprise clients in six countries.

Also funded

Robotics startup Cosine Robots raised ₹22.36 crore led by Avaana Capital, and silver jewellery brand Zavya raised ₹22.6 crore.

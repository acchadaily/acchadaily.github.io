Mbappé scores twice as Real Madrid hit PSV for 5 to stay perfect on 15 points; Arsenal held 1-1 by Dortmund

On Champions League matchday 5, Manchester City also drew 2-2 with Napoli and Aston Villa lost 1-0 at Galatasaray.

In short
15Real Madrid's points from 5 games
  • Real Madrid 5-1 PSV, with 2 goals from Mbappé
  • Arsenal 1-1 Dortmund; Man City 2-2 Napoli
  • Galatasaray 1-0 Aston Villa

Madrid come from behind

PSV led at half-time at the Bernabéu on Tuesday, 24 November, through an Ivan Perišić penalty. Real Madrid then scored five in the second half: Federico Valverde equalised, Jude Bellingham hit one from long range, Kylian Mbappé scored twice and Vinícius Jr added the last. Madrid have won all five league-phase games.

English sides stumble

Viktor Gyökeres gave Arsenal an early lead with a stunning strike in London, but Maximilian Beier equalised for Dortmund. Manchester City twice came back to draw 2-2 with Napoli, with Rayan Cherki levelling in the 86th minute, and Aston Villa lost 1-0 in Istanbul.

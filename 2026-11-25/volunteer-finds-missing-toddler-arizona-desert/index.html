After a 7-hour night search, a volunteer finds a missing 2-year-old safe under a desert bush 2 miles from home

Jay Stewart heard a small voice answering his calls near Eloy, Arizona, and kept the shivering girl warm until help came.

  • Search involved about 75 people
  • Girl found around 12:15 am, about 2 miles away
  • Rescuer made honorary search-and-rescue posse member

Lost at sunset

Little Vella wandered out of her family's front yard near Eloy at about 5 pm on Tuesday 17 November, wearing only a nappy. Around 75 police officers, neighbours and search volunteers fanned out through the desert as the temperature dropped, helped by drones and helicopters.

A voice in the dark

Just after midnight, volunteer Jay Stewart was walking along a ditch and calling her name when he heard a reply. He found her curled up in a bush about 2 miles from home, cold but unhurt, and wrapped her in his jacket.

She was checked at hospital and was home by Wednesday. The Pinal County Sheriff's Office thanked Stewart with a certificate and made him an honorary member of its search and rescue posse.

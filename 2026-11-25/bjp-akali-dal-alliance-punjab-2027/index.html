BJP and Akali Dal reunite for the 2027 Punjab election, six years after splitting over the farm laws

Amit Shah, BJP president Nitin Nabin and Sukhbir Badal announced the tie-up in Delhi on 24 November; seat-sharing is still being worked out.

accha? daily · 25 November 2026 · 1 min read

In short
117Punjab Assembly seats at stake in 2027
  • Alliance broke in Sept 2020 after about 24 years
  • Old split: SAD 94 seats, BJP 23
  • 2022 result: SAD 3, BJP 2

The announcement

The BJP and the Shiromani Akali Dal (SAD) will fight the 2027 Punjab Assembly election together. The decision was announced at a joint press conference in New Delhi on Tuesday, Guru Nanak Jayanti, by Union Home Minister Amit Shah, BJP president Nitin Nabin and SAD chief Sukhbir Singh Badal. Shah said the Akali Dal would stay the senior partner in Punjab.

How the 117 seats will be split has not been decided. In their old arrangement, the SAD contested 94 seats and the BJP 23.

Why they split, and the reactions

The Akali Dal walked out of the NDA in September 2020 over the three farm laws, which were later repealed, ending a partnership of about 24 years. Fighting separately in 2022, the SAD won three seats and the BJP two, as AAP swept the state.

Punjab's ruling AAP called the tie-up a 'thug pact', while Congress called it opportunism. Punjab Congress chief Pargat Singh said the two parties 'have no development agenda' for the state.

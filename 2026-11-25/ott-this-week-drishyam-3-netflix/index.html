OTT this week: Drishyam 3 on Netflix on 27 November, plus Blade Runner 2099 and Spider-Man: Brand New Day

Ajay Devgn's hit thriller lands on streaming on Friday, after its theatrical run began on 2 October.

accha? daily · 25 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
27 NovDrishyam 3 on Netflix
  • Drishyam 3: Netflix, 27 November
  • Blade Runner 2099: Prime Video, 25 November
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day: Netflix, 26 November

The big ones

Drishyam: The Conclusion, the third Hindi film in Ajay Devgn's crime series with Tabu and Jaideep Ahlawat, streams on Netflix from Friday, 27 November. Reports say the Madras High Court declined to stop its OTT release.

Sci-fi series Blade Runner 2099, with Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer, arrives on Prime Video on 25 November, and Tom Holland's Spider-Man: Brand New Day is listed for Netflix on 26 November.

Also streaming

Supergirl on JioHotstar (26 Nov), the campus politics drama Dhai Chaal on JioHotstar and Hanuman Ansh on SonyLIV (both 27 Nov), and Richa Chadha's Delhi 2021 on ZEE5 (25 Nov). Dates come from streaming roundups, so check the apps.

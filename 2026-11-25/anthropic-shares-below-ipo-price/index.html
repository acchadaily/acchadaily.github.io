Anthropic shares close below their $83 IPO price, falling 6.5% to about $81.91, just days after a record debut

The Claude maker opened at $111.50 on its first day of trading last Thursday, but has fallen steadily since as tech shares cooled.

From record debut to below the issue price

Anthropic raised $81.3 billion in the biggest IPO ever and began trading on Nasdaq last Thursday, opening at $111.50. By Tuesday, 24 November, the shares had fallen another 6.5% to close at about $81.91, below the $83 price at which they were sold to investors, Axios and CNBC reported. At one point during the day the stock fell as much as 9.4%.

Why it is falling

Part of the drop is the wider market: chip stocks fell on Tuesday and the Nasdaq lost 1.5% as Nvidia's big rally faded. CNBC also noted that about half of the big investors who asked for shares in the IPO got none, and they do not seem keen to buy now.

Analysts are still mostly positive. Canaccord Genuity started coverage with a 'buy' rating and a $135 target, Wedbush has a $120 target and Oppenheimer a $150 target.

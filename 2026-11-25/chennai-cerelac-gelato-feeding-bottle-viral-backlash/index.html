A Chennai gelato shop's 'Grown-Up Cerelac' served in baby feeding bottles goes viral and draws a backlash from doctors

Crowds lined up at Gelateria Ciacco after influencer posts, but critics called the feeding-bottle gimmick tasteless.

accha? daily · 25 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short

  • Offer extended by two weeks after the crowds
  • Cafe says bottles are sterilised and not reused

The viral treat

For Children's Day on 14 November, Chennai's Gelateria Ciacco launched a 'Grown-Up Cerelac Gelato' and a cold Cerelac milk, both served in baby feeding bottles. Influencer videos pulled in such big crowds that there were reports of traffic jams on Anna Salai, and the shop extended the offer by two weeks.

The cafe says the idea came from a sick regular who couldn't eat cold ice cream and was given warm Cerelac milk instead.

The backlash

Doctors and creators questioned turning a feeding bottle into a party prop. One physician pointed out that the same public is often uncomfortable with breastfeeding, while others said brands should not use childhood symbols just for attention.

The cafe said it only wanted to serve nostalgia, that customers can pick a normal cup, and that bottles are sterilised and never reused. It plans to end the item soon and move to its Christmas menu.

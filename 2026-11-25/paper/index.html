FSSAI declares a Bisleri water batch from Goa unsafe and clears prosecution; Bisleri says the ban covers just one batch

A retest found traces of chemicals called PAHs in one batch of Bisleri bottled water. Bisleri says the batch was made in September 2025 and is no longer on shelves.

Goa's food regulator first flagged a Bisleri water sample for bromate, a chemical that can form when water is disinfected. The first lab test showed 0.012 mg per litre, just above the 0.01 mg limit.

Bisleri appealed, and the sample went to the National Food Laboratory in Navi Mumbai. That lab found bromate was within limits, but it detected small amounts of polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), a group of chemicals linked to burning and fuel.

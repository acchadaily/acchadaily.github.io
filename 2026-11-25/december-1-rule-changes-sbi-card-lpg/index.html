From 1 December: SBI credit cards charge 1% on big fuel and travel spends, while LPG and fuel prices face their monthly review

Fuel spends above ₹50,000 in a billing cycle will attract a 1% fee on the whole amount. Here is what changes, and what might, as December begins.

accha? daily · 25 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
1%SBI Card fee on fuel spends over ₹50,000 a cycle
  • 1% on single transport payments above ₹30,000
  • Home LPG unchanged since 7 June 2026
  • Aadhaar email update free via app till 31 Dec

SBI Card's new fees

From 1 December, SBI Card will charge 1% (plus taxes) on any single transport payment above ₹30,000 — so a ₹35,000 payment costs ₹350 extra. Payments of ₹30,000 or less are not affected.

For fuel, the rule works on the whole billing cycle. If your fuel spending in a cycle crosses ₹50,000, the 1% fee applies to the entire amount, not just the part above the limit. Spend ₹55,000 and you pay ₹550.

Prices to watch

Oil companies review LPG prices on the first of every month. The 14.2 kg home cylinder has not changed since 7 June, while commercial cylinders move more often. Petrol and diesel prices have also been held since June, even though crude oil has fallen in the past month.

CNG has been getting costlier: Mumbai's Mahanagar Gas raised its price by ₹1.50 to ₹91.50 a kg in November, its eighth hike in 11 months.

Other dates

The ₹75 fee for adding an email to Aadhaar is waived until 31 December, but only if you do it through the Aadhaar app. From 3 December, SEBI lets NRIs, OCI cardholders and some foreign nationals finish KYC remotely. The RBI's policy meeting in early December could also move loan EMIs and FD rates.

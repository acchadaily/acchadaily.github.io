Panchayat Season 5 trailer is out: Banrakas's wife is Phulera's new Pradhan, and the show returns on Prime Video on 3 December

Kranti Devi has beaten Manju Devi in the village election, and her husband Banrakas is ready to run things his way.

What the trailer shows

Prime Video released the trailer on Monday, 23 November. The big twist: Kranti Devi (Sunita Rajwar) has defeated Manju Devi (Neena Gupta) to become Pradhan. Her husband Bhushan, better known as Banrakas (Durgesh Kumar), now has power and his own 'development' plans, which look set to divide the village.

Fans also get hints that Abhishek (Jitendra Kumar) and Rinki (Sanvikaa) may take their relationship further, while he weighs his MBA plans. The mystery of the attack on Pradhan Ji carries on.

When to watch

The new season streams from 3 December on Prime Video in India and more than 240 countries and territories. Raghubir Yadav, Faisal Malik, Chandan Roy and Ashok Pathak return too.

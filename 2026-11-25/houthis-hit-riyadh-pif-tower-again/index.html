Houthis hit Riyadh's tallest tower, the PIF headquarters, for the second time in a week; 11 people injured

Saudi civil defence reported the injuries on 24 November. The Saudi-led coalition says a drone was used and vows a 'decisive' response.

  • First strike on the tower: 19 Nov, no injuries
  • Second strike: 24 Nov, 11 hurt
  • Most international flights to Riyadh cancelled

Second hit in days

The tower that houses Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund — the tallest building in Riyadh — was struck again on Tuesday, 24 November, injuring at least 11 people. Damage appeared about halfway up its face. The first strike, on 19 November, damaged the tower but hurt no one.

The Saudi-led coalition said the attack was carried out with a drone and promised to respond 'decisively' to what it called deliberate targeting of civilians.

A widening war

Since Yemen's civil war flared again in July, the Houthis have fired at Saudi airports, oil sites and office buildings. The attacks have led to most international flights to Riyadh being cancelled and big events, including the AFC Asian Cup, being put off. Hundreds of thousands of people have been forced from their homes in Yemen.

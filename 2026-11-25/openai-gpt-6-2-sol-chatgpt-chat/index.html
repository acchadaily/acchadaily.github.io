OpenAI brings GPT-6.2 Sol to ChatGPT's main chat for paying users; it passes hard safety tests 91–94% of the time, up from about 78%

The chat-tuned model is better at reading small details in photos and searching the web, says OpenAI. Free and Go users don't get it.

accha? daily · 25 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
91–94%safety-test pass rate, vs 77–80% for GPT-6
  • Rolling out from 23 November to Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise
  • Not available on Free or Go plans
  • Safety pass rate 91–94%, up from 77–80% for GPT-6

Who gets it

From Monday, 23 November, a version of GPT-6.2 Sol tuned for everyday conversation is rolling out in ChatGPT's Chat tab for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise subscribers. It comes about a week after GPT-6.2 launched in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API. Free and Go plans are left out for now.

What's better

OpenAI says the model handles long, multi-step requests and web searches better, and is better at spotting small details in photos, like text on a label or a symbol on a sign. It is also meant to know when a follow-up helps, such as offering to watch for a price drop on something you are shopping for.

On safety, OpenAI says it follows its rules in difficult conversations 91 to 94% of the time, compared with 77 to 80% for GPT-6, and does better on teen-safety checks. These are OpenAI's own tests.

Tata Motors will raise car and SUV prices by an average 0.5% from 1 December, including EVs

The carmaker says it can no longer absorb rising input costs. Hyundai and Audi have also announced price hikes.

accha? daily · 25 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
0.5%average price hike
  • Average hike of 0.5%, effective 1 December 2026
  • Applies to both petrol/diesel and EV models
  • Audi India plans up to 2% hike from 1 January

The hike

Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles will raise prices across its range of petrol, diesel and electric cars from 1 December 2026. The average increase is 0.5%, and the exact amount will depend on the model and variant.

If you were planning to buy a Tata car, booking before the end of November could save a little.

Why prices are going up

The company says it has absorbed most of the rise in raw material costs so far and now needs to pass some of it on. Costlier commodities and currency swings squeezed its margins in the September quarter, even though domestic revenue grew about 51%.

Others are doing it too

Hyundai India has also said it will raise prices from December, and Audi India will raise prices by up to 2% from 1 January.

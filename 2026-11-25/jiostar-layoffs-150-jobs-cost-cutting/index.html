JioStar, the company behind JioHotstar, plans to cut about 150 jobs to control costs, with six months' pay as severance

The Reliance–Disney media giant calls it 'portfolio rebalancing'. It is the second big round of job cuts since Viacom18 and Star India merged in 2024.

accha? daily · 25 November 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp
In short
~150jobs to be cut
  • About 150 roles cut across marketing, production and studios
  • Q2 revenue up 10% to ₹6,806 crore; EBITDA down 11%
  • Second major layoff round since the 2024 merger

What's happening

JioStar is planning to let go of around 150 employees across marketing, production, studios and some operations teams, according to a report first carried by exchange4media and a company statement. Industry sources say the final number could end up higher.

Those affected will get at least six months' salary plus a pro-rata bonus for this financial year.

What the company says

A JioStar spokesperson said the company is shifting its money towards the businesses where it sees the strongest long-term growth, and admitted the decision will affect some employees. It promised support through the transition.

Why now

Analysts link the cuts to a push to save money as competition for advertisers gets tougher and a pause in TV audience ratings hurts ad sales.

In the September quarter, JioStar's operating revenue grew 10% to ₹6,806 crore, but operating profit (EBITDA) fell 11% to ₹1,372 crore, and its margin shrank from 28.1% to 22.1%.

The bigger picture

JioStar was formed in November 2024 when Reliance's Viacom18 merged with Disney's Star India, in a deal that valued the combined company at $8.5 billion. More than 1,000 people left in a restructuring in March 2025. Meanwhile, JioHotstar has launched in Singapore, the UK and Canada, and digital watch time is up 15% from a year ago.

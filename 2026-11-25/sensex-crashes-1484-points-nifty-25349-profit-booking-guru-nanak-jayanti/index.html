Sensex crashes 1,484 points to 82,436 and Nifty falls to 25,350, its biggest one-day drop in about five months

Investors cashed in after a four-week rally, with bank and metal shares hit hardest. Markets were shut on Tuesday for Guru Nanak Jayanti and reopen today.

What happened

Indian shares had their worst day in months on Monday, 23 November. The Sensex lost 1,483.72 points, or 1.77%, to end at 82,435.99. The Nifty 50 dropped 450.95 points, or 1.75%, to 25,349.65.

Only last week the Nifty had touched a nine-month high, and the Sensex had gained more than 2,300 points over the week. Monday wiped out a big chunk of those gains.

Why markets fell

The main reason was profit-booking: after four straight weeks of gains, many investors chose to sell and lock in profits. A sell-off in US tech stocks, fears of an AI bubble and doubts over US interest rates added to the gloom.

The monthly derivatives expiry was also moved to Monday because of Tuesday's holiday, which made the swings sharper during the day.

Who got hit

Private and public sector banks, financial companies and metal stocks led the fall. Tata Steel, Axis Bank and HDFC Bank were among the biggest drags on the Nifty. IT shares held up better than the rest of the market.

Smaller companies fell less: the Nifty Midcap 100 slipped about 1% and the Smallcap 100 about 0.7%. The rupee also weakened by 30 paise to about 92.92 to the dollar, ending a five-day winning run.

What next

Exchanges were closed on Tuesday, 24 November, for Guru Nanak Jayanti. Wednesday's session will show whether Monday was a one-day shake-out or the start of a longer pause after the rally.

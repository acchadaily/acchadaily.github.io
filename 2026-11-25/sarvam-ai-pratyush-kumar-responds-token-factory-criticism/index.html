Sarvam AI co-founder Pratyush Kumar hits back at critics, saying its Token Factory 'runs on privately-procured GPUs'

Critics online accused the government-backed AI startup of using public money to host Chinese models like DeepSeek. Sarvam says that is wrong.

accha? daily · 25 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
4,096subsidised H100 GPUs Sarvam got earlier
  • Sarvam got 4,096 H100 GPUs worth about ₹247 crore under IndiaAI
  • Government-backed GPU access ended in March 2026
  • New Sarvam-105B-1711 model released

The criticism

Sarvam recently launched Token Factory, a service that runs AI models for businesses from India. Along with Sarvam's own models, it hosts Google's Gemma and China's DeepSeek and Kimi. That sparked online criticism that Sarvam was using taxpayer-funded computers to serve foreign models instead of building Indian ones.

The founder's reply

In a post on X, co-founder Pratyush Kumar said Token Factory uses GPUs Sarvam bought privately. He said the IndiaAI Mission subsidy went to cloud providers, not to Sarvam directly, and that its government-backed GPU access ended in March 2026.

He argued that running models locally helps Indian companies keep data in India and get faster responses, and that most of the few trillion AI tokens India uses daily are served from abroad.

Still building its own

Under the mission, Sarvam had received 4,096 Nvidia H100 GPUs for six months, worth about ₹247 crore including a ₹98.68 crore subsidy. It has now released an upgraded open model, Sarvam-105B-1711, and says new vision, speech and edge models will come in the next two weeks.

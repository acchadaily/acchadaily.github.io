Shadowfax buys WeFast's India business and Borzo's tech for about ₹23.3 crore to enter on-demand city deliveries

The listed logistics company gets WeFast's network of over 32,000 delivery partners as it goes after small shops and person-to-person deliveries.

accha? daily · 25 November 2026 · 1 min read

  • Expected to close by 31 March 2027

The deal

Shadowfax Technologies will pay $2.5 million (about ₹23.3 crore) in two parts: $2 million for Borzo's technology and software rights in India plus $50,000 for a licence to the Borzo name, and $450,000 for WeFast India's business assets such as contracts, equipment, data and some employees. The deal should close by 31 March 2027.

Who is WeFast?

Started in Mumbai in 2016, WeFast lets people and small businesses book a rider to move goods within a city. It works in more than five cities, has over 32,000 delivery partners, makes over 1.2 crore deliveries a year and earned about ₹200 crore in adjusted revenue in 2025.

Why Shadowfax wants it

Shadowfax, which has 3.8 lakh delivery partners, wants to serve shop owners, distributors and regular users, not just big e-commerce firms. CEO Abhishek Bansal said the deal widens its platform across parcels, hyperlocal delivery and on-demand transport. Its hyperlocal revenue grew 87.7% to ₹341 crore in the September quarter.

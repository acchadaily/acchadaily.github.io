What is a credit rating 'CreditWatch'?

A credit rating tells lenders how safe it is to lend money to a company. When an agency like S&P puts a rating on 'CreditWatch negative', it is warning that it may soon lower that grade, usually within about 90 days, while it studies a new event. It is not a downgrade yet. For a company, a lower rating can mean paying a little more interest when it borrows. For Nvidia, S&P's worry is how the board oversees its CEO, not its cash.

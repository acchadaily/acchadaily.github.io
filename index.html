What is a grey market premium (GMP)?

Before a company lists on the stock exchange, some people informally trade its IPO shares in an unofficial 'grey market'. The extra price they pay over the IPO price is called the grey market premium. Many investors watch it to guess how much a stock might gain on listing day. But these deals have no rules, no guarantee and no protection if something goes wrong, which is why SEBI now says GMP should not be treated as a guide to returns.

How does the World Chess Championship match work?

It is a one-on-one match between the reigning champion and a challenger who earns the spot by winning the Candidates Tournament. This year's match in Geneva has 14 classical games, with two hours each for the first 40 moves. A win is worth one point and a draw half a point, so the first player to reach 7.5 points takes the title. If it ends 7–7, four faster rapid games decide the champion.

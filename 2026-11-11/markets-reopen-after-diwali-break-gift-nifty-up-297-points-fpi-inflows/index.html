Markets reopen after the Diwali break with GIFT Nifty up 297 points; foreign investors have put in ₹53,739 crore this month

November's foreign buying is the biggest monthly total since September 2024. Lower oil prices are also helping.

accha? daily · 11 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹53,739crore of FPI buying in Nov
  • Sensex (9 Nov): 81,106.65
  • Nifty (9 Nov): 25,044.65
  • Nifty resistance: 25,150–25,200

Where things stand

NSE and BSE were shut on 10 November for Balipratipada. When they last traded, on 9 November, the Sensex closed at 81,106.65 and the Nifty at 25,044.65. That was their best close since late February. GIFT Nifty, which trades in Gujarat during the break, rose 297 points to 25,341 on Tuesday, pointing to a strong start on Wednesday.

Money flowing in

Foreign portfolio investors have bought ₹53,739 crore of Indian shares so far in November. That is the most in any month since September 2024. HSBC raised India to 'overweight' this week, and Brent crude fell below $90 for a while, both of which helped.

Levels to watch

Choice Broking's Sachin Gupta says the Nifty needs to hold above 25,150–25,200 to go higher. Analysts see support near 24,850–24,900.

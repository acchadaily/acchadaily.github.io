Google's Pixel Tag, its answer to the AirTag, goes on sale today; India price ₹3,799

The Bluetooth tracker costs $29 in the US, and a four-pack is ₹12,900 in India.

accha? daily · 11 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹3,799price of one Pixel Tag in India
  • US: $29 for one, $99 for four
  • India: ₹12,900 for a four-pack
  • Battery life: about a year

What it does

The Pixel Tag is Google's first Bluetooth item tracker. It uses Ultra-Wideband to point you to a lost item with an arrow and distance, and Google's Find Hub network of Android devices to locate it further away. It has a speaker, an IP67 rating against dust and water, alerts if you leave it behind, and can be shared with up to 10 people.

The extra trick

A button on the tag makes your phone ring, something Apple's AirTag cannot do for an iPhone. The coin-cell battery is user-replaceable and should last about a year. It works with phones on Android 9 or later. Google first showed the tag in August and set 11 November as the sale date.

Suriya's Scene crosses ₹100 crore India gross in five days, ₹174 crore worldwide

Jithu Madhavan's Diwali action comedy is Suriya's third hit of 2026, after Karuppu and Vishwanath & Sons.

accha? daily · 11 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹174Crworldwide gross in 5 days
  • Opening day: ₹19.15 crore net
  • India net after 5 days: ~₹97.4 crore
  • Overseas: ₹61.25 crore

The numbers

Scene opened with ₹19.15 crore net and held steady through the Diwali weekend. On Tuesday, its fifth day, it added about ₹12.85 crore net by 10 pm, according to Sacnilk figures cited by The Week, taking the India net total to about ₹97.4 crore and gross to ₹113 crore. Overseas markets have added ₹61.25 crore. Trackers give slightly different numbers, so final totals may change.

The film

Suriya plays a police officer, DSP Inbaraj, with Nazriya and Naslen in key roles. Director Jithu Madhavan is known for the Malayalam hits Aavesham and Romancham. More than 62 lakh tickets have been sold so far.

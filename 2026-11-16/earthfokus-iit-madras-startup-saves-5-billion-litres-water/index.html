An IIT Madras-incubated startup has helped 130+ buildings save 5 billion litres of water by finding hidden leaks

Chennai's EarthFokus cut one big office campus's daily water demand by nearly 80%.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
5 billionlitres of water saved
  • Buildings: 130+
  • Founded: 2019, Chennai
  • Low-flow tap: 0.5 L/min

How it works

EarthFokus, founded in 2019, uses sensors and software to track water use and spot leaks, recovers about 30% of water lost from cooling towers, and fits low-flow taps that run at as little as 0.5 litres a minute, against 10–12 litres for a normal tap.

Results

WeWork India's use fell from 45 to 29 litres per person a day, with a target of 20 by FY28, and Cognizant's Chennai campus cut daily demand by nearly 80%.

Trump, Xi and Putin's first three-way meeting in Shenzhen will focus on nuclear arms; the US says it expects no deal

The leaders meet on the sidelines of APEC this week; Ukraine is not on Trump's list.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
1stever three-way US–China–Russia leaders' meeting
  • Venue: Shenzhen, China
  • Date: 19 Nov
  • New START lapsed: Feb

What to expect

The White House has confirmed the meeting on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen. Nuclear weapons are the stated focus, after the New START arms treaty between the US and Russia lapsed in February.

A US official said: "We do not expect to get any kind of agreement." An administration official said Trump will not raise Ukraine, and no separate Trump–Putin meeting is planned. The Kremlin called the meeting "extremely significant".

Why it matters

It is the first time the leaders of the world's three biggest nuclear powers will sit down together, at a time of war in the Gulf and tension over trade and chips.

Markets this week: FPIs have bought ₹84,290 crore of Indian shares in November; WPI inflation, Nvidia results and Nifty expiry ahead

GIFT Nifty pointed to a slightly higher open on Monday after three straight weekly gains.

In short
₹84,290 crFPI buying in Indian equities, 2–13 Nov
  • Sept WPI: 11.39%
  • Oct CPI: 6.09%
  • Nifty Friday: 25,144

Money is coming back

Foreign portfolio investors bought a net ₹84,290 crore of Indian equities over the nine sessions from 2 to 13 November, PTI data show, after pulling out about ₹34,843 crore in October and ₹35,861 crore in September.

The Sensex ended Friday at 81,592 and the Nifty at 25,144, a third straight weekly gain. Early on Monday, GIFT Nifty indicated an opening about 58 points higher, while most Asian markets were slightly lower.

What to watch

October's wholesale (WPI) inflation is due at noon today; September's 11.39% was a series high and some economists expect October above 12%. Retail inflation for October was 6.09%, above the RBI's tolerance band.

Nifty's weekly expiry is on Tuesday, and Nvidia's results after the US close the same day could sway tech stocks worldwide.

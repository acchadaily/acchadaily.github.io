A critically endangered spoon-billed sandpiper tagged in Russia turns up on Bengal's coast, about 8,000 km away

Fewer than 500 of these tiny shorebirds are thought to be left in the wild.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
<500spoon-billed sandpipers left in the wild
  • Tagged: 6 July 2025
  • Birds released by programme: 236
  • Returned to breed: 19

The sighting

A spoon-billed sandpiper wearing the lime-green tag '2K', fitted in Chukotka, Russia, in July 2025, was photographed at Bakkhali in South 24 Parganas on 24 and 25 October. Only three of these birds have ever been photo-recorded in Bengal.

A rescue story

The species is critically endangered. A head-starting programme in Russia has released 236 birds, and 19 have come back to breed, so every sighting along the route helps scientists protect its wintering sites.

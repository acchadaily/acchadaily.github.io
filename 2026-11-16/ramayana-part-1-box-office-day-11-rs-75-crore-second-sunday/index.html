Ramayana: Part 1 earns ₹75 crore on its second Sunday, taking its India net total to ₹760.60 crore in 11 days

It will also release in China, Russia, Japan and South Korea in early 2027.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹75 crnet on Day 11 (second Sunday)
  • Day 10: ₹72.90 cr
  • Hindi share Day 11: ~₹55 cr
  • New markets: 4

The numbers

Ramayana: Part 1 collected about ₹75 crore net in India on Sunday, slightly up on Saturday's ₹72.90 crore, according to Sacnilk's early estimates. That takes its India net total to about ₹760.60 crore in 11 days.

Hindi shows brought in about ₹55 crore of Sunday's total, with the rest from the Kannada, Malayalam, Tamil and Telugu versions.

Going global

Bollywood Hungama reports the film will release in China, Russia, Japan and South Korea in the first quarter of 2027, and has already been sent for censor clearance in China.

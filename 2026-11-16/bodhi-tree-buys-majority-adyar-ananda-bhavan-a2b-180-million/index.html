James Murdoch and Uday Shankar's Bodhi Tree buys a majority of sweets chain Adyar Ananda Bhavan (A2B) for $180 million

The deal values the Chennai chain, which started as a sweet shop in 1979, at about $350 million.

The deal

Bodhi Tree Systems, backed by James Murdoch and Uday Shankar, is buying a majority stake in Adyar Ananda Bhavan for $180 million. Reuters puts the valuation at about $350 million, a little over twice A2B's FY25 sales. It is Bodhi Tree's first food investment, and Uday Shankar will chair the board.

A2B began as a Chennai sweet shop in 1979 and now has about 160 stores in India, 30+ abroad and over 15,000 staff. FY25 revenue rose 7% to ₹1,487.5 crore, while profit slipped 7% to ₹36.2 crore.

What's next

The plan is to expand into western India and Europe, and to make the company "fit for the public markets", as co-founder K.T. Srinivasa Raja put it, a hint at a future IPO.

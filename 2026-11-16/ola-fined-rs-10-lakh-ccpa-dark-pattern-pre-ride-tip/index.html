Ola fined ₹10 lakh by consumer watchdog CCPA for pushing riders to 'tip' before a driver accepts the ride

The regulator called Ola's pre-ride 'tip' and 'add-on' prompts a dark pattern and ordered the feature switched off immediately.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹10 lakhpenalty on Ola for pre-ride tipping prompts
  • Tips allowed: only after the ride
  • Law: Aggregator Guidelines 2025, clause 14.15
  • Ministry clarification: 11 Aug

What happened

The Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) has fined ANI Technologies, which runs Ola, ₹10 lakh and ordered it to stop asking riders for a 'tip' or 'add-on' before a booking is accepted.

Consumer Affairs Minister Pralhad Joshi called it "a firm step against dark patterns".

What Ola was doing

Before a driver took the trip, the app nudged riders to add extra money to get a cab faster. The CCPA found that rides without the extra payment had lower acceptance rates, and passengers weren't told that.

The regulator classed the prompts as 'confirm shaming', a design trick that makes users feel bad for saying no. Renaming 'tip' to 'add-on' didn't change its view.

The rule it broke

India's Motor Vehicles Aggregators Guidelines 2025 allow tips only after a ride is over. The CCPA says Ola kept the pre-ride prompt even after a transport ministry clarification on 11 August.

Why it matters

Pay-more-to-get-a-cab buttons have become common across ride-hailing apps. This order makes clear that regulators see them as pressure tactics, and other platforms with similar features are likely to review them.

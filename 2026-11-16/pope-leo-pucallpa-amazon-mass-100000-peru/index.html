Pope Leo draws about 100,000 to a Mass in Peru's Amazon city of Pucallpa, calling for protection of the forest and its people

He wraps up his South America tour in Lima and flies back to Rome on 17 November.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read

  • Chiclayo Mass: 1.5 million

What happened

Local authorities estimated about 100,000 people at the Pope's Mass in Pucallpa, in Peru's Ucayali region. Speaking about the Amazon, he said "very little has been done, and very slowly", and that violence against people is also violence against the land.

Earlier, the Vatican counted 1.5 million at his Mass in Chiclayo, where he once served as a bishop.

The tour

The trip, which began on 6 November, has taken him through Uruguay, Argentina and Peru, about a month after lung-cancer surgery in October. Reuters estimates about 10 million people attended events across the tour.

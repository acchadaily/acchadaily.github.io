A drone flies food and blankets to a 38-year-old chimp stranded by floods at a Florida sanctuary

Kaleb was filmed happily pulling a banana out of the paper bag.

The rescue

Floodwaters left Kaleb, a 38-year-old chimpanzee, stuck on a small island at Save the Chimps, a 150-acre sanctuary near Fort Pierce, Florida. Staff flew food and blankets to him by drone until the water went down.

All the animals stayed safe, and the water had receded a lot by Saturday. The chimps there were rescued from labs, entertainment and the pet trade.

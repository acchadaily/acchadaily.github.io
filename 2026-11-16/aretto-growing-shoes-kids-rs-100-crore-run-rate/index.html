Kids' brand Aretto's 'growing shoes' fit three sizes in one pair; it now targets a ₹100 crore run rate by mid-2027

The Pune brand has sold over 4 lakh pairs and gets about 40% of its discovery from Instagram.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
4 lakh+pairs of expandable kids' shoes sold
  • Stores: 80
  • Run rate now: ~₹60 cr
  • Target: ₹100 cr by mid-2027

The idea

Aretto makes children's shoes with an expandable sole, so one pair grows through three sizes. That lets it cut its range from about 16 sizes to 6, a big saving on stock for a kids' brand.

The Pune company has 80 stores, aims for 100 by March 2027, and says its annual run rate is about ₹60 crore today. It is growing nearly threefold a year, says CEO Satyajit Mittal.

The marketing

Instagram drives about 40% of discovery. Half its digital budget goes to Meta, 30% to Google and 20% to influencers. It is chasing a premium kids' footwear segment (₹2,500 and up) worth about ₹2,500 crore, within a ₹13,000 crore kids' footwear market.

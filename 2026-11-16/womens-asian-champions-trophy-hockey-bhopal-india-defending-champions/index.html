Women's Asian Champions Trophy hockey starts in Bhopal on 19 November; defending champions India open against Malaysia

Salima Tete leads a 20-member squad chasing a fourth title.

4thtitle India are chasing
  • Teams: 6
  • Dates: 19–28 Nov

The tournament

Six teams, India, Japan, China, Malaysia, Thailand and Korea, play from 19 to 28 November at the International Sports Complex in Nathu Barkheda, Bhopal.

India won the title in 2016, 2023 and 2024. Captain Salima Tete says she is confident the team can carry its Asian Games form into the event.

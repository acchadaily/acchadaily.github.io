Lakshya Sen comes from a game down to beat Koki Watanabe and become the first Indian to win the Kumamoto Masters

It is his second Super 500 title in a row and boosts his bid for the World Tour Finals.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
13-21, 21-18, 21-14Sen's win in the final
  • Match time: 1 hr 4 min
  • Ranking points: 9,200
  • Head-to-head vs Watanabe: 4-2

What happened

Lakshya Sen lost the first game to home favourite Koki Watanabe but fought back to win 13-21, 21-18, 21-14 in 1 hour 4 minutes on Sunday. He is the first Indian to win, and to even reach the final of, the Kumamoto Masters in Japan.

"It feels really good to have won a title here," Sen said, recalling that he made the semi-finals last year.

Why it matters

It follows his Hylo Open win, making it back-to-back Super 500 titles and his first season with two World Tour crowns. The title is worth 9,200 ranking points.

Sen needs a top-eight finish in the race to the World Tour Finals in Hangzhou (9–13 December). The Hong Kong Open, starting Tuesday, is the last qualifier.

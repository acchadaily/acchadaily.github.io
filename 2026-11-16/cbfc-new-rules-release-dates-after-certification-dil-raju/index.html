New CBFC rules mean films can't announce release dates before certification, says producer Dil Raju

Final cuts now have to reach the censor board about a month before release.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
1 monthbefore release a film's final cut is due
  • Guidelines: Sept
  • Advisory: Oct
  • Final cut due: ~1 month early

What changed

Under guidelines issued in September and a detailed advisory in October, producers must submit a film's final version, with titles, graphics and sound, about a month before release, and can announce a release date only after certification. The board also gets the final say on titles.

"Releasing a film became quite problematic," said Dil Raju, who chairs the Telangana Film Development Corporation. He says the Telugu industry is preparing a representation, and Kerala film bodies have called the title rule an unnecessary expansion of the board's powers.

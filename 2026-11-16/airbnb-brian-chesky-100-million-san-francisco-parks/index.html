Airbnb's Brian Chesky gives San Francisco $100 million for parks, the biggest single-donor gift in the city's history

A new 10-acre waterfront park with a beach is among the first projects.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
$100Mfor San Francisco's public parks
  • Immediate funding: $39M
  • New coastal park: 10 acres
  • Joined waterfront park: 5 acres

The gift

Airbnb co-founder and CEO Brian Chesky is giving $100 million to San Francisco's parks. $39 million goes straight to five projects, and the other $61 million will fund projects the city's parks department picks over the next year.

Chesky called it "giving back to the city that made Airbnb possible".

What gets built

India Basin Waterfront Park in Bayview-Hunters Point gets a 10-acre coastal park with a gravel beach and boathouse, closing a gap in the Bay Trail. A new half-acre park opens three years early, and two downtown waterfront spaces will be joined into one 5-acre park.

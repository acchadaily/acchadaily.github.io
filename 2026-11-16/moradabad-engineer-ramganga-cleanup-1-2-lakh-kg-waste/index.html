Sunday clean-ups led by a Moradabad engineer have pulled over 1.2 lakh kg of waste from the Ramganga river

What started on Earth Day 2018 has grown into 218+ drives with thousands of volunteers.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
1.2 lakh kgof waste removed from the river
  • Drives: 218+
  • Volunteers: 5,000+
  • Started: 22 April 2018

The campaign

Kapil Kumar, 35, a mechanical engineer, started cleaning the Ramganga through his NGO Parivartan on Earth Day 2018. Since then there have been 218+ drives with 5,000+ volunteers, and a typical Sunday clears 300–700 kg.

Recognition

The India Book of Records lists it as the longest riverbank cleanliness drive by an NGO, and the group has sent a 10-point plan to the national and state Clean Ganga missions.

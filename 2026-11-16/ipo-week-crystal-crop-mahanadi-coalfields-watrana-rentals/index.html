IPO week: Crystal Crop opens Wednesday at ₹259–272, and Coal India's Mahanadi Coalfields arm follows on Thursday

Three issues open this week, including Coal India's sale of about 10% of Mahanadi Coalfields.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹455 crCrystal Crop Protection's IPO size
  • Crystal Crop lot: 55 shares
  • MCL OFS: 66.18 crore shares
  • Watrana: ~₹120 cr

The line-up

Crystal Crop Protection (18–20 November): price band ₹259–272, lot of 55 shares (₹14,960 at the top end). The ₹455.49 crore issue mixes ₹354.77 crore of new shares with a ₹100.71 crore sale by the promoter; money will go towards repaying debt and acquisitions.

Mahanadi Coalfields (19–23 November): a pure offer for sale of up to 66.18 crore shares by Coal India, about 10% of the company. The price band had not been announced at the time of writing.

SME pick

Watrana Rentals (19–23 November), an SME issue of about ₹120 crore, all fresh shares, with a lot of 600 shares.

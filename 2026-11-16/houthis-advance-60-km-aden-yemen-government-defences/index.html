Yemen's Houthis advance to within 60 km of Aden as the government fortifies the city; 600 detainees freed

Military sources say the rebels have taken two districts in Lahj province.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
60 kmHouthi front line's distance from Aden
  • Detainees freed: 600
  • Districts taken: 2
  • Fighting resumed: July

What happened

Houthi forces have captured the Al-Maqatirah and Al-Madaribah districts in Lahj province and hold the Al-Kharaz camp, about 60 km from Aden, military and government sources told AFP. Government troops have pulled back from areas near the Bab el-Mandeb strait and are building sandbag defences and tunnels around Aden.

The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted missiles and drones aimed at Riyadh.

A small humanitarian step

The ICRC said the Houthis released 600 detained government personnel held since fighting resumed in July. The Houthis tried and failed to take Aden early in the war in 2015.

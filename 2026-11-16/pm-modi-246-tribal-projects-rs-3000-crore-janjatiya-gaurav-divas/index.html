PM Modi launches 246 tribal development projects worth ₹3,000 crore on Janjatiya Gaurav Divas

The projects, marking Birsa Munda's birth anniversary, include 38 new Eklavya model residential schools.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
₹3,000 crworth of tribal projects
  • Projects: 246
  • New EMRS schools opened: 38
  • Venue: Gadchiroli (virtual)

What was announced

Joining a programme in Gadchiroli, Maharashtra, by video link on 15 November, PM Narendra Modi inaugurated 173 projects and laid foundation stones for 73 more, together worth about ₹3,000 crore. The day marks the birth anniversary of tribal icon Birsa Munda.

Inaugurations included 38 Eklavya Model Residential Schools and 100 multi-purpose centres under the PM-JANMAN scheme. Foundation stones were laid for 19 more EMRS schools and 12 tribal marketing centres.

Also mentioned

Modi spoke about tribal communities' rights over bamboo and listed new institutions, including a museum in Mizoram and a Tribal Research Institute building in Arunachal Pradesh. The day closed a fortnight of events held from 1 to 15 November.

India defend just 209 to beat New Zealand by 22 runs and win the ODI series 3-1; Arshdeep takes 3 for 30

It is New Zealand's first home ODI series defeat in seven years.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
3-1India's ODI series win in New Zealand
  • Margin: 22 runs
  • India: 209 all out
  • NZ: 187 all out

The match

At Bay Oval in Mount Maunganui, India were bowled out for 209 in 42.1 overs. Axar Patel and Ishan Kishan made 46 each, while Jacob Duffy and Mitchell Santner took three wickets apiece for New Zealand.

The chase looked comfortable at 64 for 1, but New Zealand slid to 119 for 6 and were all out for 187 in 48.1 overs. Dean Foxcroft top-scored with 31.

The bowlers did it

Arshdeep Singh was Player of the Match with figures of 10-2-30-3. Mohammed Siraj and Axar picked up two wickets each, and Yash Thakur took his first ODI wicket. Gurnoor Brar was named Player of the Series.

Stand-in captain Shreyas Iyer admitted the batting wasn't great but said the bowlers all "bowled with the same direction".

Why it matters

The win bounces India back from the heavy defeat in the fourth ODI and hands New Zealand their first home ODI series loss in seven years. ESPNcricinfo notes it is also India's first away ODI series win under coach Gautam Gambhir.

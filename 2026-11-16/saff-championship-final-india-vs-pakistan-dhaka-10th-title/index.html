India face Pakistan in the SAFF Championship final on Tuesday, chasing a record 10th title

It is Pakistan's first-ever SAFF final; India beat them 3-2 in the group stage.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
10thSAFF title India are chasing
  • Semi: India 2-1 Bhutan
  • Group game: India 3-2 Pakistan
  • Kick-off: 7:30 pm IST, Tue

Road to the final

India beat Bhutan 2-1 in the semi-final, with goals from Vishnu PV and Chinglensana Singh. Pakistan stunned hosts Bangladesh 1-0 with a late Abdul Samad goal to reach their first SAFF final.

The teams met on 5 November, when India won 3-2 with two goals from Suhail Ahmad Bhat.

Watch it

Defending champions India are in their 10th straight final and have won nine titles. Kick-off is 7:30 pm IST on Tuesday at the National Stadium in Dhaka, streaming free on VETO.

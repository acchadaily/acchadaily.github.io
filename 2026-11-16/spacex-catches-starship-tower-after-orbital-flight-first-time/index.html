SpaceX catches a Starship with its launch tower after an orbital flight for the first time; the ship deployed 26 Starlink satellites

The 'chopsticks' caught the upper stage, but the Super Heavy booster was lost in the Gulf.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
21 hrsthe ship spent in orbit before the catch
  • Starlink satellites: 26
  • Booster catches so far: 3
  • Starship orbital flights: 2

What happened

SpaceX's Starship Flight 15 launched from Starbase, Texas, on Friday afternoon local time. After more than 21 hours in orbit, the ship flew back and was caught by the arms of the launch tower on Saturday, the first time a Starship upper stage has been caught after an orbital flight.

"Mechzilla has caught the Starship," SpaceX host Dan Huot announced.

The mission

The ship deployed 26 next-generation Starlink V3 satellites. This was Starship's second trip to orbit, after a milestone flight in September.

Not a perfect day

The Super Heavy booster's boostback burn ended early and it exploded on hitting the Gulf of Mexico, so there was no double catch. Boosters have been caught three times before.

Why it matters

Catching the ship, not just the booster, is the key to making the whole rocket reusable, which is how SpaceX plans to cut launch costs sharply and fly Starship far more often.

Parliament's Winter Session likely in the first week of December; the women's quota–delimitation bill would need about 361 votes

The government is weighing a fresh session or reconvening the Monsoon Session, which was never formally closed.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
361votes needed for the constitutional amendment
  • NDA strength: ~320 MPs
  • Proposed seats: 543 → 816
  • April vote: 298–230

What's expected

Government sources say the Winter Session is likely to start in the first week of December, IANS reports. One option is to recall the Monsoon Session, which was adjourned on 13 August but not prorogued. That needs just two days' notice, compared with 14 days for a fresh session.

The big bill

The government plans a constitutional amendment linking 33% reservation for women to delimitation by expanding the Lok Sabha. It needs a two-thirds majority, roughly 361 of 540 sitting MPs, while the NDA has about 320.

Under the model outlined by Home Minister Amit Shah, Lok Sabha seats would rise from 543 to 816 and southern states' seats from 129 to 195, keeping their share near 24%. An earlier version was defeated 298–230 in April. Opposition parties have raised their own demands, including over the electoral roll revision.

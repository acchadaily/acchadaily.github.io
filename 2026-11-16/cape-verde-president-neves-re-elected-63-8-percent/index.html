Cape Verde re-elects President José Maria Neves with 63.8% of the vote, provisional results show

His main challenger, Joana Rosa Amado, was the country's first woman presidential candidate.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
63.8%Neves' provisional vote share
  • Runner-up: 31.6%
  • Eligible voters: ~420,000
  • Observers: ~150

The result

Provisional results give Neves 63.8% against 31.6% for former justice minister Joana Rosa Amado, enough to avoid a 29 November run-off. With about 96% of polling tables counted, he had 79,945 votes to her 39,544. Four other candidates each got under 2%.

Turnout appeared low, at around a third of the nearly 420,000 eligible voters, though that figure is provisional.

Context

Neves, prime minister from 2001 to 2016, was first elected president in 2021. The island nation ranks third out of 54 on the Mo Ibrahim governance index and was moved up to upper-middle-income status by the World Bank in July.

EaseMyTrip's Q2 loss shrinks 98% to ₹57 lakh as hotel revenue nearly triples; its CMO resigns a year after joining

Hotels and holidays are carrying the travel platform as air-ticket revenue falls.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp
In short
+189%jump in hotel and package revenue
  • Revenue: ₹157.9 cr (+33.5%)
  • Bookings: ₹2,458.4 cr
  • Air revenue: −21.9%

The results

EaseMyTrip's net loss for July–September narrowed to about ₹57 lakh from roughly ₹36 crore a year earlier. Operating revenue rose 33.5% to ₹157.9 crore and gross bookings grew 25.5% to ₹2,458.4 crore.

Hotel and package revenue jumped 189% to ₹92 crore, while air-ticket revenue fell 21.9% to ₹56.3 crore. Ad spend dropped 12% to ₹19.5 crore.

Leadership change

Chief marketing officer Manmeet Ahluwalia has resigned for personal reasons, effective 30 November. He joined in November 2025 after about 11 years at Expedia.

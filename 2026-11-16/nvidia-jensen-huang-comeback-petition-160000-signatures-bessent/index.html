'Stand with Jensen': 160,000+ sign a petition to bring Jensen Huang back to Nvidia, as Musk backs it and Bessent says hire an outsider

Four days after he quit, Silicon Valley is split over whether Nvidia's founder should return, a day before the chipmaker's results.

accha? daily · 16 November 2026 · 2 min read

In short
160K+signatures on the 'Stand with Jensen' petition
  • Huang resigned: 12 Nov
  • Results: Tue 17 Nov
  • Expected revenue: ~$109B

What happened

An online petition asking Nvidia's board to reinstate Jensen Huang has passed 160,000 signatures, CNBC reports. Elon Musk posted that he is "signing it too", and Epic Games' Tim Sweeney and Y Combinator's Garry Tan have also backed it.

More than 7,500 signers say they work at Nvidia, though names and employers on the petition are self-reported and unverified. The petition site says neither Nvidia nor Huang endorses it.

A comeback push

The Financial Times reports Huang has talked to confidants about returning, and allies are said to have set up a 'war room' to lobby the board. Investors Brad Gerstner and Gavin Baker signed an open letter asking the board to keep every option open, including his return.

Not everyone agrees. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Sunday that Nvidia should look outside for its next chief, and analyst Dan Ives said he sees "no chance" Huang comes back as CEO.

Where Nvidia stands

Huang resigned on 12 November. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, and the board says it will consider internal and external candidates while an independent investigation continues. Business Today reports the stock has fallen more than 23% since the allegations surfaced.

What's next

Nvidia reports quarterly results after the US market closes on Tuesday, its first under an interim boss. Analysts expect revenue of roughly $109 billion, and options markets are pricing a move of about 9% either way.
